Natalie wondered whether Diana Eland was truly able to come and go from the hotel with Austin.

Soon, Natalie arrived at Austin's office. She hadn't even stepped inside yet when she saw a young woman holding a cup of coffee enter ahead of her.

In a sweet, almost coquettish voice, the woman said, "Mr. Powell, here's your coffee, just as you requested-no sugar."

The sound of her voice was one most men could hardly resist. No matter how guarded their hearts were, they would soften a little.

Natalie raised an eyebrow, watching closely for Austin's reaction. After all, Austin was a man.

Their relationship had been stable for years, and with three children now, they knew each other even better. As time went on, the passion naturally faded. Some people couldn't help but get distracted, glancing at something or someone else in those moments.

Even if that person was far from perfect, curiosity would always draw them in as long as they were given a novel experience.

Austin didn't even look up. He simply hummed in acknowledgment.

Diana bit her lip and lowered her voice. "My parents are arranging a blind date for me, but I don't want to go. You know, I've liked you since middle school."

Natalie froze. "Damn!"

Natalie was instantly alert.

She couldn't believe her luck. While others had spent ages trying to catch someone in the act without success, here she was, barely arriving, and already witnessing the whole situation unfold before her eyes.

"I'm married. You can leave now," Austin said, his voice firm and cold.

Natalie had been married to Austin for years, so she knew his temper. He might not show it now, but she could tell he was running out of patience. She couldn't help but wonder who this Diana was.
