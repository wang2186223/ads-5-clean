Joe glanced over immediately. He saw Skylar helping Caleb to his feet.

“Ms. Sullivan seems to know the guy who fell off the motorcycle,” Paul said.

Joe frowned. His eyes were focused on Caleb, who was all eyes on Skylar. “Caleb?”

“Yeah. That really is Mr. Alexander Senior’s grandson, Caleb.” Paul was surprised.

Skylar knew who Caleb was. What were the odds?

Joe remained focused on Skylar. Her face was pale, probably from the shock of witnessing the accident.

She usually appeared to be brave, but an accident got her looking pretty shaken up. She was still a woman with a delicate heart, after all.

“Should I go help Ms. Sullivan and Caleb?” Paul was ready to get out of the car.

Joe saw that there were already plenty of people assisting and felt that he wouldn’t be much of a help even if he went.

He withdrew his focus from Skylar when Paul looked at Joe. “No need. Let’s head to the hospital.”

Paul was then reminded that they were heading to the hospital today.

Gloria insisted that Joe have his regular health checks every two months because of his insomnia. So, he

would head to the hospital to keep his health in check.

Since his schedule was empty that day, he could take the time to visit the hospital.

At that time, Caleb and Skylar were already in the ambulance with the help of passersby. The traffic police

also towed Caleb’s bike.

Half an hour later, Caleb and Skylar arrived at the hospital.

After a thorough check–up, the doctor confirmed that Caleb had fractured his left leg and would require

surgery.

What made it worse was that he had an old injury on that leg, and this accident just aggravated it. There

was no avoiding a hospital stay now,

Caleb wasn’t thrilled about being hospitalized. “Is it really that bad? I don’t want to stay here. Can’t I just

get a cast and go home?”

‘Listen to the doctor. Unless you want to give up on riding bikes?” Skylar advised after seeing the doctor’s annoyed expression.

“Are you the patient’s family? Please pay at the counter first.” The doctor looked at Skylar.

Skylar nodded. “Alright, I’ll take care of it now.”

Caleb blushed when Skylar didn’t deny being his family. Did she just admit being related to him?

A few minutes later, Skylar returned to the hospital room.

Caleb was still blushing. “How much was it? I’ll transfer the money to you,”

“Pay me back once you get your salary. You should let your parents know about your surgery so they can come and take good care of you. It also requires a relative’s signature,” Skylar said, looking at the receipt.

“My parents can’t make it and my grandpa won’t be able to come either. I could ask Uncle John but he’s busy.” Caleb’s eyes darted as he concocted a plan.

He still hadn’t figured out how to tell Skylar about him being Alexander’s grandson. So, he couldn’t let

Alexander come over.

So, John was his only option. Besides, he could use this situation to his advantage and get some sympathy from Skylar since there were no family members there to take care of him.

Skylar sensed that Caleb was hiding something. She remembered that he had dropped out of high school, which likely meant there were reasons he couldn’t continue his studies.

“Then have your Uncle John sign for the surgery. I’ll bring you food whenever I’m available. If you need anything, ask the nurse to contact me,” Skylar said.

Caleb’s eyes lit up at the mention of food. Did he finally have the chance to taste Skylar’s home– cooked

meals?

Skylar checked the time. “You still have a test result that hasn’t come back. It should be out now. I’ll go

pick it up.”

*Skylar, you really are amazing.” Caleb was extremely grateful to her.
