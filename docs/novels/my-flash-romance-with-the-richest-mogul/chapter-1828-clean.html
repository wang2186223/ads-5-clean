Zea asked, "Then what about Cyril?"

Randall had finally regained his composure and began to think more rationally about the situation. "It probably wasn't the work of some female ghosts or Manfred. He's locked up in the storage shed, hasn't eaten a single piece of bread, and is so weak from hunger that he couldn't have harmed Cyril. This was likely the work of someone. Someone must have hired them to break in."

Zea nodded repeatedly, finding it reasonable.

Randall continued, "We cannot let this matter become public."

"But if we don't investigate further, won't that be unfair to Cyril? He's our only son,"

Zea said, her voice trembling as she thought of her son now rendered useless.

Her anger flared. Her son had been reduced to a helpless state while that wretched woman's son still thrived.

Randall sighed deeply. This was a heavy blow for him as well. "From now on, we'll tell everyone that Cyril is ill and needs to rest and recuperate."

"Does that mean you won't let Cyril go out and meet people anymore?" Zea gasped, realizing that Cyril, the supposed heir to the Atkinson family, could no longer leave the house.

Randall was helpless. "Even if he did go out, people would just mock him. We can't control what others say."

Zea cried out, "Then Cyril will just suffer without any justice? I feel like this has something to do with Manfred. Do you think he found out that you wanted Cyril to be the heir? He might have been upset and hired someone to harm Cyril."

Though she voiced her suspicion, a part of her couldn't shake the feeling that Manfred, confined to the storage shed, was under constant surveillance. He had been trapped there for days, only getting a sip of water now and then. He couldn't possibly have hurt anyone.

"I've told you, it's impossible for Manfred to be involved. He's been confined for years and doesn't have the means to do anything. Even if he resented Cyril, all he could do was harbor ill feelings.

"And even if he was somehow connected to those female ghosts, what could they do? All they can do is scare people," Randall said. He was clear about the situation.

Zea stared intently at him. "Randall, if Cyril can no longer have children, will you make Manfred the heir and let him show himself again?"

"Don't be ridiculous. How could Manfred compare to our son? Don't think too much about it. Go and check Cyril and encourage him to regain his strength," Randall said, avoiding her gaze. He stood up and walked toward the door.

Zea bit her lip.

Randall reached the door, then turned back to glance at her, letting out a long sigh.

Zea stood staring at the closed door, her expression darkening with each passing moment. She had served Randall with all her heart for many years, and she wouldn't let Manfred take advantage of the situation.

Even if Cyril could never have children, she still had other ways to secure her position.

Everything in the Atkinson family was hers and Cyril's. Manfred could dream all he wanted-he would never take it from them.

...

Vivian realized something when she got home.

School was starting tomorrow, and she had to return to campus for classes.

She still had an experiment left unfinished. A few classmates and her mentor were waiting for her.

She wouldn't have much time to visit Manfred in the next few days. She thought about it and figured the supplies she had prepared for him would be enough for a while.

She figured he wouldn't run into trouble with everything going on at the Atkinson residence. If something did happen, the bracelet would let her know.

So, with peace of mind, she went back to school. The beginning of the semester was always busy. She was running around from morning till night, and by the time she got back to her dorm, it was just for a quick wash-up before sleep.

A week passed in a blur. Before she knew it, the weekend was here. She had originally planned to head back to Galaxy Villa, but just as she was leaving the campus and heading to the subway, she spotted Hadwin.

"What are you doing here at my school? Don't tell me you've got a new girlfriend

here?" she asked, looking around.
