Skylar was on the antique street in Jipsburg. The street was lined with everything imaginable, and setting foot there felt like traveling back to ancient times.

She had a huge bag with her today. The ancient painting she had retrieved from Williams Residence was stowed away in her bag.

The old man had said that his grandfather had left the painting behind and claimed that it was from 1120 BC. Yet, it was impossible to determine the artist due to its poor state. The old man had been around this street for ages, and the highest offer anyone had made for the painting was merely a thousand dollars.

But Skylar had recognized the painting as the work of a painter from 1120 BC who was known for depicting picturesque scenery.
