When Joe met Skylar for the first time, he knew she was beautiful and stood out among the crowd. She looked gentle as she busied herself in the kitchen. Her soft look would make one forget about how tired. they felt.

Skylar smiled when she saw Joe standing at the kitchen door. “I’ve prepared two dishes. Would you join me for a meal?”

“Okay,” Joe replied.

Perhaps because he was home, his head hurt less. It was probably because he felt more relieved.

He had drunk a few glasses of alcohol during the gathering that night. He had barely eaten anything, but he wasn’t hungry. However, he suddenly felt slightly hungry when he saw Skylar’s smiling face.

After getting changed upstairs, he went downstairs. On the dining table were two dishes and two plates of pasta.

She had prepared salad and steak.

Although these were common dishes, she made them look exceptionally appetizing

“Mr. Martin, thank you for today,” Skylar thanked him as soon as she took a seat.

“No trouble at all. You don’t have to thank me.” Joe was slightly displeased at the way Skylar addressed him. She sounded very distant when she thanked him. However, he wasn’t too bothered.

He added, “I should thank you instead, as I’ve been eating the food you prepared.”

Skylar smiled. “Let’s dig in. If we keep thanking each other, the food will get cold.”

“Okay.”

Joe rarely ate salad, but he had half a plate that day. Although the salad was just a mixture of vegetables, Skylar made them taste great.

“I’ll send you to work tomorrow morning. The chauffeur will pick Grandma and then you when you get off

work,” Joe said after he was done with the meal.

“Thank you.” Skylar nodded.
