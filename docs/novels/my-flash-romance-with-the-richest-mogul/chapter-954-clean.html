Vera was attending a friend’s birthday party. After sipping a glass of red wine, she didn’t immediately check her phone when she received a

message.

It wasn’t until she went to the restroom that she looked at her phone. When she saw a photo sent by a friend, her expression changed.

Her friend had messaged. “They’re on the same flight as me to Fachington. They seem to be getting along well, and Joe appears to treat her quite nicely. Are you sure you don’t mind?”

Vera stared at the photo with frustration. It was a casual, candid shot, not very clear. But it was obvious that the two people were sitting closely together, with Joe leaning toward Skylar.

Body language wouldn’t lie. Skylar clearly had a significant place in Joe’s heart. They were even married.

This marriage…

Initially, Vera thought Joe wouldn’t care much about Skylar. After all, it was a flash marriage arranged by Gloria with no emotional

connection. She had hoped that with her return and presence, Joe would lose interest in Skylar.

But now, they were going to Fachington together.

For the past few days, due to the incident with Christopher, she had been holding back and waiting for Joe to make a move and come closer to her.

But now…

She felt Molly was right. Skylar must have used some underhanded tactics. In the face of such tactics, she would clearly be at a

disadvantage.

Thinking about it, she couldn’t stay seated. Vera picked up her phone and dialed a number. “Book me a flight to Fachington for tomorrow.”

Then she texted Molly, “Please have the items you mentioned delivered to my house tonight.”

to talk too much, fearing that he might find out about Minerva’s

pregnancy.
