“Enjoy,” Skylar smirked and pressed play.

The video began playing in front of them.

The extreme dirty talk they heard made Samuel and Kenny reconsider how much they

knew their dearest sister.

How could that obedient sister of theirs behave in such a manner?

Upon seeing it, Maisy began trembling. She shrieked, “Skylar! It’s fake. Samuel, Kenny,

listen to me. All of it is fake. Skylar got someone to edit it. All of it is fake! Skylar, you’re cruel! How could you make such a video?”

“Are you telling me this is fake? It’s real. I would’ve been able to tell if it was fake within seconds. Why’s Christopher here as well? He even recorded videos of how you had sex with these men. You… Maisy, you’ve lied to us!”

Samuel stared intently at Skylar’s phone and analyzed every frame. It left him

dumbfounded.

A million thoughts zipped through his brain.

He suddenly turned to Kenny, who was frozen with shock. Samuel asked, “Kenny, have you known about this for a long time? How could you accept this?”

Once the video finished, Skylar opened another. It was a recording of Maisy doing drugs in

a crowd.

A bunch of men surrounded Maisy as they ingested the drug. It would’ve made them so

high they forgot where they even were.

Looking at Maisy’s seductive moves on the dance floor, Kenny paled even further. Samuel’s eyeballs nearly fell out from the surprise.

Skylar rolled the window down entirely and glared coldly at the three of them. She warned, * If the police get a hold of this video, you’ll be accompanying your father in prison, Maisy. Also, the two of you better stop disturbing me. Even looking at you makes me nauseous.”

She closed the windows and sped away. Skylar could imagine the chaos that was bound to erupt between them.

Kenny shook from head to toe. When he recovered from his shock, he slapped Maisy.

“Disgusting! Maisy, you disgust me!”

How could she do who knows what with those men and then put on an innocent act in

front of them?

Maisy was disoriented from being hit. She refused to give up and continued attempting to explain herself, “No, all of it is fake. It really is. Believe me, alright? Skylar is trying to drive a wedge between us with this artificially manufactured video!”

“Alright. Since you’re insisting that the video’s fake, come with me to the police station to make a report. Have the police question Skylar. If she’s framing you, she’ll be punished by the law,” Kenny said furiously. He grabbed Maisy’s arm as though he was going to drag her

to the station.

How would Maisy have the guts to go? She frantically shook her head and cried, “No, I can’t go. Kenny, I can’t go. If I do, I’ll be screwed.”

How could Kenny not know what was going on?

Maisy’s terror was a tell–tale sign that it was real! Everything had been fucking genuine!

Samuel’s head was spinning. Every frame of the video was playing in his mind.

She was their sister whom they’d grown up with. They’d watched over her meticulously

since she was just a child. No matter what she wanted to do, they’d protected her. Even

when their biological sister, Skylar, returned home, they still believed they shouldn’t treat

Skylar well. They had to treat Maisy better.

Yet, this was how she was repaying them!

She was looser than the loosest celebrity that he could think of. It made his scalp prickle

with disgust.

Kenny roared, “Fuck off, Maisy. The further you go, the better. I never want to see you again. You no longer have anything to do with the Williams family. Just looking at you makes my stomach churn!”

Maisy trembled. Her face was scarily pale and covered in tears. She said, “No, that’s not it. This is fake. Skylar is framing me. Haven’t you guys always believed me? Why don’t you believe me anymore?”
