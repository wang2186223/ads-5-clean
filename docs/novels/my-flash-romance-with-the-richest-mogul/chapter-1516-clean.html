Suddenly, Janine realized that her past thoughts seemed a bit laughable. The person she had wronged the most was herself. Finn was not worth it. Finn was left speechless.

Finn had initially thought that coming to Janine would make things easier, but he hadn't expected her to have changed so much. She was no longer as easy to deceive.

But now, he couldn't back down.

In his panic, he didn't know what to do and suddenly grabbed Janine's hand. "I know I was wrong, Janine."

Janine immediately pulled her hand away. She couldn't accept any man touching her except for Ted now. "Whatever right or wrong doesn't matter anymore. Don't bother us anymore. Let's go our separate ways."

When Janine said "let's go our separate ways", Finn suddenly felt that Janine was still kind at heart.

He thought there might still be a place for him in her heart, and that she was just angry at him for abandoning her for 20 years.

"Before I came to see you, I already decided to compensate you. I have a villa and a bank card with one million dollars. These are my way of making up for everything," Finn said as he took a property deed and a bank card from his bag.

"The pin code for the bank card is your birthday. The villa is in Jipsburg, not far from here. It's about a 40-minute drive."

Janine glanced at the kindness in Finn's gestures and subtly raised an eyebrow. An old saying came to mind—a fox in the henhouse.

She knew there was some ulterior motive behind his sudden generosity. "So, what is it you really want from us?"

Finn took a deep breath. From his expression, it was clear that he thought these two offerings might sway Janine. He spoke earnestly, "My request is simple. I want Steven, Charles, and Harvey to go to the hospital and get a blood test."

"A blood test?" Janine raised an eyebrow, her eyes scanning Finn. "Are you terminally ill?"

The question was similar to what Harvey had asked earlier.

Finn shifted awkwardly. "I'm not terminally ill, and there's nothing serious going on. I just need them to go for a compatibility test."

"Compatibility test?" Janine's voice was sharp.

She immediately understood. That was why he had suddenly shown up.

"If you're not terminally ill, then someone close to you must be. One of the kids in your newly formed family?" Janine asked coldly.

She thought back to how Finn had ignored the children when they needed him most. Now that he had a use for them, he was back. He was still as selfish as ever.

Finn couldn't bring himself to meet her gaze. With things said this plainly, there was no point in avoiding it anymore.
