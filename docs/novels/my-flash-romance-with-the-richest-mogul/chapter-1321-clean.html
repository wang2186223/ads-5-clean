"At Mrs. Neal Senior's birthday party that day, I promised Mr. Knight I would go to his house to look at his paintings. He said they were seriously damaged, and I need to see if they can be restored."

Skylar thought of another matter. "Grandma, things with Joe are progressing well. Once he trusts me more, I'll try to bring him over to meet you. You need to take care of your health in the meantime."

Hearing Joe's name, Gloria's eyes lit up. She beamed and nodded eagerly. "Yes, yes! I'm filled with anticipation. I will definitely take care of my health."

She couldn't help but think about how much she'd missed Joe.

So, while Skylar was busy tomorrow, she planned to sneak a glance at him. She wouldn't disturb Joe; she just wanted to see him from a distance. As long as he was alive, that was enough for her. She understood that everything else would take time.

But lately, Gloria had a nagging feeling that her time was running short. The only thing that comforted her was knowing Joe was alive and that Skylar would be there for him.

They had both endured so much together, and she believed their bond would only grow stronger as a couple.

She felt relieved.

Skylar noticed that Gloria's complexion had improved and smiled. "I feel the same way. Let's both stay healthy. By the way, Mr. Knight lives near several Floan restaurants. There's a place known for its roasted chicken. After my visit, I'll bring some back for us to enjoy together tomorrow."

Gloria's eyes lit up at the mention of delicious food. However, Veronica felt a pang of sadness.

Gloria didn't want Veronica to say anything to Skylar. In truth, it was only when she ate with Skylar that Gloria would manage to take a few more bites, appearing to have an appetite. But when Skylar wasn't around, Gloria couldn't help but feel nauseous, and she hardly ate anything at all.

She was truly holding on by a thread. Otherwise, she wouldn't have promised to take Gloria to see Joe.

She hoped Joe would recover his memory soon and return quickly. Gloria was truly hanging on at this point.
