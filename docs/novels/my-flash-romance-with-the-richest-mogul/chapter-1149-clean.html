She suddenly remembered that she had sent Joe a message a little over an hour ago while waiting in line at the deli. However, after running into Claudio, she had completely forgotten/ about it and hadn’t checked if Joe had replied.

“Sure, let’s eat together when I get back.”

“Good, take care.”

Skylar read his message over again, focusing on the words “far” and “safe“. Her anxiety gradually faded.

He would be back tomorrow. There was nothing to worry about. She just needed to wait for him to return safely.
