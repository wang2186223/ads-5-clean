Vivian looked at Noah and said, "Since Manfred entrusted you with handling this matter, you should be the one to decide. There's no need to ask me."

With that, she turned and walked away.

She had originally planned to take a casual stroll around the residence, but this unexpected disturbance had completely ruined her mood.

As she left, Noah frowned in thought.

Seeing a glimmer of hope, Candice quickly pleaded her case, "Mr. Atkinson surely won't be willing to let me go. Mr. Noah, just let me see him. I promise he'll allow me to stay. If I can stay, I'll owe you a great favor. Whatever you ask of me in the future, I'll do it."

Noah considered her words carefully. "Fine, I'll give you a chance."

However, an hour later, when Noah brought Candice to meet Manfred, who had just returned to the residence, they had barely spoken two sentences before Manfred coldly ordered them to get out.

The two of them stood there in stunned silence, completely terrified by Manfred's expressionless face.

Before they could plead their case any further, they were thrown out.

Naturally, this matter never reached Vivian's ears.

Manfred was a master at earning people's favors. In just a short time, all those in the Atkinson household who had once questioned him had completely fallen silent.

Time passed quickly.

Viola appeared again, asking the same question as before-whether she wanted to stay in this world and live alongside Manfred.

Though she hesitated, she looked at Viola's kind face and gave the same answer.

"I don't want to stay. I want to return to the modern world with Manfred, to a time when we can live normal lives. Granny, this world isn't real, is it? It's like a game or a story from a novel, right?"

Viola looked at her with surprise. Then, she slowly smiled.

"Vivian, you're very smart and rational. Even though you love Manfred, you haven't let yourself be completely consumed by emotion." There was admiration in her eyes, along with a hint of relief.

Vivian gazed at Viola and continued, "Granny, you've been by my side since I was little. You taught me so many things, which is why I trust you completely.

"You were the one who helped me find my parents. So, you also want me to go back and reunite with them, don't you? And you want Manfred to have a family and loved ones as well, right?"

Viola's gaze grew even more affectionate. "You're absolutely right, my clever child. Would you like to hear a story? A story about why I brought you to meet Manfred?"

"I'd love to."

And so, Viola told her a story-one about Manfred and her in their past lives.

When Vivian finished listening, she was utterly shocked. The story Viola told was the same one she had seen in the bronze mirror when she first obtained it.

She and Manfred were the two people from that story. Yet, in their previous lives, they had never ended up together.

Viola was someone Manfred had saved in that past life. If not for him, she would have been run over by a carriage and died. So, she had been searching for a way to repay him.

It had taken her a long time to find both Manfred and Vivian in this life.

As it turned out, she was also deeply connected to Vivian's parents. If not for her help, Skylar would never have gone through everything that led her to meet Joe and eventually give birth to Vivian.

Everything that had happened was part of fate's plan, but also the result of Viola's intervention.

"So that's how it is. Then, can I bring Manfred back to my world with me?" Vivian asked.
