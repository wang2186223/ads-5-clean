Skylar would help her walk from upstairs to downstairs.

Her favorite activity was sitting together with Skylar to watch TV. The duo would chat about the characters on screen, sharing lighthearted moments. Though she still struggled to eat-managing only a few spoonfuls of oatmeal-she relied on nutritional supplements to maintain her energy.
