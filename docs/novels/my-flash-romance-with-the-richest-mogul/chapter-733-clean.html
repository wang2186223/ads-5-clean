Charles ignored Freddie’s earlier questions. He answered his last question with, “I’ll prepare a few flavors, so he has a few options.”

“No, this isn’t about flavors. Is he really Joe Martin?” Freddie felt like he was living in a dreamland.

Hadn’t Charles told him that he wasn’t from a powerful family? He’d been acting as an extra in many movies but was still a D–list actor, even after all these years.

When did he get to know Joe?

“Yeah,” Charles replied dismissively.

He had a range of pasta flavors stocked in his cupboard. He took one of every flavor.

Freddie stood by the kitchen door and looked into the living room.

Once Joe was left alone with Skylar, his icy expression melted. Freddie had no idea what they were talking about, but he could tell that her words were putting him in a good mood.

Magic. This had to be magic! A guy that Freddie had only heard in the news. was now the brother– in–law of the actor he managed.

Skylar glanced at her phone. Natalie had sent videos of a few people standing up to defend Charles. They revealed Felicia’s true colors. It wasn’t a singular occurrence. Dozens of people were voicing out.

“Skylar, I’ve forgotten that Joe is your husband. No one can hold a flame to the PR team of Martin Group. They’re awesome! Mr. Powell commented that finding all these people within a day was an

impossible task no one else had ever managed to achieve. How impressive.”

After Skylar replied to Natalie’s text, she looked at Joe and said, “Sorry for the inconvenience, but thank you either way.”

Skylar’s courtesy caused Joe’s eyes to darken.

Everyone was astounded by how matters were developing. They weren’t expecting such a plot twist.

few celebrities who’d disappeared from public view for no apparent reason explained why they had done so. They described how Felicia had bullied them.

New information kept popping up. One couldn’t finish a video before another popped up.

Charles had originally been the eye of the hurricane. Now, he was no longer their focus.

However, there were still numerous fans of Felicia’s who were defending her.

Many people also said they were waiting for Charles‘ response.

However, the number of people coming forward was increasing exponentially. In addition, some of the brands who’d announced the cancellation of their partnership with Charles had deleted their announcement posts. It was unclear what was going on.

Then, Charles posted a story describing what had happened. He asked the people who’d filmed or taken pictures of them to submit it as evidence. This would prove that he wasn’t telling lies. They would also take legal action against those who’d filmed them secretly.

Charles‘ strongly worded post changed public opinion entirely.

Everyone commented on Felicia’s post and questioned her about what happened.

Felicia’s PR team was working overtime.

In the end, Adele said helplessly, “Martin Group’s PR team were the ones who retaliated against us. In addition, they were supported by a lawyer, not to mention those groups of people speaking out against you. Even if we hired influencers to speak for you, it wouldn’t help.”

It was 8:00 pm when the tables had finally turned on their advantageous situation.

Felicia was pale when she asked, “Are you sure it’s Martin Group’s PR team? Why would they participate in matters of the entertainment industry?”

Adele had no idea: She replied, “We can’t figure that out either, Ms. Ingram. Many brands have announced they want to break their collaboration contracts with you. The situation does not look promising. Prepare yourself for the worst, Ms. Ingram.”

They weren’t clear regarding the situation. However, Adele knew. Her predictions had come true.

Adele exclaimed, “Skylar’s too capable.”

Felicia bit her lip and said, “I have Rowan and Mr. Ziegler’s number. I’ll ask them to help me arrange a meeting with Joe. Perhaps he’ll stop helping Charles and Skylar after seeing me.”
