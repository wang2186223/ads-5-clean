Another two months passed when Skylar came across some unexpected news about Raven.

The inte was flooded with reports about her.

During a recent live stream, Raven had suddenly fainted, and shortly afterward, one of her studio employees leaked that she had been hospitalized and diagnosed with late-stage pancreatic cancer.

While this wasn't entirely shocking-after all, even public figures were not exempt from life's trials, like illness and death-it was Raven's reaction that stirred controversy.

Apparently, while in the hospital, Raven caused a huge scene, claiming to be Joe's wife and demanding the best doctors to treat her.

Unfortunately for her, her outburst was caught on camera and uploaded online. The clip quickly went viral, with many ridiculing her, saying that her illness had also affected her mind.

People mocked her for trying to attach herself to Joe Martin, the CEO of the Martin Group.

But no matter how skilled a doctor might be, late-stage cancer often offered no hope.

If Raven had come to the hospital six months or even three months earlier, her prognosis might not have been as grim.

That evening, Skylar, Joe, Paul, and Rowan were having dinner together when Paul received a phone call.

Initially, no one paid much attention to Paul taking the call, but his sudden outburst caught everyone's attention.

"You're crazy! If you're sick, just stay in the hospital and get treated. Why on earth should Mr. Martin visit you? And why are you calling me about this? Let me make it very clear-stop dreaming.
