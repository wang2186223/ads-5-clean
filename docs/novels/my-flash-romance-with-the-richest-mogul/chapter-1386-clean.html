Stephen was incredibly handsome in Rosa's eyes.

Sometimes, when he looked at her earnestly, it felt as though his eyes could speak, as if they were telling her that he liked her.

Of course, this might just be her imagination.

After all, she really needed a man who could love her deeply and wholeheartedly right now.

And just now, with just a single glance exchanged between them, Stephen had kissed her out of the blue. It completely caught her off guard.

"Do you... Do you know what you're doing?" Rosa asked, catching her breath while blushing.

Stephen seemed equally flustered, probably not expecting himself to suddenly kiss her. He scratched his head, his face red, and fumbled for words.

"Please don't be mad at me. I... I don't know what came over me just now. I couldn't control myself. I think I've fallen for you. So, I acted on impulse. But just now, I really wanted to kiss you."

This clumsy explanation left Rosa stunned for a moment, and then she couldn't help but laugh.

"You like me? Do you know I'm at least five years older than you? Besides, I saw you making a phone call the other day. Wasn't that your girlfriend? If you like me, what about your girlfriend? How are you going to explain this to her?"

Stephen scratched his head again.

"I... I do have a girlfriend, but she's the one clinging to me. I only agreed to date her because I didn't know what else to do.

"I used to think that feelings like love were all so abstract and unimportant. But... But since I met you, I can't stop myself from wanting to be close to you. My heart raced uncontrollably since the first time I saw you."

Rosa was taken aback by Stephen's sincere words. He had liked her from their first meeting?

Thinking back now, it did seem plausible. Otherwise, why would someone care so much about a stranger? Why would he go out of his way to help her unconditionally?

Rosa's heart began to race. Even

though she knew she didn't like Stephen much, she still felt an emptiness in her heart and couldn't resist his affection.

So, she instinctively hugged Stephen again.

"Prove it to me then. Kiss me again. Show me if you're truly sincere or just sweet-talking," Rosa said. Her voice was soft and seductive

almost hypnotic.

Stephen's eyes widened in joy, and he looked at her excitedly. "I... I really like you! It's true! If I'm lying, I swear-"

Before he could finish, Rosa kissed him, and he deepened the kiss.

As Rosa closed her eyes, the deep affection in Stephen's eyes instantly turned cold. He knew he had won over another woman's heart.

Soon, everything Rosa had would belong to him.

Compared to Simona, he thought Rosa was much easier to manipulate as she was vulnerable and lonely.

However, he had no intention of letting go of Simona. Between the two, Simona was younger, wealthier, and held a higher status in Jipsburg. That was the circle he truly wanted to enter!

Little did Stephen know, his passionate kiss with Rosa on the street wasn't just seen by Skylar-it had been captured on video.

Aiken soon received the video.

The camera used was of high quality, ensuring the video was crystal clear. Even in the dim light of the evening, the faces of the two were unmistakable.

Aiken's expression turned icy as he watched the video. It was as if he were staring at something utterly repulsive.

After that, he sent a short clip from the video to Skylar.

"You know this woman, don't you? If she harasses Joe again, show her this clip to shut her up," Aiken said.
