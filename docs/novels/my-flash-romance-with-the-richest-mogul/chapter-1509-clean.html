Finn fell silent, unable to respond immediately. Seeing Finn speechless, Harvey wasn't surprised.

Even if he didn't know the full story, it didn't change the fact that Finn had abandoned Janine and them. Harvey took out his phone and started to order an Uber.

"Harvey, I'm your father. I know I didn't come looking for you all these years, but there were reasons for that. I've also heard that you've all been doing well, so I didn't want to disturb you. Just get in the car, and we can talk," Finn said, his tone calmer, despite the anger still simmering inside him.

He knew his children were still upset with him for abandoning Janine and them. But given the circumstances back then, any man in his shoes would've made the same choice.

He wasn't a saint, so everything he did back then had been for his own sake. However, now, it had become the reason his children held such animosity toward him.

He wondered how Janine had raised them to hate him so much.

Harvey was about to flat-out refuse, but then something crossed his mind. "Alright, I'd also like to know why you suddenly decided to find us."

He opened the back door and sat inside. Finn had planned for Harvey to sit in the passenger seat, but since Harvey didn't want to be that close to him, he let it go. Although Finn wasn't satisfied with Harvey's behavior, he was relieved that Harvey had agreed to get into his car.

The small details didn't matter-not now.

Harvey didn't give Finn his home address but instead directed him to a hotel. Harvey had no intention of letting Finn know where he lived, ensuring he couldn't show up uninvited later.

Finn followed Harvey's instructions and input the address into the car's navigation system, but he didn't rush to speak.

Despite Finn's silence, Harvey was growing impatient. "You said it was a long story, right? Then start talking."

He wanted to see what could have led someone to abandon their wife and children, only to show up years later without any shame.

Finn sighed deeply before speaking.

"Back then, I did have an accident. After I was rescued, I had no chance to return home. My leg was injured, and later, due to other reasons couldn't come back. But I never stopped thinking about you all."

"Enough of this. I'm not a child anymore. You can try using these excuses on kids, but I'm not buying it. I can guess exactly why you had an accident and why you abandoned us, leading everyone to think you were dead.

"So stop wasting my time with ridiculous lies. I only want to know why you decided to show up now."
