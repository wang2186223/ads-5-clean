Seeing that Ursula seemed frightened, Skylar continued, “Those who did wrong have already been punished. You don’t have to keep hurting yourself. I can help you deal with this necklace. After that, you can focus on getting well again, just like before.”

Ursula’s eyes suddenly turned red, as if overwhelmed with a deep sense of grievance. In the

end, she couldn’t hold back and burst into tears.

Skylar didn’t say anything further and waited patiently for Ursula to calm down.

Anyone who had experienced what Ursula did would be unable to bear it. At such a young age, she had seen the darkest side of the world, with her closest family member dying right in

front of her. How could she not be afraid?

Ursula cried as she ate her meal.

“When I was little, I envied other kids whose moms were with them every day. My mom had

to work in the city and could only come home to see me once a year.

“Later, when I grew up, I came to find her. At first, she didn’t want me to stay and work as a maid for the Ruell family, but I wanted to be with her.

“I thought she was afraid that I couldn’t handle the hardship. But if she could, I could do it too. I wasn’t a child anymore.

“But I never imagined that they would bully my mom. She had to endure it because she needed the job. She said she needed to save up to buy a house for my brother to get married.

“My dad was a gambler, and he died early. So, my whole family relied on my mom. That was when I realized my mom had been living in hell all these years, enduring it for my brother and

1. me.

“I never expected them to be so inhumane. They drugged me, took advantage of me when my mom was not around, and then gave me money just like they did with my mom.

“She wanted to report it to the police, but they weren’t scared at all. They said that their family lawyers could make sure my mom and I would spend our lives in prison and that we should think carefully about whether we wanted to take the money and keep serving them or go to jail.

“We were terrified, especially my mom.

“They’re monsters! When they tried to attack my mom that day, she fought back and accidentally fell down the stairs while trying to escape. Then they used money to try to silence me, but I couldn’t accept it.

“I know this necklace I’m wearing is cursed. One day, I was crying in the park for a whole afternoon without realizing it. When night fell, an old man sat beside me and asked why I was

crying. I told him that someone had hurt me and my family and that I was scared.

“Then, he comforted me and gave me this necklace. He said that it could help me punish the bad people, but I would have to pay a price.

“He said I probably wouldn’t get married in this lifetime. But I’m already so dirty, I never

thought about getting married. I don’t deserve it.”

Skylar quietly listened to Ursula as she told her story. But her eyes darkened when she heard that an old man in the park had given the necklace to Ursula.

So, the person who had been missing for years had reappeared.

She didn’t believe that their encounter in the park was a coincidence. It must have been deliberate. That person wanted to use Ursula to deal with the Ruell family.

If Ursula hadn’t met her, she would have carried this necklace for the rest of her life, suffering not only from poor health and a shortened lifespan but also a lack of prospects for marriage.

Ursula took a deep breath before saying, “But after meeting you, Ms. Sullivan, I calmed down. My mom wanted me and my brother to live well. I can’t destroy myself just for revenge, especially when they’ve already received their punishment.”

One of them was on the brink of death, and another had lost his family and was constantly attempting suicide. It was enough. Those people could only live in fear for the rest of their

lives.

But she hadn’t done anything wrong. Why should she suffer along with them for her whole life?

Every time she saw the necklace, it reminded her of them and what they had done to her and her mother, trapping her in a constant state of fear.

For some reason, last night, Ursula seemed to wake up suddenly and even had a dream in which her mother told her to trust Skylar and not to let herself be trapped by this pain for the

rest of her life.
