Alexander glared at Caleb and said, “Your intentions toward Skylar are painfully clear. Stop staring at Skylar and find another girl for yourself.”

“I’m just a boy. Aren’t you a little too young to have a great–grandson? Based on my predictions, you’ll be waiting for at least another decade. Please wait patiently. There’s no hurry.”

“Go away! I won’t give a damn even if you stay a bachelor for the rest of your life,” Alexander scolded, rolling his eyes.

Caleb wasn’t doing particularly well. A great–grandson was entirely out of the picture.

Caleb chuckled and said, “I won’t stay single forever. I’m the of desire for a million girls. They’re lining up to date me. My problem is that I’m not shallow enough. I like girls like Skylar. If someone like her comes along, I’ll marry her for sure.”

“In that case, you can stop dreaming. There won’t be another girl like Skylar,” Alexander huffed.

Caleb pursed his lips and said, “I’ll get to work.”

Their age gap was too big. He didn’t want to continue talking to Alexander.

Skylar and Joe arrived in the parking lot.

On the way there, they passed a bunch of Skylar’s colleagues. All of them were extremely curious about Joe.

Skylar only introduced Joe as her boyfriend.

They arrived in the parking lot, and Joe opened the car door. As he did so, Joe scrutinized Skylar. He asked, “Why did you introduce me as your boyfriend?”

It seemed like she hadn’t gotten used to him as her husband.

Skylar opened the door to the passenger’s seat. She cared little about Joe’s question. She replied, “My colleagues don’t know that we’re married. I’ve never told them about it. If I introduce you as my husband, it might come as a shock.”

“Next time, you can introduce me as your husband,” Joe insisted. He pulled out his documents, and as he spoke, he placed special emphasis on the word “husband“.

Skylar grinned and replied, “Alright.”

She would definitely introduce Joe as her husband when an opportunity presented itself.

Joe pulled her into his embrace and kissed her hard. He had wanted to kiss her from the moment he saw her through the window. She was his woman.

Skylar was shocked.

She looked around and sighed in relief once she confirmed that no one was around them. If journalists were stalking Joe for a scandalous picture, her marriage with Joe would become public knowledge.

“Why do I feel like we’re cheating? Our marriage has been legalized. No one can do anything to us even if we make out right here,” Joe argued.

He touched his lips, which were still warm with Skylar’s heat. His lips were smudged with her lipstick. She usually wore pastel lipsticks that didn’t make a bold statement. The stains on his fingers reminded him of

his impulsive actions.

Joe couldn’t help himself. He sealed her lips with his once more.

Skylar’s eyes widened. He wasn’t scared of letting others know about their relationship at all.

In the last few days, she’d felt like a pair of eyes were watching every action. She felt extremely unsafe. However, her paranoia dissolved in Joe’s kiss.

A few feet away, Christopher sat in a car, watching them kiss like they were the only people in the world. His eyes glinted furiously. Skylar was his woman!

Joe was evil. How could he be with Skylar?

A telephoto lens was aimed at the couple in another car. It snapped a photo of their actions and was immediately sent to Molly.

When she saw the picture, her expression turned somber. She showed it to Edwin and commented, “It looks like Joe really adores Skylar.”

Joe’s expression darkened. He said, “A man will never stay loyal to a woman forever. However, even if he has fallen for her, I will not give them my blessing.”

“I’ve just found out that Skylar has an ex. We can make use of that fact,” Molly proposed.
