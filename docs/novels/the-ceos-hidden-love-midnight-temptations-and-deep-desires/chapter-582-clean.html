Chapter 582

Karen reckoned that if she got involved with a guy like Jeffrey, the chances of things going south were nearly 100%. She wasn't so full of herself as to think she could be the one-in-a-million exception to his track record.

In the end, it would likely just result in a breakup with wasted time and emotions. What would be the point of such a relationship? Was it just for his handsome face or his athletic build?

Karen scoffed at the thought.

Sure, he was easy on the eyes—no denying that. But ever since they’d had their fallout, things hadn't been the same. While he was physically striking, their connection felt completely out of sync; for her, the emotional friction was almost unbearable.

All things considered, there was no reason for them to be together. It was better to just forget it.

“If you had already made up your mind, why did you tell me you'd think about it?” Jeffrey’s voice broke through her thoughts.

“What, did you think I had a choice back then?” Karen figured Jeffrey wasn't dense; he should have caught on to her hesitation that day. Him coming to press her for an answer today felt unnecessary.

“So, what? You're just going to keep going on these blind dates?” Jeffrey tried to keep his voice steady, mindful of the children playing nearby.

“What's it to you? It's not like I'm your girlfriend,” Karen retorted. She pulled out her smartphone and showed him a chat screen. “See this? My parents introduced us. We've been talking, and we actually hit it off. You should probably move on and find someone else to pursue.”

Karen believed that for Jeffrey, it was all about the chase—a fixation on what he couldn't easily have. She knew his charm worked on plenty of women; with his rugged good looks and bad-boy persona, he could have easily been a star. Even she had once been momentarily captivated by that face.

But she knew that once the novelty wore off, the interest would vanish. The idea that she could somehow change a man like Jeffrey was not just ridiculous—it was delusional.

Sure enough, Jeffrey saw the last message on her screen: a sweet “Goodnight, sleep tight!” from the other man. Their conversation appeared to be warming up.

Jeffrey clenched his jaw so hard it made a sound. “Delete it, Karen. Now.”

“Why on earth should I listen to you?”

Jeffrey’s eyes flashed with anger. “You really think I won't cause a scene right here?”

“Are you out of your mind?”

“Don’t make me say it again!”

Karen snorted and turned away to shield Abigail, choosing to ignore him. There were kids around; she was certain Jeffrey wouldn't do anything truly drastic in front of them.

But while he didn't touch her, he suddenly reached out and snatched her phone from her hand.

“Jeffrey! What are you doing?”

“What's the unlock code?”

“I'm not telling you!”

He smirked coldly. “Fine.”

He set Langston down, then took Karen's phone and hurled it toward the pavement. The device hit the ground with a sharp crack, the screen shattering into a web of broken glass.

Passersby stopped to stare, their expressions a mix of shock and curiosity. The sudden move left Karen stunned, and the children looked on with wide, startled eyes.

“Jeffrey, what is wrong with you?” Karen hurried to embrace both kids, trying to soothe their fear.

“I'll get you a new one,” Jeffrey said, his voice dropping to a low, possessive tone. “I don't want you using that phone to talk to other men anymore.”
