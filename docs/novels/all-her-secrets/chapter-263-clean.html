“Miss Hart, Catherine is my student. If there is anything she does not do well, I will advise her. As teachers, aren’t you going too far in making such accusations against the students in my class without knowing the situation?”

“I advise you to take care of the students in your class and improve their grades when you have time so that you won’t be criticized again at the next homeroom teacher assembly.”

These words hit the point!

It could be said that the homeroom teacher of Class 8 had now mentioned the homeroom teacher of Class 7’s sore spot, and saying those words in front of Catherine made the homeroom teacher of Class 7 more embarrassed.
