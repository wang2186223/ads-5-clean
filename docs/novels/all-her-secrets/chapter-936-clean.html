"It's getting late. I have to leave. See you next time, Grandpa!" Rylan said.

When Aaron heard that Rylan was leaving, his face darkened. Rylan had only been here for a short while, and now he was planning to leave.

Rylan felt increasingly uncomfortable. He wanted to spend more time with his grandfather, but he was worried that if he didn't leave soon, there might be trouble. Despite his reluctance to leave, he stood up. As he was about to leave the yard, Aaron called out, "Rylan!"
