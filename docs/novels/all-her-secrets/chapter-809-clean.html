Catherine wasn't exaggerating; Rylan could indeed get a meal based on his appearance. He could easily deceive quite a few naive girls with his handsome looks. Otherwise, the school wouldn't have been filled with rumors and gossip about Catherine because of Rylan during this time.

After hearing Catherine's sarcasm, Rylan didn't get angry. Instead, the smile on his face became more apparent. "Catherine, I like the way you speak. I didn't expect you to appreciate my appearance as well."

Rylan knew very well that his face was attractive to girls, but he never cared about these things. But if Catherine liked it, it would be a different story. He didn't mind using his looks to his advantage.

Seeing that she couldn't shake off Rylan, Catherine could only let him follow.

The crowd discreetly observed Catherine and Rylan, whispering and gossiping.

"Isn't that Catherine? I remember the handsome guy who used to be by her side wasn't this one, right?"

"Who knows, maybe he's her new boyfriend."

"Haven't you seen how capable Catherine is? If we had half of her abilities, we wouldn't be single until now."

Endless rumors and gossip surrounded them, but Catherine was accustomed to these discussions, expressionlessly walking toward the food counter.
