Zobber was focusing on completing the new mission Catherine assigned her, with no spare time to gossip.

The holiday had come to an end, and Catherine returned to school. After Beatrice left, the dorm room seemed more spacious and comfortable.

As soon as Catherine entered the room, she saw Amelia sitting on a chair with her feet propped up. Amelia was watching a live-streaming video on her computer.

Hearing the sound, Amelia turned her head and saw Catherine. She waved at Catherine excitedly and asked, ” Catherine, do you play the mobile game ‘PlayerUnknown’s Storms?’”

Catherine nodded slightly. She had indeed been playing that mobile game recently.

Amelia looked down on Isabella for being a bookworm and not playing games. Seeing Catherine nod, Amelia became extremely excited and quickly introduced her new idol.

“Catherine, since you play this game, you must know the top player, Hero. He’s become very popular on the internet, not only because he’s good at the game, but also because he’s incredibly handsome. He’s been dubbed ‘Gaming Adonis’ by his fans.”

Amelia excitedly pointed at the computer screen, almost dragging the man from inside the computer out and presenting him in front of Catherine.

Considering Amelia’s excessive excitement, Catherine glanced up at the computer screen.

Indeed, there was a pretty handsome face displayed on the screen.

The young man did have a handsome face. He had a refined oval face, a prominent nose, and three- dimensional facial features. He looked truly alluring.

Catherine took a quick glance and noticed the nameplate placed on the table. It was “Hero.”

For the past few days, there had been a player with the same name constantly sending her friend requests in the game.

Catherine had never accepted any of his requests. She never friended people she wasn’t familiar with.

Catherine’s lack of interest didn’t diminish Amelia’s enthusiasm. She continued to talk non-stop, introducing her new idol.

“Catherine, let me tell you, Hero is not only amazing at gaming, but he’s also incredibly intelligent. He’s in the same university as us. It’s said that he got accepted into Styre University this year. And because he’s participating in the competition, the school has granted him an exemption from physical training. Isn’t that amazing?”

Catherine didn’t say anything, as she had no interest in these gaming celebrities. Faced with Amelia’s enthusiasm, she just wanted to rest.

At the Golden Star Training Center, Hero’s teammates noticed that Hero was still staring blankly at his phone, which made them curious.

“Cap, hasn’t that mysterious player friended you yet?”

Hero furrowed his brow and remained silent. He had sent countless friend requests, but there was no response. He also noticed that the mysterious player hadn’t been online recently. He had checked this player’s game record and realized that she had only played a few dozen matches this season, which didn’t seem like the record of a professional player.

If this player was just an ordinary player, then they must have been an incredibly talented one. Having someone like them on the team would undoubtedly enhance their chances of winning the competition.

Knowing Hero’s dilemma, his teammates decided to give him some advice.

“Cap, I don’t think it will be difficult to find this person.”

Hero immediately looked up at his teammate. “Do you know this player?”

The teammate shook his head and said, “Of course not. If I knew such an amazing player, I would have bragged about it every day. Jacob is a computer genius at Styre University. We can trace this player’s location based on the IP address. Once we have the location, it should be much easier to find this player.”

Hero agreed with this idea. Even though it seemed a bit intrusive to invade someone’s privacy, in order to find this talent, he was willing to give it a try. Once he had made up his mind, he immediately stood up. “I’ll go talk with Jacob about it.”

In the female dormitory at Styre University, the sound of an alarm clock woke up the sleeping residents.

Isabella was always the first one up, followed by Amelia, and finally Catherine.

Unlike the other two, although Catherine woke up the latest, she was the quickest to leave. She was efficient and swift in her actions. With a simple tidying up, she could be ready to go.

As Catherine was about to leave, Amelia quickly called out to her, “Catherine, wait!”

Catherine turned around. Amelia pulled out a book and handed it to her. ” Catherine, your book. I went to pick it up for you yesterday.”

Catherine glanced down and reached out to accept it, saying softly, “Thanks.

This kind of thing was always taken care of by Ronin, and Catherine had never picked up her own books before. When she was at Loyalty Academy, her backpack was always kept in the drawer, with

books from various subjects inside.

The way classes were conducted in college was a bit different, with no fixed classrooms. Depending on the course, students had to bring their own books to school for each class.

If it weren’t for Amelia’s reminder, Catherine would have just walked straight to the classroom like that.

Watching Catherine’s departing figure, Amelia couldn’t help but squint her eyes and turn to Isabella behind her.

“Bella, don’t you think Catherine has a great presence? She’s so cool when she said ‘thanks’ just now.”

Isabella chuckled and grabbed Amelia’s hand. “Alright, stop daydreaming and go to class. I don’t want to be late.”

Catherine walked downstairs and saw Ronin already waiting there. Holding a delicate food container, he rushed up to Catherine.

“Catherine, your breakfast.”

The lid of the food container was transparent, through which Catherine could see the delicious breakfast inside. All of it was her favorite, perfectly suited to her taste.

Catherine reached out and took it, asking in return, “Did you eat?”

“I did,” Ronin replied without hesitation. He had woken up at five in the morning just to prepare breakfast for Catherine. He knew that she wouldn’t be accustomed to the breakfast in the cafeteria. Since he was accompanying her to university, he had to take care of her from every aspect.

As for himself, he would just eat an egg later.

Ronin caught sight of the book in Catherine’s hand and widened his eyes in surprise.

“Catherine, you went to pick up the books yourself?”

He hadn’t known about this before. It was after his roommate told him that he knew he had to go pick up the books himself. After picking up his own books, he went to Catherine’s class and picked up her books as well.

Catherine raised the book in her hand and said, “Amelia picked them up for me.”

Hearing Catherine’s response, Ronin felt a little unhappy. His job had been taken over again. But it was a good thing. Catherine’s roommates weren’t annoying and were willing to take care of her.

Ronin was happy that Catherine seemed satisfied with the breakfast he had prepared.

“Catherine, I’ll accompany you to class.”
