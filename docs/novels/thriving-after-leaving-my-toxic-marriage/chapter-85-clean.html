Chapter 85

Sarah's eyes sparkled with excitement as soon as she heard the words.She quickly grabbed Chris' arm. "Chris, if Alexander wants to see me, I have to go."

Without waiting for a response, she dashed towards the stairs, her mind racing with thoughts of the priceless necklace Chris had mentioned.

Could it be that Alexander had taken a liking to her and wanted to give her the necklace?

The idea of a necklace worth billions of dollars made her heart pound.

Alexander must have realized she was better than Wendy, and that's why he wanted to see her alone.

With a mix of excitement and nerves, she reached the door at the far end of the second floor. Her cheeks were flushed, and her hand trembled slightly as she knocked.

The door creaked open on its own, revealing a pitch-black room.

"Alexander?" she called out cautiously, her voice echoing in the darkness.No response.

The darkness swallowed her up, and fear crept into her heart, making her shiver.

Suddenly, the door slammed shut with a deafening bang, trapping her in the darkness.

"Alexander! Are you there?" Her voice trembled as she frantically searched the walls for a light switch.

Her fingers only found cold, hard walls.

Just as she was about to break down, a sharp click sounded, and the lights suddenly flicked on.

The blinding light made her instinctively close her eyes. When she opened them again, she saw a man in the room with her.

It was the same man who had tried to assault Wendy in the garden earlier.

Sarah's face went pale, and she bolted towards the door.

"Sarah, stop right there!" The man roared, stepping forward to block her path.

"You can't run! How dare you deceive me! You said that woman was a promiscuous slut and encouraged me to harass her. Now, because of your lies, I've lost my deal with the Turner family and have been kicked out of Aurora City. You ruined me! You owve me!"

The man screamed hysterically, and foul-smelling saliva spattered onto her face. His crazed demeanor terrifying Sarah.

"I didn't lie!" Sarah was terrified but still tried to defend herself, her voice shrill.

"She is a slut who flirts around. Who knows why Alexander protects her!Besides, it was your own evil intentions that got you into trouble. What does it have to do with me?"

The man was furious, shouting. "If it weren't for you, I wouldn't have noticed her! If it weren't for your promises tha the Collins family would cooperate with me. I wouldn't have been foolish enough to provoke her!This was all your plan from the start!"

"You! Don't slander me! I have no idea what you're talking about!" Saruh's face twisted with fear as she tried to push the man away, but he grabbed her again.

She looked at the crazed man in front of her with fear in her eyes, her voice trembling, "Let go of me! I'm'from the Collins family! This is the Turner family's party. If you dare to mess with me. you'll regret it!"

"You ruined me! If it weren't for you, I wouldn't be in this situation!" The man's eyes were bloodshot, and he looked like he wanted to tear Sarah apart.

Sarah was scared out of her mind, desperately swinging her purse at the man. "Get away from me! Even if I did tell you to harass her, so what?You've already pissed off the Turner family. Do you want to mess with the Collins family too? Think about what you're doing!"

Taking advantage of the man's momentary distraction, Sarah lunged for the door handle.

"Where do you think you're going?" Francis, who had been hiding in the corner,grabbed Sarah and twisted her arm behind her back, dragging her to the sofa and foreing her to sit down.

"What do you want? Let me go!" Sarah struggled desperately."Help! Is anyone there? Help!"

At that moment, two people walked in from another door, and the man was forcibly taken away by several bodyguards.

Sarah's eyes widened in terror, her pupils dilating as she saw Wendy and Alexander approaching her.

"Wendy! Alexander!" She screamed in fear. "Alexander, it's not what it

looks like. I can explain."

But when she met Alexander's cold, disgusted gaze, her heart sank,realizing she was doomed.

She turned to anger, pointing at Wendy and cursing, "You bitch! You set me up! You're so cruel!"

Wendy remained calm, staring at her with a mocking smile. "You've repeatedly caused trouble for me. Do you really think the Collins family can protect you forever?"

"I'm from the Collins family! What's wrong with Ethan backing me up?Aren't you relying on Alexander to support you? I did nothing wrong.Stop slandering me!" Sarah screamed.

Alexander's gaze was sharp, fixed on Sarah, making her feel scared. "Sarah,can you say that the incident in the garden today had nothing to do with you? Why did you do it?"

"Alexander! You have to believe me. It wasn't me; it was her!" Sarah desperately pointed at Wendy. "She must have brought that man here to frame me! I'm innocent!"

Meanwhile, Lydia had changed out of her dirty dress and into a new,elegant gown in her room upstairs.

Just as she was about to go downstairs, she heard a familiar scream.

Lydia was puzzled and approached the door, listening for a moment. She recognized Sarah's voice.

It sounded like she was arguing with someone. Listening more closely,she confirmed it was Wendy.

The tio seemed to be in a hested argument. with Alexander's voice mixed in.

Not daring to delay, Lydia hurried downstairs to find Ethan, her face anxious, "Ethan, quickly go upstairs. Sarah seems to be arguing with Wendy!"

"What? That bitch dares to bully my daughter!" Juniper instantly flew into a rage and rushed towards the stairs.

Ethan and Lydia hurriedly followed to the second floor.

As soon as they pushed open the door, they saw Sarah crying bitterly.pleading with Alexander, while Wendy and Francis stood by, watching coldly,

Juniper's heart ached, and she rushed forward to support Sarah, "Sarah!What's wrong? Who bullied you?”

Turning sharply, she pointed at Wendy. "You bitch! What did you do to Sarah?"

Ethan's face was serious, his gaze locked on Alexander, his tone firm. "Mr.Turner, the Collins family may not be as powerful as the Turner family、but we are not to be bullied!"
