The garden was well maintained by the gardener and looked exceptionally beautiful, but Spencer

After elaborating, Ben added sorrowfully, “We held onto the last sliver of hope when we went to see Dr.

However, before taking his last breath, Dr. Belch told us that his apprentice, Brancesco, would be able

“I'll think of something too.” A frown creased Spencer's forehead. “We can't give up as long as we have

“That's right.” Ben nodded. “We've never given up and have been trying our best to find a cure.”

“Mr. Zachary is all the family has now. If he were to collapse, it would be the end of the Nacht

household's century-long legacy...”

Spencer's eyes watered as he spoke. “I promised Mr. Henry to watch over Mr. Zachary. How am I going

to face Mr. Henry if anything were to happen to his grandson?”
