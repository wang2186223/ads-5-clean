Zachary turned his head and saw Charlotte, who was in a white outfit, looking cool yet alluring. Pulling

him behind her, she shielded and protected him like a queen.

On the other hand, the three women were sprawled out on the couch, looking disheveled.

Even with a different personality, this woman is quite charming!

and the other one was cursing at Charlotte. "You b*tch, how dare you..."

Charlotte frowned at her response. What's going on? Do they know me?
