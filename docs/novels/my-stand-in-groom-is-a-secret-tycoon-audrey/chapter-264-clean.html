Bruno was really upset, his voice sharp and carrying a bit of an accent.

Everyone in the room was rattled. Damian was the first to regain composure and asked, "Mr. Hayes, do you have evidence for your claim? Baselessly accusing a company veteran could be quite disheartening." Damian was not taking sides; he believed that everything needed to be supported by evidence.

"Evidence, right?" Alistair asked with a smirk, pulled out his phone, and played a recording.

Soon, the voices of Dominic and Bruno came from the phone.

Occasionally, people could hear the clinking of glasses.

At the end, Bruno's sycophantic voice sounded, "Here's to Mr. Hayes's success! Once Rhodes Enterprises falls, Audrey, a defenseless woman, will be at your mercy."

Hearing this, Bruno was panicked.

He glared at Alistair in horror, his eyes wide with anger, wishing he could kill Alistair immediately.

An executive said, "Bruno, after working together for so many years, I never expected you to be such a jerk."

Another executive scolded, "You've been slacking off over the years, and yet your benefits keep improving. Has the company ever mistreated you? How could you do such a heartless thing?"

Someone commented, "Changing jobs is normal, but betraying your former employer is horrible. Do you really think Mr. Hayes would hire someone like you?"

Several executives were furious, pointing fingers at Bruno as they reprimanded him.

Bruno had intended to argue with Audrey, but the criticism from the executives angered him instead.

He asked, "Don't you understand that to succeed, you need to align yourself with those in power? You're just jealous because Mr. Hayes values my abilities." Hearing this, the other executives all shook their heads in dismay.

Bruno was hopeless.

Audrey had reached her limit and seized the opportunity, saying, "Since you think Dominic's someone you should side with, I won't force you to stay with us." Bruno's heart sank when he heard this.

Audrey looked at him with cold eyes, her face expressionless, and said, "Bruno, you're fired."

Hearing he was fired, Bruno instantly panicked.

Getting fired and resigning were two completely different things.

If Bruno got fired, not only would he be blacklisted in the industry, but Dominic probably wouldn't arrange a job for him as promised.

Thinking about this, Bruno got anxious and pointed at Audrey in anger, saying, "Audrey, you've really been fooled by that guy!"

The more Bruno talked, the angrier he became, and he threatened, "I'm the Marketing Director and everyone in the department is loyal to me."

Audrey watched Bruno coldly, curious to see just how shameless he could be.

As expected, Bruno tried desperately to defend himself, "I was planning to do a proper handover since we've worked together for so long, and then resign.

"It's unfair that you suddenly fire me. Aren't you worried about demoralizing your employees and bringing the company down?"

Audrey's eyes flashed with sarcasm as she countered seriously, "If I keep troublemakers like you at the company, it might really go under."
