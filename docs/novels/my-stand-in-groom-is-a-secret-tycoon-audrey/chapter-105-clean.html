Although Fiona was Eliza's biological mother, they had only been together for a short time, and she didn't dare to force Eliza to abort the child.

Helpless, she could only sigh and say, "Since you've thought it through, just don't regret it in the future."

She tucked Eliza in and said softly, "Don't go anywhere for the next two days. Stay in the hospital and protect the baby. I'll pay for your hospitalization fees." Eliza nodded obediently.

But as soon as Fiona left, she called Dominic's mother. "Hello, Mrs. Hayes. I'm Eliza."

Zephyra's expression darkened when she saw who was calling. She said sullenly, "Why are you still calling me?"

The thought of the company's stock price plummeting and the scandal of Dominic having an affair with someone at the bridal shop made Zephyra furious.

She thought Eliza caused all of this to happen.

Zephyra thought, 'Ever since Dominic met this woman, he had been unlucky. Could this woman be Dominic's jinx?'

At the thought of this, Zephyra's tone became even worse. She scolded coldly, "Shameless bitch, our family has been ruined by you. Why don't you take a look at the occasion when you're seducing men? Stay away from my family in the future." Zephyra didn't feel that Dominic had done anything wrong. Instead, she pushed all the blame to Eliza.

Hearing that, Eliza's expression did not change at all. She only said calmly, "Mrs. Hayes, I'm pregnant." Zephyra was stunned. Then, she heard Eliza say, "The child is Dominic's."

This was the first time Audrey slept alone after getting married. She found it difficult to fall asleep.

When she woke up the next day, she saw dark circles under her eyes and sighed deeply.

Even though she and Alistair had only been married for less than half a month, Alistair had influenced her deeply in her daily life and other aspects.

She even hoped to smell the fragrance of breakfast, as usual, the moment she opened her eyes.

Her phone on the dresser suddenly vibrated with a buzz.

Audrey picked it up and took a look.

It was a message from Alistair. [I've arrived safely. Don't worry.]

Audrey knew he must have arrived last night. He must be afraid it would affect her sleep, so he sent a message in the morning to inform her that he was safe. Audrey watched silently for a while and did not reply in the end.

She didn't know how to reply.

Audrey could not get over it when she thought about how Alistair had lied to her.

She was too afraid that Alistair would hurt her like Dominic.

She feared falling into the whirlpool of love again and being hurt badly.

Taking advantage of Alistair's return to Willowdale, she had to sort out her thoughts and decide whether to continue with him.

After putting on makeup, Audrey rushed to the company without eating breakfast.

Thinking of the friction during last night's anniversary celebration, she had expected Eliza would come to cause trouble at the company. However, she did not even see Eliza after working the entire morning.

Audrey couldn't help but be a little curious. She twirled her pen and pondered, 'What's going on? Did Eliza suddenly change?'

Although she had a peaceful morning, she started to feel uneasy.

Afraid Eliza was holding back a big move to mess with her, Audrey quickly called Emily into her office to ask about the situation.

"Did you see Eliza today?" Audrey asked.

"Audrey, don't you know?"

"Know what?" Audrey was confused.

Emily's eyes immediately lit up. She lowered her voice and gossiped excitedly. "Last night, Mr. and Mrs. Rhodes and Eliza got chased out of the Barrett family's anniversary celebration. I think they said their invitations were fake. "Mr. Rhodes felt embarrassed and slapped Eliza. Eliza fell and almost had a miscarriage. She's still recuperating in the hospital."

Emily's eyes were filled with a gloating smile when she said this.

Audrey was not surprised to hear Emily say that Albert hit Eliza.

She knew Albert too well. Even if it were his biological daughter, he would still abandon her mercilessly after she lost her value.

This was also why she didn't dare to relax for the past few years.

She was afraid Albert would scheme against Fiona until Fiona had nothing left if she was not careful.

Unfortunately, Fiona did not understand her painstaking efforts. She thought Audrey was deliberately going against Albert and felt that Audrey was insensible.

However, after Fiona knew that Eliza and Dominic had an affair but deliberately took sides with Eliza, Audrey's weak affection for Fiona was gone.

She would no longer have any hopes for the Rhodes family again.
