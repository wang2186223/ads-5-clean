chapter87

"This gown looks very expensive. You said before that you didn't take a single cent from the Rhodes family. Then where did you get the money to buy such an expensive gown?" Eliza asked.

Two days ago, Eliza and Fiona went to Koivara's headquarters together when they were customizing the gown.

She had never seen this style in Koivara's ready-made gown.

'Could Audrey be wearing a fake?' Eliza wondered.

Everyone's attention shifted from the Rhodes family's scandal to Audrey's gown.

"Now that she mentioned it, I've never seen this dress in Koivara. Jacqueline, have you seen it before?" someone asked.

Jacqueline's family background was better than everyone else's, and she was the only daughter in the family. She was popular. It was said that every year before Koivara's new products were released, they would send the clothes to her to choose first. Jacqueline crossed her arms arrogantly. Her eyes were filled with disdain as she deliberately said, "I've never seen it before. Perhaps my family isn't rich enough to come into contact with gowns of this level."

Everyone burst into laughter.

Sophie, standing at the side, knew how to fawn on others. She smiled and added, "Jacqueline, if your family isn't rich enough, we'll probably be poor people."

"Does this mean that Jacqueline has never seen this dress before? Is this Koivara's new product? It can't be a fake, right?"

"I don't think so. No matter what, she has seen the world in the Rhodes family for over 20 years. She won't wear fake clothes even if she's wearing a cheaper gown. This is too low..."

"You don't know how poor her original family is. It's said that her father was an Uber driver. How can such a family support the vanity of living like a prince for more than 20 years?"

"She's from a small family, yet she's wearing fake clothes at such a high-end event. She's so unpresentable..."

Seeing Audrey being publicly humiliated, Eliza was extremely pleased.

Fiona's expression changed. She felt a little embarrassed. She glared at Audrey unhappily and said, "Audrey, when did you become so vain?"

Audrey looked at her coldly and did not respond.

Fiona sighed. With an earnest tone, she said, "If you don't have the money to custom-make a gown, you can tell me. I still have a lot of Koivara dresses in my wardrobe. I've only worn them once or twice, and I can let you wear them. You don't have to wear fake clothes and let others suspect that the Rhodes family treats you harshly."

Fiona thought, 'I had thought of giving Audrey some money.

'However, Audrey was so stubborn that she refused to accept it no matter what. She was determined to prove herself.

'But now, she's wearing fake clothes to such an event and embarrassing us.'

Originally, Audrey did not care about what others said.

In any case, she had been the center of attention since she was young.

These people looked down on her, and she could not be bothered to be associated with them.

However, Fiona was different. She was someone Audrey had once sincerely protected and loved.

Audrey used to believe that sincerity could be exchanged for sincerity, but what she got from Fiona was disappointment again and again.

She had really... had enough.

Their last bit of kinship was gone.

Audrey sneered and reached out to Fiona. She said bluntly, "Mrs. Rhodes. If I remember correctly, I bought every single dress in your wardrobe. If you're so generous, why don't you return them to me in cash now?" Fiona was speechless.

She did not expect Audrey to say such a thing. For a moment, her expression alternated between awkward and angry, which was interesting.

Eliza took a step forward and said firmly, "If you want to settle the score like this, my parents must have spent more money on you all these years. When are you going to pay them back?"

Facing her aggressive attitude, Audrey only chuckled.

She said, "Why don't you let them calculate the profits I've brought to the corporation in the past two years and their investment in me? Do they dare to calculate?"

Even Albert's expression changed slightly when he heard that.

Audrey was indeed the hero who saved Rhodes Enterprises. This debt could not be settled...

Eliza gritted her teeth. She was unwilling to let Audrey suppress her again. Just as she was about to speak, she was interrupted by Fiona.

"Audrey, at the end of the day, it's wrong for you to wear fake clothes today. I've taught you to be honest since you were young. How can you fake it for the sake of your pride?"

Audrey raised her eyebrows and sneered. "You don't have to worry about whether my dress is real or fake. You better keep your daughter in check first."

Fiona shook her head in disappointment. She wanted to continue educating Audrey when someone suddenly said, "I don't think this dress is fake. What kind of fake looks better than a haute couture?" As soon as these words were spoken, this small area instantly fell into a strange silence.

A moment later, someone muttered softly, "Maybe the imitation is too good. What's the saying? It's a high-grade good among fake."

"In any case, it can't be a dress better than a haute couture dress. The only dress of Koivara that is better than a haute couture dress can only be their unique treasure..."

However, everyone knew Audrey could not afford to wear their unique treasure.

"Now that you mention it, it reminds me that this dress does look a little like Koivara's unique treasure."
