They thought they were speaking quietly, but Alistair, who had just finished paying, happened to hear everything. The downside of conducting experiments is that you have to be available all the time.

Audrey raised an eyebrow in surprise. "You're going back to the lab this late?" she asked. She had wanted to go shopping with Violet.

Violet chuckled and raised an eyebrow, saying, "That's right. I'm off now, so I won't interrupt your romantic evening."
