Suddenly, a honk echoed in her ears. Audrey looked toward the sound and saw a familiar car parked by the roadside.

The car window slowly rolled down, revealing Alistair with a strikingly chiseled face.

As their eyes met, Alistair's tense expression visibly relaxed instantly. He lifted an eyebrow and chuckled lightly at Audrey. He said, "Honey, we're going home!"

No sooner had Alistair spoken than the back seat window went down. In the back, Sunny sat in a suit, holding a bouquet of vibrant red roses. He didn't say anything, but his well-behaved appearance was quite charming, Seeing this, Audrey's slight disappointment and melancholy instantly disappeared. Feeling like she'd shrugged off some burdens, she quickly walked to the car.

Audrey asked, "Why are you here? Weren't you supposed to wait for me at home?" She hadn't noticed how bright and gentle her smile was then
