Everyone had been still lost in the lingering notes of the song, so they wondered why the music had stopped suddenly, and they all looked towards the stage in confusion.

On the stage that looked like an underwater paradise, Audrey had left the piano, who was now standing at the center.

Her dress fluttered in the mist, and a beam of light shone from behind her, making her look like a spirit who was about to ascend with the light.

The only light on the dark stage illuminated hope for everyone. Audrey lifted her arms and started to dance gracefully.

The melodious singing once again filled the entire stage, "Suddenly, a light shone down. In the endlessly deep sea, someone was singing a lullaby. The voice was gentle, holding the sea spirit closely..."

As everyone was captivated by Audrey's dance, the melody reached its peak. Audrey did a split leap in the air, showing a brilliant smile, and her voice filled with newfound determination and calm.

She continued singing, "In the warmth of the song, the sea spirit woke up. At this moment, the cold mud turned into cotton. The rough sand became ash.

"The sea spirit heard someone calling him back home. He went through the darkness, enjoying the salty sea breeze. He stood on the radiant clouds."

As the lyrics ended, the whole stage lit up. Audrey's green chiffon dress shimmered with a soft blue glow under the lights, as her transparent white wings fully opened, breaking through the darkness toward the light.

The entire stage went quiet for a moment. It took a full two minutes for the audience to come back to their senses. Everyone then stood up. With tears in their eyes, all of the audiences looked at Audrey on stage, crying and clapping wildly.

Meanwhile, the audience in the livestream was going wild too.

[I really regret not getting tickets. That's so sad. I can't even imagine how amazing it must be to hear this song live!]

[You're amazing, honey. I'm going to listen to this song forever.]

[Honey, you always bring us huge surprises and I'm so lucky to be your fan!]

[I apologize for criticizing Audie before. From now on, I'm your die-hard fan.]

[Audie, you deserve it, you've got to win this time!]

[I'm here. It was truly amazing and breathtaking. You can't understand the feeling of tears just from listening, I can't even describe it. Oh, my! I'm going crazy!]

Because of internet delay, the live audience's comments appeared in the livestream much later. However, without exception, they were all praising Audrey. Both fans and newcomers were deeply captivated by the song.

Yann was also very excited. He felt ashamed for previously thinking Audrey had run out of talent. After hearing the song, Yann immediately gave the show the competition's only perfect score during the scoring round.

Not only that, but he also gave a heartfelt comment, "This is the best music I've heard all year, Audie. You're amazing! I hope we can work together sometime in the future!"

After this show, Yann finally acknowledged Audrey's talent. He thought that a genius like her would be wasted if she joined his music studio. That was why he didn't even mention it.

On stage, Audrey bowed to Yann and said, "Thank you for your recognition, Mr. Green."

Since she had just finished a performance, her breathing was still heavy and her voice was slightly trembling. After Yann finished his remarks, the other two judges also gave her high scores as expected.

Seeing the result, Raeyon in the waiting area suddenly felt furious, her face turning pale.

She clenched her fists tightly, her body shaking uncontrollably because of jealousy. She was clear that she was bound to lose.

Raeyon thought, 'Audrey, this

cunning girl, always knew how to pull off these tricks. She obtained JA's help earlier, and now even Yann is impressed by her. Others would definitely look up to her.'

Thinking about it, Raeyon realized she had no chance of winning. She had planned to go for another provocative dance routine to grab

attention like she did before, and she

had even picked a more daring

dress.

But now that Audrey's soul-stirring song was out, taking a vulgar route would only attract criticism and

scorn.
