chapter66

Brightview Group was one of the most powerful companies in Riverdale. If someone was invited to their party, it meant that their business was on the right track and that they were qualified to work with Brightview Group. Because of this, the anniversary party of Brightview Group every year attracted countless businessmen. Wealthy merchants were even proud of being invited.

Now, Alistair openly wanted to kick the Rhodes family out. To the Rhodes family, this was not as simple as not being able to attend a party.

This meant that the Rhodes family could never work with powerful companies.

Logan thought, 'Why is he so ruthless to them?'

Since he had just woken up, Logan was still a little slow to react. He asked in a daze, "What did the Rhodes family do?"

"Just tell me yes or no." Alistair did not give him a chance to finish asking.

"Sure, of course!" Logan would surely choose Alistair over the Rhodes family.

Logan said without hesitation, "As long as you can come, I guarantee that no one from the Rhodes family can come to the anniversary party!" "Okay, bye."

Logan raised his eyebrows and threw himself back into the soft blanket.

He just realized what was going on.

Alistair had just arrived at Riverdale. The Rhodes family did not even know who he was. It was impossible for them to provoke him.

From the looks of it, he was doing it for Audrey.

He thought, 'Since they offended Mr. Hayes... No, to be precise, since they have offended Mr. Hayes's woman, the Rhodes family will not have a good time in the upper-class circle in Riverdale in the future. Logan did not have the slightest bit of sympathy in his heart. In fact, he even wanted to add insult to injury.

The next day.

As soon as Audrey entered the elevator, she heard a colleague say in a low voice, "Hey, have you heard? Brightview Group has internal news that our company has been kicked out of the invitation list of their anniversary party!"

As soon as these words were spoken, the colleagues who were originally lazy instantly jerked up.

A person asked, "How is that possible? Where did you hear this rumor from?"

Some people felt that it was unlikely.

The two companies still had business dealings, so the Brightview Group would not do such a thing.

A boy in his twenties patted his chest and said, "When has my news ever been fake?

"This news came from my friend who worked in Brightview Group's president's office.

"I heard that it was the idea of executives from their company. The invitation was about to be sent out, but it was withdrawn at the last minute."

For a moment, the people in the elevator looked shocked.

A person asked, "If this is true, will there be any major changes in our company?"

Another person said, "No matter how big the change is, it can't compare to Ms. Rhodes's resignation. The company has been relying on Ms. Rhodes heavily for the past two years. If she leaves, our company probably won't be able to last long!" Someone else asked, "Did Brightview Group kick us out this time because they heard that Ms. Rhodes was going to leave?"

Someone took the opportunity to sigh, "Regardless of whether it's true or not, let's make plans of what we should do next."

If Brightview Group really kicked Rhodes Enterprises out because Audrey left, they would have to take sides in this conflict. If they continued to stay in Rhodes Enterprises, they would probably not have a good future. Everyone was worried.

Audrey, who was standing at the far end of the elevator, listened and felt that something was wrong.

She thought, 'Why would Brightview Group kick Rhodes Enterprises out for no reason?

'The two families are still working on a project together!'

She couldn't figure it out. After everyone got out of the elevator, Audrey called Jacob.

After understanding Audrey's question, Jacob said politely, "Ms. Rhodes, the ones who were kicked out of the invitation list are Albert and Eliza. Your invitation has been sent to your assistant." Hearing this, Audrey was so shocked that she forgot to react.

She thought, 'What does that mean? Did the Brightview Group do this to back me up?'

Audrey asked, "Is it convenient to ask why?"

Jacob answered, "I don't know the exact reason. I only know that this was an order personally issued by Mr. Barrett last night." Audrey was even more confused.

She thought, 'Logan's order? I have never interacted with this person before, so why did he help me? Could it be because of Alistair?'

After recovering from her shock, Audrey quickly came back to her senses.

It was rumored that Logan was a typical playboy, however, this person was a business genius.

Since taking over Brightview Group, he had carried out a series of drastic reforms.

He had even become more famous than the Hayes family.

She was not related to Logan, but Alistair was Logan's friend when they were in school.

Previously, Logan had lent Alistair the VVIP card of a private restaurant. It seemed that they had a good relationship.

Audrey thought, 'Did Brightview Group kick Albert and Eliza out because Alistair asked them to?

'However, this concerns the future cooperation between the two companies. The initial investment has already reached tens of millions of dollars.

'How can Logan agree to a request from an ordinary friend so easily?'

She recalled Jacob's sudden change of attitude when he signed the last contract.

Audrey felt that she could not understand Alistair at all.
