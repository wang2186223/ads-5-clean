A few minutes later, Audrey sat in the passenger seat, watching the scenery fly by the window. The dim streetlights cast her reflection on the glass. Her expression was dark, her brows furrowed in anger, giving her the look of a fierce demon.

Alistair was driving and glanced back at her, comforting, "Don't worry. With my parents there, your mom will be fine."

After receiving the call, Jessica had become so emotional that she fainted on the spot.

Fortunately, Francis and Evelyn were there to help get Jessica to the hospital, while they headed to the school to deal with Miles's situation.

Seeing the worry on Audrey's face, Alistair reassured her again, "I've already got someone investigating your brother's situation. Don't worry. I'll make sure the school gives him a fair resolution."

Hearing this, Audrey's face finally softened a little.

Just then, Michael sent over the surveillance footage from the school.

Audrey had never believed that Miles would start a fight without reason, so when she received the footage, she immediately opened it to watch.

A few moments later, she punched the car door in anger, her face darkened with fury. "These bastards, they've gone too far!"

She never imagined that her brother would suffer such brutal bullying at school. They had forced him to drink water from a toilet bowl. 'That's just too much!' she thought.

Audrey could no longer hold back the boiling anger in her chest. She wanted to rush to the school right away and teach those arrogant little thugs a lesson.

'They're just a bunch of students, yet they act so ruthlessly. They're nothing but pieces of shit!' Audrey thought.

Alistair stopped the car on the side of the road, unbuckled his seatbelt, and gently pulled her into his arms.

Alistair gently rubbed her back, his voice soft and calm. "It's okay, don't be mad. I'll stand up for him. I won't let them get away with the bullying."

Nestled in his warm embrace, Audrey listened to his soothing, magical voice. Slowly, her emotions settled.

She was touched by Alistair's thoughtfulness, but she understood Miles even more. The young boy was sensitive and proud and certainly didn't want to meet Alistair for the first time under such circumstances.

Thinking of this, Audrey took Alistair's hand and said softly, "After you drop me off at the school, wait outside. It's better if I handle this myself."

Alistair looked uneasy. "But..."

Audrey cut him off firmly, "Trust me, I'll take care of it."

Seeing her insistence, Alistair reluctantly agreed. 'After all, if she can't solve it, I can still be there to back her up,' he thought.
