Dominic's heart felt empty and he suddenly panicked.

He wanted to push Eliza away, but Albert and Fiona walked over.

The two of them still treated Dominic as their son-in-law.

However, the daughter this time was Eliza.

Albert said, "Dominic, you're here. Coincidentally, I have something to talk to you about at work."

Before Dominic could speak, Fiona said, "Honey, don't mention work when you're out to play. Let the couple have a good chat. Dominic has been busy with work recently and didn't accompany Eliza much." Fiona's tone was somewhat reproachful.

She was dissatisfied with Dominic's cold attitude toward Eliza these days, but she could not embarrass him in front of others, so she found an excuse for him, saying he was busy with work.

The two families still had business dealings. Dominic had no choice but to consider their reputation and could only feign civility with them for the time being.

He said, "It's my fault for that. After I'm done with this, I'll accompany Eliza more."

Eliza enjoyed it very much. She smiled obediently and said considerately, "It's okay. I know you're also doing this to let me and the baby live a good life." "Yeah." Dominic nodded perfunctorily. He kept looking at Audrey from the corner of his eye, but she never looked back.

Eliza was so happy that she did not notice that Dominic was distracted.

She held his arm and walked around, wishing to engrave the words "fiancee" on her forehead.

Everyone in Riverdale knew that Dominic and Audrey had almost gotten married.

They had just broken up when Eliza became his fiancee.

Even a fool would know that this matter was not simple.

Although everyone despised it, they did not dare to show it.

They even had to praise Dominic and Eliza for being a perfect match.

After all, the Hayes family was one of the top families in Riverdale and inextricably linked to the Hayes family in Willowdale.

They could not afford to offend them easily.

Listening to the praises of the people around her, Eliza was overjoyed and deliberately looked at Audrey provocatively in the VIP seats. Seeing Eliza's smug expression, Audrey could not help but want to laugh.

'How stupid of her to want the whole world to know that she was a mistress,' Audrey thought.

Although Audrey did not care about the lovey-dovey couple, she felt disgusted to see them loitering around the banquet hall.

After greeting the big shots in the business world around her, she got up and went to the washroom to refresh herself.

Seeing her leave, Eliza thought that she had finally won a round. She felt much better.

The surrounding flattery and praises made Albert feel a little smug.

He said, "Dominic, I heard Mr. Alistair Hayes will come today. Can you introduce me to him later?"

He looked at his future son-in-law expectantly, his eyes filled with longing and ambition.

However, Dominic did not hear it clearly at all.

After Audrey left, his heart also flew away.

Eliza shook his arm and reminded him coquettishly, "Dominic, my father is talking to you."

Dominic came back to his senses and asked perfunctorily, "Huh? What did you say, Mr. Rhodes? Sorry, it's a little noisy here."

Albert did not mind Dominic's indifference since he wanted to ask Dominic for a favor. He continued asking warmly, "I said, can you introduce me to Mr. Alistair Hayes today?"

Originally, he had promised to bring Felix to the anniversary celebration party today to broaden his horizons.

However, he immediately regretted it when he heard that Alistair was coming. He only brought Eliza and Fiona in the end.

Although Felix and Zara were angry, Albert felt everything was worth it as long as he could use Dominic to establish a relationship with the Hayes family in Willowdale.

Seeing that Dominic did not respond for a long time, a hint of anxiety flashed across Albert's eyes. He said, "You must be very familiar with Mr. Alistair Hayes. Please help to introduce me to him. It's a piece of cake for you." Dominic frowned slightly. 'Even a fool like Albert wants to know Alistair? Why didn't he think about his abilities?'

However, he couldn't say that.

He lowered his eyes without batting an eyelid and said in a businesslike manner, "Today is the Brightview Group's anniversary celebration. The Barrett family must have spent much effort to invite him over. He might not have time for me." Albert was determined to get to know Alistair. Naturally, these words could not make him give up.

He looked at Dominic eagerly and said, "In terms of seniority, you can be considered Mr. Alistair Hayes's nephew.

"He made a special trip here. As a junior, it's reasonable for you to go and greet him. You can bring me along at that time."

Dominic was speechless.
