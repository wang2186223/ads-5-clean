Seeing that Eliza was distracted, Fiona secretly reminded her, "Eliza, this is a good opportunity to leave an impression on Mr. Hayes. Don't miss it." Eliza exclaimed softly and frowned. "Mom, what are you talking about? I already have Dominic."

Fiona glared at her and said softly, "Silly girl, I'm not asking you to seduce Mr. Hayes. He has high standards and might not like people from a small place like us.

"You just have to show your face and make him remember you. It'll be convenient for you to ask someone to do something in the future."

'If we can hook up with Mr. Hayes, our family's meteoric rise will be just around the corner,' Fiona thought.

Even a tiny part of the business held by these top wealthy families was enough for them to live on for the rest of their lives.

"Mom, you're thinking too simply. Why would he remember me?" Eliza sounded a little unhappy.

She felt that Fiona treated her as a tool to cozy up to the rich and powerful.

She said, "More than half of the people in the banquet hall are here for Mr. Hayes. They all want him to remember them. I don't have the ability..." Fiona was still insisting. "Don't belittle yourself. You're Dominic's fiancee after all. We're related. Mr. Hayes will more or less show you some respect." Eliza replied, "Alright, I understand."

Even though she was unhappy, she could only agree perfunctorily.

After all, she did not dare to resist Fiona's arrangements.

Fiona probably also felt Eliza was a little unhappy, so she frowned slightly.

'If this is Audrey... She is so smart that she can do things beautifully without anyone's reminders.' Thinking of this, Fiona could not help but sigh. Eliza sensed her emotions and felt uneasy.

She wondered, 'Where did Dominic go? Why isn't he back yet?'

Just as Eliza was frustrated, she finally saw someone walking out of the washroom.

However, it was not Dominic, whom Eliza had been looking forward to for a long time. Instead, it was Audrey and her "gigolo".

Since Audrey brought Alistair into the venue, many people in the banquet hall had noticed the couple.

It couldn't be helped. The couple was so good-looking. It was hard not to look at them.

"Damn, look, who's that man beside Audrey? He's so handsome."

"He's indeed handsome. The two of them are even holding hands... Could he be Audrey's new lover?"

"I'm so jealous. Why is Audrey so lucky? Her ex-boyfriend, Dominic, is already handsome enough. I didn't expect her new lover to be even more gorgeous."

"Oh my god, the two of them are simply compatible. Honestly, I used to think that Dominic was not worthy of Audrey. They gave me the feeling of a princess and a knight whenever he stood with her. However, these two are a perfect match. It's as if they were born to be together."

"You're exaggerating, girl..."

"I announce that I'll start being a fan of this couple. They're so compatible."

Eliza frowned, got up, and quickly walked toward the two people in the crowd.

She was aggressive. As soon as she went up, she denounced Audrey. "Where's Dominic? Where did you seduce him?"

Audrey looked at Eliza as if she was looking at a fool.

She had seen brainless people but had never seen such a brainless one.

'Is a piece of trash like Dominic worthy of my seduction?' Audrey thought.

Seeing that she did not speak, Eliza became even angrier. She said aggressively, "Say something. Audrey. Did Dominic go to look for you? You seduced him again, right? Why do you have to go against me all the time?"

She said quickly, one sentence after another, not giving Audrey a chance to speak. She even pointed at Alistair and said indignantly, "You already have this gigolo by your side, yet you still want to be entangled with my fiance. Don't you think it's very cheap?" After Eliza said these words, Audrey felt speechless, while many people watching the commotion wanted to laugh.

They thought Eliza must have gone crazy seeing that Audrey had found an even better man.

Although Dominic was good-looking and had a decent family background, it was nothing compared to Audrey's new lover.

Alistair had tolerated Eliza for a long time. At this moment, his eyes were filled with coldness. He wanted to speak, but Audrey stopped him.

'It's just Eliza. I don't need him to help me,' Audrey thought.

Previously, she did not want to argue with Eliza, but even the most tolerant person had a temper.

'Eliza kept finding fault with me. Does she think I'm easy to bully?' Audrey thought.

Audrey's expression turned cold. She sneered mockingly and said coldly, "It's your business if you like to be with the man I don't want anymore. Don't think that everyone is like you."

Eliza's expression froze. She was about to retort.

However, Audrey did not give her a chance to speak. Audrey quickly added, "If you can't keep your man, that's your problem. I'm a clean freak. I'll never come back to take a second look at the trash I throw away. Moreover, my husband is ten thousand times better than Dominic. Do you think I'll still like him?"

Eliza was rendered speechless.

The surrounding people had been watching the commotion. One of them spoke at that moment.

She said, "Audrey's new lover is more handsome than Dominic. If it were me, I would choose this new lover too."

Eliza was instantly angered. She shouted angrily, "What's the use of being handsome? He's just a gigolo who lives off a woman. He has no ability and no money. He could enter the banquet hall because you used your company's authority to help him get the invitation, right?

"Such a spineless man does not even deserve to carry shoes for Dominic."
