Audrey shook her head and pushed the box into the house.

A few seconds later, she took out the two invitations and handed one to Alistair.

"Tomorrow is the Brightview Group's anniversary. Can I have the honor of asking you to be my male companion, Mr. Hayes?" Audrey asked.

Her eyes were smiling, and her soft voice carried a hint of playfulness, making it difficult for Alistair to refuse.

Alistair's heart skipped a beat, but he could only say helplessly, "My company has an important signing ceremony tomorrow morning. I need to preside over it. I might not be able to accompany you." "So, you don't have time for an entire day?" Audrey sounded a little regretful.

The anniversary would last all day and she wanted to introduce Alistair to her friends.

Alistair thought about it and said, "I can rush over after 10:30."

Audrey expressed she did not mind and said, "That's alright. It's good that you can go. I'll leave this invitation letter for you."

Alistair accepted the invitation and said, "I made an appointment with stylists. They will come to our house tomorrow to do your styling."

He didn't make it in time to give her the gown. He knew she would need the stylist.

"Okay." As expected, Audrey did not refuse.

She returned to her room, took the gown out of the box, and hung it up. Then she looked at the time on the wall.

It was 9:28 P.M.

It was almost time for her beauty sleep.

Audrey turned around and glanced at the large bed behind her.

Last night, she and Alistair almost had an "accident" in this bed, and then he slept there.

'Tonight...'

Those passionate images appeared in her mind. Audrey's entire body heated up, and even her earlobes turned red.

However, she gradually accepted that she was married and quickly calmed down. After leaving the room, she even took the initiative to ask Alistair. "Do you want to take a shower first?" She thought that she had concealed her nervousness very well, but the truth was that her ears were so red that they seemed to be dripping blood. Her tone was also a little trembling. Alistair took in all these details. Although he was surprised by her change in attitude, he did not want to push her too hard.

There was still a long way to go. Alistair did not want to leave her with any bad experiences.

So he said, "You can go first. Have a good night's rest. I still have work to do. I might stay up late."

It meant that nothing would happen tonight...

Although Audrey claimed to be prepared, she still heaved a sigh of relief when she heard this. "Alright, don't stay up too late. Your health is more important." Alistair agreed with a smile.

The next day.

Coincidentally, the Brightview Group's anniversary celebration was on Saturday. Audrey did not go to work and slept until ten o'clock.

When she woke up, Alistair had already gone to the office.

The stylist Alistair had arranged to meet arrived after she woke up.

At the same time.

Brightview Group's anniversary celebration, held at Moonlight Villa, had already welcomed the first batch of guests of the day.

The Barrett family owned Moonlight Villa.

The grand door had flowers carved on it. Entering the villa, all they could see was the lavish lobby. The complex and ornate lighting glowed in soft, warm colors.

They laid a soft carpet on the floor. On the four tall walls hung a few famous vintage paintings. The interior decorations were gorgeous and expensive, showing the owner's extravagance.

Starting at 10 AM, luxury cars worth millions of dollars stopped in front of the villa one after another.

The people coming in and out were all famous people from Riverdale.

Many media outlets came when they heard Alistair from Willowdale was also coming.

They racked their brains to get in and see Alistair's face. If they could get an exclusive interview, it would not be a dream for them to climb up the ranks.

Albert came early with Eliza and Fiona.

Seeing that someone was chased away because they did not have an invitation, Albert was very proud. He was not stingy with his love and praise for Eliza. "It's so difficult to get an invitation, but my daughter can get three. She's so capable." Eliza smiled awkwardly. She felt so guilty that her palms were sweating.

After all, the invitation letter in her hand was bought with money. She was worried that she would expose herself.

When she got closer, she saw that the invitation letter someone else had brought looked similar to the one she had bought.

Eliza secretly heaved a sigh of relief...
