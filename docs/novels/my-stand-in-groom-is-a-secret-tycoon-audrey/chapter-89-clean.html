chapter89

Eliza felt her legs go weak, and her expression was gloomy. 'How did that happen?'

She dared to say with certainty that Audrey's invitation was fake because she had secretly destroyed the real one.

She did not expect Audrey to obtain an invitation within a day. Moreover, it was an even rarer VIP invitation.

Meeting the mocking gazes of others and thinking about the fake invitation letter she had spent one million dollars to buy, Eliza gritted her teeth. The expressions of the surrounding people changed as well.

Someone said, "I heard this year's anniversary celebration produced ten VIP invitations."

These ten invitations naturally belonged to the real big shots of Riverdale.

"The CEO of Salman Technology is only holding ordinary invitations. But Audrey, the fake heiress, got a VIP invitation..."

Jacqueline's expression didn't look good either.

The CEO of Salman Technology was her father.

However, she reacted quickly and immediately praised Audrey. "Sure enough. Audrey has been better than us in everything since we were young."

Audrey did not even want to bother with such a snobbish person. She did not even look at Jacqueline.

Jacqueline smiled awkwardly. She didn't have the cheek to stay any longer. So she lifted her skirt and walked away.

As soon as she left, everyone's attention fell on Eliza.

Someone couldn't help but mock, "It's fine if you're stupid, but you even misled others. You almost implicated the intern. You're stupid and bad." Everyone agreed with these words, and many people laughed.

Eliza bit her lips in embarrassment. She lowered her head, wanting to reduce her presence.

Albert and Fiona also felt embarrassed. They pulled Eliza and were about to walk in.

At this moment, someone else exclaimed as she held her phone. "Oh my god, check your Twitter now. Koivara claimed Audrey's dress. It's their shop's unique treasure. Moreover, there is a shocking piece of news." Everyone was shocked. 'Koivara posted and claimed Audrey's new dress? And there was even a shocking piece of news?'

The people watching the commotion for a long time at the anniversary celebration became even more excited when they heard this. They took out their phones and logged into Twitter.

They were dumbfounded when they saw the latest post on the Koivara Group's certification account.

"Oh my god, am I seeing things? Does this post mean that the person wearing their shop's treasured gown is the chief designer of their brand?"

Everyone found it unbelievable.

They put down their phones and looked at Audrey and Eliza.

Only the two wore Koivara gowns. Eliza wore a limited edition haute couture gown. The style was not novel. Some celebrities had worn it before, and Eliza was not the first to wear it.

On the other hand, the dress on Audrey, which Eliza described as a high-quality imitation A-grade replica, was the same as the treasure of Koivara.

So, Audrey was not wearing an A-grade replica but the real treasure of Koivara, and she was Koivara's chief designer.

This realization made everyone present boil with excitement.

"So, in the past two years, the few luxury bags that were popular globally were all designed by Audrey?"

She was so beautiful and talented. They all felt she was like the female lead of an idol drama.

For a moment, everyone looked at Audrey with envy and admiration.

"I finally understand why they couldn't wait to attack her. She's so outstanding. That's why they hate her."

Upon hearing this, the person who had attacked Audrey to please Eliza instantly regretted it.

Koivara's international status was unquestionable. Some domestic A-list celebrities had to line up to wear Koivara's clothes to the red carpet.

This brand was not only about whether a person had money or not.

Only those with a high enough social status and influence could be favored by them.

This was also the reason why everyone was praising Eliza just now.

However, compared to Audrey, the chief designer of Koivara, Eliza, who could wear its dress, was not worth mentioning.

In comparison, no matter which aspect, Audrey could easily surpass Eliza.

All of a sudden, the socialites around Eliza distanced themselves from her.

Some people even looked at Eliza in disgust. They blamed Eliza for causing them to offend Audrey.

Some people who knew what was good for them immediately came to apologize to Audrey.

"Ms. Rhodes, I'm sorry for what happened just now. Someone with an evil heart used me. So I misunderstood you. I'm sorry." Apologies sounded one after another.

Even Sophie put away her pride and apologized. She was the one who was most displeased with Audrey.

She knew very well that Audrey, who had left the Rhodes family, might be someone she could step on.

However, as the chief designer of Koivara, Audrey was not someone she could offend.

She could not bear the consequences of angering Koivara.

After all, it was not only for their reputation but also a symbol of their status and wealth to wear Koivara outfits.

If she got blacklisted by Koivara, she could forget about buying any luxury brand clothes in the future.

"Audrey, you have a good figure. This dress seems to be tailor-made for you. It's so beautiful on you."

"It's also because Audrey has a good temperament. If it were anyone else, they wouldn't be able to display such an effect."

"Koivara has good taste. Such a high-end and exquisite dress should be on someone like Audrey."

Listening to their apologies and compliments, Audrey did not feel very happy.

All she felt was hypocrisy.

Although these people were not as detestable as the culprit, Eliza, they were still disgusting.

She did not want to face these people. So she turned around and asked Lance, "Excuse me, can I go in now?"

Lance said respectfully, "Of course. Your seat is in the front row. I'll bring you in."

After that, he turned his body slightly and bowed to invite Audrey into the venue.

When everyone saw this scene, they realized that Audrey was not only the chief designer of Koivara but also a guest of Brightview Group.

They recalled how they had accidentally offended Audrey because of Eliza's misleading and intentional provocation.

For a moment, everyone looked at Eliza with resentment.

"Those who grew up in the poor village are short-sighted."

"I can't believe the Rhodes family treats such a person as a treasure. They're blind. Let's go. Don't interact with such a person in the future. It's embarrassing." These words were like a slap to Eliza's face.

Her cheeks burned, and she bit her lip, unable to speak.
