Albert didn't forget that Eliza embarrassed him because she knew nothing when she signed the contract.

He was a little hesitant.

Audrey also had an invitation to the Brightview Group's anniversary.

He could fire Audrey silently. But if Audrey spouted nonsense at the party...

It wouldn't be good if someone spread the rumor that when he got what he wanted, he killed those who had helped him.

Thinking of this, Albert said cautiously, "The anniversary is coming. Why don't we wait until this is over before firing Audrey?"

Eliza was instantly unhappy.

She looked up at Albert with tears in her eyes. She choked and said, "But when I think about seeing the culprit who hurt me in the company, I feel terrible."

Albert sighed and said, "Just bear with it for now."

Eliza felt even more aggrieved. She said softly, "Dad, I know you're afraid that after Audrey is fired, she will badmouth us to others in Brightview Group's anniversary celebration." Since she had guessed correctly, Albert did not deny it.

If they could not attend the party in the end, Audrey could say whatever she wanted at the party.

This was too disadvantageous for them.

Albert was worried they would make a fool of themselves if things got ugly.

Eliza raised her eyebrows slightly and said confidently, "Dad, Dominic has already agreed to get us an invitation to the anniversary celebration.

"At that time, as long as we think of a way to make Audrey unable to attend the anniversary celebration, won't your worries be gone?"

When Eliza was sad, Fiona was also worried.

She did not understand the company's operations and thought things were that simple. She immediately told Albert, "Let's do as Eliza says and fire Audrey first. This time, she went too far. She won't admit her mistake if we don't teach her a lesson." When he heard Eliza promise she could get the invitation, Albert smiled happily.

'This new daughter of mine is quite useful,' he thought.

He slapped his thigh happily and said, "Alright, alright. We can fire her as long as we get the invitations."

It was to his liking because he had long disliked Audrey.

He did not care for a daughter who had never been close to him since she was young and had been stepping on his head since she came to the company.

Her plan succeeded. Excitement flashed in Eliza's eyes.

Eliza couldn't wait to see Audrey's embarrassment when she was chased out of the company...

At two in the afternoon.

Audrey finally drove back to the company from the hospital.

However, when the car parked in the garage and she was about to enter the company with her bag, she realized that the device at the door had failed to recognize her face. Audrey didn't bring her employee card when she left at noon because their employees could use facial recognition to get in and out.

Now that it failed to recognize her face, Audrey could only ask the receptionist who was returning to the company with her coincidentally. "Please help me see what's going on. Is there a problem with the instrument?" Audrey asked.

The receptionist pursed her lips nervously. It was as if she was in a difficult position. She even looked at Audrey with some sympathy. Audrey raised her eyebrows. "What's wrong?"

The receptionist said softly, "Ms. Rhodes, there's nothing wrong with the equipment. It's..."

"What is it? Don't hesitate when you speak."

The receptionist gritted her teeth and confessed, "Mr. Rhodes said you got fired from the company, so he has frozen your access rights." 'Huh...' Audrey was so angry that she laughed.

'Is there something wrong with Audrey's brain? How dare he fire me at this juncture?' Audrey thought.

"Ms. Rhodes, please go in quickly." The receptionist called Audrey and opened the door for Audrey with her access card.

Audrey looked at her in shock.

The receptionist pursed her lips and smiled shyly at her. She said softly, "Back then, it was because of you that I could keep this job. This is all I can do for you now." Although she might be fired if she did so, at least she had lived up to her conscience.

Audrey was stunned.

She didn't remember helping this young receptionist.

The receptionist did not explain and only urged, "Ms. Rhodes. Ms. Harper is throwing your things in your office now. You should go and take a look."

"Thank you." There was no time to think. Audrey nodded at the receptionist and walked straight to the office.

'Throwing my things, huh?' Audrey thought furiously.

She wanted to see how capable Eliza was.
