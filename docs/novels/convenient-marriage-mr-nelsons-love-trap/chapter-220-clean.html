Jayden looked at her, "You yourself are heartbroken. Why do you still want to comfort me?"

Victoria forced her smile, "Do you think it was fate that brought us together?”

"Huh?”

"We are both so miserable”.

Jayden was speechless.

When she thought about that, she smiled bitterly.

Victoria was shocked for a moment. She did not expect that her expression was noticed by her.

Victoria said nonchalantly, "Nothing special. I just thought of a joke."

"What's the joke about?" Jolene asked casually. She was free and chit-chatted with her.

Jolene laughed, "Your classmate was really funny."

Victoria also laughed. She felt less depressed at the same time.

When Jayden returned, he saw the two girls laughing and joking happily in the living room. He felt like

When they saw him, they stopped laughing. Jolene took the folded clothes up, "I put them into the

cabinet," she said.

Victoria also stood up and said, "Let me help you."

The two persons stood up from the sofa and entered the room while Jayden stayed in the foyer and

Why did they avoid him?
