She was just testing him a while ago?

She also didn’t know. She just felt that she would be happy as his woman. He had the charm of a

Wasn’t to fight for what you love the popular saying right now?

Was it wrong for her to fight for what she loved?

Everyone knew that it wasn’t easy to work in the entertainment industry. But everyone still recklessly

If he wasn’t interested in money, why did he want to become a celebrity?

Sean stood inside the elevator and looked at Tommy who was almost devastated, “I became a celebrity

“I won’t leave. I’ll wait for you,” Tommy firmly said. He had been with Sean for many years already. He

Sean gestured a sign of a good buddy at him. Then, the elevator doors closed.

Tommy turned around and left in disappointment.

The fifteen million was gone just like that.

He turned around and looked at the huge reception hall in the first floor of the company. The marble

He sighed, “You can be impulsive if you have a rich brother. Is fifteen million even money?”

He was still thinking about that fifteen million in pain when he walked out.

That was just one fee for an advertisement.

It was only a few minutes of advertisement.

“Fuck, money is just paper. The paper used to wipe butts.”

Boyce Shawn was also there sitting next to Armand.
