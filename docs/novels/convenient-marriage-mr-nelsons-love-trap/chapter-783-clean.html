Theresa was quite tired. Dizzily, she was quite sleepy. Right then, she heard the message tone of her

tone again. After hesitating for a moment, she reached out to get the phone - it was an instant message

from Oscar.

He sent her a GIF on which a dog was frightened first. Then he asked, “Have you got the gift from me

to you?”

“Some desserts. When we were chatting a few days ago, you said you would do surgery today... I

Theresa looked over at the delicate and pretty desserts on the table, feeling quite warm in her heart.

touched.

She curled up her lips into a smile and replied, “Yes, I have.”

“Do you like them?”

Theresa replied fast, “Yes.”

Then she added, “They are so yummy!”

Oscar replied to her with an emoji to express his happiness.

Theresa laughed while staring at the screen.

Theresa immediately replied, “Are you all right?”

“I’m fine. I bit that dog to death.”

“Haha... Am I fiercer than a dog?”
