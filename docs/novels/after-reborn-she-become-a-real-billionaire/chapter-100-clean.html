Chapter 100

“Hello Renea! Good News! I have good news for you. Guess what happened! Forget it, let me tell you, the actress that I have been eyeing, Amelia, who works for K.W. Media has come to the audition today!” Carson said excitedly.

Compared to before when Carson found the heroine of his heart, he is even more excited today.

“Yeah, congratulations!” Renea said flatly, as if she had already guessed it.

“Since all the actors are currently finalized, we can start the movie when the New Year is over,” Carson said to himself.

Renea listens quietly.

“Well, Renea, I’m done with you. I have to finish up the last bit of business before the year, and then I will go home and have a peaceful New Year’s Eve.”

“Hmm.”

“Happy New Year, Renea!”

“Happy New Year.”

Renea hung up the phone and looked out the window. It was only 29 today, and the fireworks were already thundering outside.

The window view outside is as lively as it’s online right now.

A tweet from Kevin blew up the entertainment world.

[@Hello Renea, Aria Adair]

It was accompanied by the image of a book from the popular fairy novel, Reborn; A Second Chance!

“Crap what the hell? Renea is going to play my Aria Adair!”

“What qualification does Renea, a school student, have to play my Aria?”

“Don’t people say that Kevin has a sharp eye? How could he look at Renea?”

“Ruined! Ruined! My Aria is ruined! I thought that since the copyright of Reborn; A Second Chance, is in the hands of K.W. Media and Kevin, it wouldn’t be ruined! However, it seems like I am too naive to think that!”

“This Renea... She would not have been having an affair with Kevin, would she?”

Does anyone remember, the video of Renea on the internet a while ago in which she has been misunderstood as the Sugar baby? At that time, Kevin had personally tweeted to clarify for Renea. When did Kevin personally send out a tweet to clarify a scandal for someone?”

“This Renea is really powerful. Kevin is famous for not being close to a woman, but he would have been taken down by Renea. This woman has a foxy face that seduces men at a glance!”

“She is a vixen! First, she hooked up with Aron, and now she is hooking up with Kevin. And I heard that she is also interested in getting close to Richard, but she was rejected by him!”

“Do not mention Richard. He is uniquely beautiful and he will never look at someone like her!”

The online public opinion is getting more and more outrageous and most of the people were scolding Renea.

After all, in the eyes of the book fans, if Renea played the Aria, it was like ruining an original plot.

When Renea went to check on her Twitter account, she found that her private message box had already exploded. But she didn’t even click it. After re-tweeting Kevin’s tweet, she went offline.

That’s the reason why Amelia auditioned for it.

Of course, she was not going to make a deal with Kevin because she wanted Amelia.

The script was so intriguing that it made her feel like she was right there in the middle of it and that’s made her decide to take on the show as a way to lay the groundwork for a fortunate-behind-the-scene movie.

Besides Kevin is the best director, and she could learn a lot of things from him.

“Sh*t! Sh*t! Sh*t!” After seeing the Twitter, Richard exploded in foul language.

“Kevin, what do you mean this? You wouldn’t have a crush on my Renea, would you? Let me tell you, she is a lot younger than you and you can’t have any thoughts about her!”

“Your Renea!” Kevin looked at him, his eyes slightly deeper.

Richard looked at him in disgust and said, “Don’t think nasty thoughts. I only treat Renea as my sister, unlike you!”

“Sister!” Kevin bit the sound of these words extremely heavily, and a touch of frost condensed under his eyes.

Richard lowered his head and the look on his face also became ugly.

The word sister was like a taboo in the White family.

A while later...

The cold light on Kevin’s face disappeared, and he said, nonchalantly, “You are the one with the nasty thoughts. I chose her because I think she’s suitable for the role of Aria.”

“Really?” Disbelief was written in two big words on Richard’s face.

“In that case, I think I am quite suitable for the male lead. Why don’t you let me do it?”

“I thought you said you were not going to act in a fairy drama anymore!”

After Richard became famous by playing a role in an immortal drama, he has not worked in any immortal since then.

Because of this drama, Richard had attracted several fans. However, this group of fans had solidified his role. As an actor, he wants to be diversified, so that he can give the audience the feeling that they will not only remember him because of his one role, but he wants that each role can give the audience a different view.

“That was in the past. I’m now able to penetrate the hearts of people in every role I play. There is nothing that I can’t do again,” Richard said confidently and proudly.

Kevin said, “It’s quite surprising for me. However, if you think it’s suitable for you, then you can try it.”

To be honest, Kevin was planning to do just that. It was just that Richard’s rebellious tamper could only go against the grain.

Seeing the official announcement on Twitter, Kailey was so angry that her facial expression had been distorted and her eyes were like a quenched poison.

Susan was also indignant at the side and said, “This little **** Renea. Her luck is too good!”

“Mom, I’m going for an audition,” Kailey said with determination.

Although the main characters have been officially announced, there are still groups of roles that support the cast, which will be still recruited online.

After all, if it had not been for Kevin tweeting about the official announcement that Renea working in this drama, no one would have known that Reborn; A Second Chance had already begun preparation for the start of filming.

It was recommended before, but there was a delayed response, and because of that they all thought that it had not started shooting yet.

“Kailey, you are going to audition for a sporting role. That’s a supporting role for that **** Renea!” Susan thought she had heard wrong.
