Chapter 95

The last sentence of Kailey completely broke down Susan’s defense.

‘Yes, even if Dylan could have his sentence commuted, he could no longer be the hear of Morris family, much less her reliance.’

‘If the Morris family thrown into crisis because of him, not only would she not be able to save Dylan, but she would also jeopardize her own lifetime of glory and prosperity.’

‘Wouldn’t that make Renea goal came true?’

‘Luckily, Kailey reminded me, or else I would have really fallen into the trap of this woman named Renea!’ She thought.

Kailey knew that Susan had already been convinced by her and a sister smile at the corner of his mouth instantly disappeared.

She would not allow anyone to stumbling block on her way to success. Not even Dylan or Susan.

“Let’s hurry back before dad finds out. And from now on, don’t mention my brother in front of Dad. Dad is also doing it for the Morris family.” Kailey said.

Susan compromised and nodded, “Kailey from now on, Mom will only have one daughter and it’s you. You are all now mom has.”

“Don’t worry mom, I won’t let you down.” Kailey said confidently.

“I heard that there is a new development in Dylan’s case,” Stella asked curiously.

Renea answered indifferently, not even taking her eyes off the book.

Stella thought thoughtfully as if she had thought of something, her eyes rolled and she laughed and said, “Renea are not you looking for the jade pendant. Let’s use Dylan’s matter as a bargaining chip to make them take out the jade pendant.”

Hearing that, Renea looked up and smiled at her.

“My uncle is Aaron. They won’t disbelieve him.”

Renea laughed sardonically, “It’s not they don’t believe, it’s just that they won’t...”

“What do you mean?” Stella was puzzled. ‘Could be that there were still parents who don’t want to save their son?’

‘It seemed like there is. Isn’t that what happened to her dad?’ She thought.

“If the jade pendant was solely related to my birth, Leon would not have gone to the trouble of getting a couple to pretend my parents. However, if the jade pendant is involved more than my birth and possibly related to the Morris family, Leon would never take out the jade pendant even if he had to gave up on his son.”

From what had happened so far, Renea wanted to know the secret behind the jade pendant even more. And she would certainly figure it out.

Stella nodded in understanding. After all, human selfishness was unimaginable.

“For Leon, the Morris family is more important than just a son like Dylan. What’s more, there is also a daughter, Kailey. And it’s not that as if there is no one to inherit the family’s property. Beside what Leon’s lack is never an heir, but a tool to utilize.”

“At that moment, even if Dylan can be saved, he is no longer useful. While Kailey is different. She have been cultivating since childhood, and it was more than enough to develop into entertainment industry. As long as Kailey become a popular flower girl, it was nothing for her to inherit the Blue Records Entertainment.”

After Susan’s delays in coming to her, Renea already knows the outcome.

She still overestimated Susan. However, come to think of it, a person who could give up her daughter for the sake of the Morris family, and agree to feed a person from an unknown source, how good she could be?

Stella nodded, approvingly. But then, she worriedly asked again, “What about you, Renea? The jade pendant is so tightly covered by them, how can you get it?”

“It’s fine. Something that is hidden even better will still have its day of appearance,” Renea laughed confidently.

Even if doesn’t show up for the rest of its life, the result will be the same.

She wanted nothing more than but just to destroy the Morris family.

Looking at the murderous aura in Renea’s cold eyes, Stella not only did not feel terrible but felt heartbroken.

If she had not been hurt to the core, how could she had the look that looked in her eyes?

At the end of the last semester, Renea didn’t return to the Morris home. Instead, she rented a small apartment near the campus under the pretext of having found her biological parents.

The apartment had one bedroom, one hall, one bathroom, and one kitchen. Even though it looked small, it was unbelievably good for Renea.

The apartment had everything she needed. Although it was a little old, it had saved her a lot of money from buying it.

“Renea it’s so small. How can you live there? You had better come with me to the AK Mansion.”

Stella looked at the apartment up and down and began to dislike it.

Aron carried the stuff in, but he didn’t say anything.

Even though he also thought the same, he would not force Renea.

“I think it’s quite good. Since, I’ll live alone, it doesn’t need to be too big. What’s more, it’s close to the school. And when the next semester starts, I can stop leaving in the school, in this way you don’t have to condescend to accompany me to live in the school...”

“It’s not aggravating, I am not aggravated at all by living in school...”

Stella swallowed the rest of the words she was going to say after meeting the biting gaze case by Aron...

Renea smiled and went to clean up.

Aron followed her and asked, “Do you need my help?”

“No, thanks. The landlord had already cleaned the apartment, so there is nothing much to do. What’s more, I am used to categorizing things myself, in this way it is easier to find.”

Since Renea had refused, Aron didn’t say anything more. After moving the things from the car to the apartment, he sat on the sofa and used his cell phone to deal with things.

Stella helped Renea with the simple cleanup.

The apartment had everything, so Renea only needed to categorize some daily necessities and books. In just less than an hour, she was done with everything.

Looking at her small apartment, Renea had a satisfied smile on her face.

“Are you hungry? Let’s go out to eat something,” Aron who appeared behind her, said. His voice was as gentle as ever.

Renea turned back and smiled faintly and said, “Aron and Stella, you two guys go ahead. I still have to go to buy some daily necessities and I’ll be back later. I will just casually cook a bowl of noodles for dinner.”

“Then I will keep you accompany,” Stella said.

“No need.”

Aron looked at Stella and said, “Don’t you still have something to do?”

“Huh?” Stella was dumbfounded.

Aron narrowed his eyes slightly and faintly said, “In the morning, big brother did call and ask when are you going back?”
