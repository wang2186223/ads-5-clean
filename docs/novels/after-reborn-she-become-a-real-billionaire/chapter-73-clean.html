Chapter 73

“Where do she come from? Is there anything special about sister’s background?” Kailey asked curiously.

This was also what she always wanted to know. For her, Grandpa didn’t even want his granddaughter.

He even left the inheritance for her.

“I’m afraid she has other secrets. Otherwise, the jade pendant left by the sir Morris would not have disappeared without a trace,” Susan said thoughtfully

“Jade pendant? What kind of pendant?”

“Your grandfather had always loved it and cherished it very much. When they sir Morris was still alive, he had locked it in the safe. After the sir Morris passed away, the jade pendant in the safe disappeared. I thought that the sir Morris had given it to that little **** Renea.”

“But Dylan said that Renea didn’t know about the jade pendant and was still inquiring about his background. The reason why there has not been any progress in Dylan’s case was because Renea had not found the jade pendant and wanted to find it through us. That’s why he didn’t make a move on Dylan.”

Kailey understood a little, “Mom, do you mean that sister is secretly telling us that if we want to save my brother, we have to take out the jade pendant to exchange for him.”

“That should be the case. Renea probably want to look for the jade pendant, but she didn’t find it. I have been the old house, but it was also not there.”

Susan’s words caused Kailey to sink into deep thought.

“The jade pendant must have been kept by your father, but I have searched all over the place, but I have not found it. I don’t know where your father put it.”Susan said with some frustration.

“Mom, why don’t you tell Dad directly and ask him to take out the jade pendant to save brother?”

“No!” Susan immediately stopped her.

“Don’t you know your father’s character? He values the Morris family more than his own life. In his eyes Dylan is no longer his son. If the jade pendant is involved in too many things, not only will the father not take out the jade pendant, but he also hide it better.”

Kailey nodded thoughtfully and held Susan’s hand, “Mom, don’t worry, I will help you find the jade pendant to save my brother.”

“Kailey you are really my good daughter,” Susan was very gratified.

Kailey smiled faintly. A cold glint flashed across her eyes instantly.

All the hopes of the Maori family were now pinned on her. As long as she performed well, she would be the only hero of Blue Records Entertainment.

She would never allow Dylan to share her share.

Oliver, who had not been able to lure Renea out of school, was enraged and dejected.

If Renea had not come out of the school, how could she have vented her anger?

“Oliver what’s wrong with you?” Kailey asked as she came over and handed over the milk tea.

Those who know her family background all avoided her. Only Kailey was close to her.

Some people said that those who were close to each other were popular, and Kailey was also scolded, but they didn’t scold her so hard.

But it was obvious that Kailey didn’t have to be slandered like this. She was still with her at all costs.

Add this movement Oliver had completely changed her opinion on Kailey.

“Well don’t be upset. It’s not up to you to choose where you come from. It will be fine after a discussion.” Kailey comforted her.

Oliver shook her head and took a deep breath of milk tea. “I am angry, because Renea isn’t coming out of school.”

“Huh?” Kailey looked at her in confusion.

Oliver looked at Kailey and said with some consideration, “I want to ask her out for a talk. Take it as a way of making peace. As long as she doesn’t talk about my family background, we will just stay out of each other’s business. I have asked her out several times, but she is unmoved.

Kailey gave a smile and said, It’s not your fault for being able to meet her. Dad called her several times to invite her back to dinner. However, she didn’t even want to come back home.”

“She is just a fake daughter. What’s there to be so proud off?” Oliver said grumpily.

“She is Aron’s friend now. The Kingston family is one of the four big families in the capital city. They have very special kind of relationship.”

Kailey mumbled to herself with a faint smile on her lips. She looked both envious and yearning. However, the viciousness in her eyes revealed her jealousy.

Oliver was indignant and said, “It’s just a little bit of beauty. Who knows what what kind seductive means she used?”

“To be honest, my sister is also very pitiful.”

Kailey’s words were full of sympathy, which made Oliver explode in an instant. “She has occupied your life for 18 years, and still stays in the Morris family and doesn’t leave. Do you to still sympathize with her?”

“Don’t say that. my sister wants until now, she doesn’t know who her parents are. Speaking of which, she is more pitiful than me. The reason why she stayed in the Morris family is that she wants to find about her family background. Recently. she has been looking for a rose shaped jade pendant. I hear that it has something to do with her family background.”

“This jade pendant was always in my grandfather’s hands. But after my grandfather’s death, it disappeared.”

Like a casual chat, Kailey revealed Renea’s whereabouts to Oliver.

Not letting her down, Oliver listened to what she said and was quite interested in it.

“Where did the jade pendant disappear?”

Kailey shook her head and said, “I’m not too sure myself.”

Oliver’s long eyes narrowed. The corner of her mouth curled into a cold smile. She looked thoughtful.

Oliver called Renea once again.

Renea’s brows creased slightly when she saw the number that had frequently appeared in the past few days.

“Hello.”

“Renea are you looking for the rose shaped jade pendant?”

Renea’s brows furrowed even more when she heard this. ‘How did Oliver know that she was looking for a jade pendant?’ She thought.

“I know where the jade pendant is?”

“Where is it?” Oliver chuckled. It seemed that she had fish had taken the bait.

“Do you want to know? See you at East River Bridge at 8:00 o’clock in the evening?” After that, Oliver hung up the phone.

Renea’s lips curled up, as she put away her phone.

“Who is it?” Stella asks curiously.
