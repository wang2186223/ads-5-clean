Chapter 93

Hearing Renea’s words Susan’s face turned green and white from anger.

“Mrs. Morris, if there is nothing else, I’m going to rest. After all, it’s quite late.”

After saying that, Renea was about to leave, but she was stopped by Susan. She said, “Renea, although you are not our real daughter, we have raised you for 18 years. Could be that you don’t have any feelings for us at all?”

“What about you? Mrs. Morris has there ever been a hint of affection in your heart for me?” Renea asked with a smile that was filled with sarcasm.

In her previous life, she had held herself back and put this so-called kinship first. But what about them? And what they did do to her?

Even if there was the grace of nurturing, she had long been paid it. And as for what they owed to her, she will naturally take it back, one by one.

Susan hid her anger lowered her voice against her will and said, “Although you are not my daughter, but after raising you for so many years, how could there be no feelings at all?”

“Renea, Dylan is your brother. Even though he has done something wrong, but he had always treated you well. So, please help him.” Susan begged.

For some reason, she had a strong permission that Renea had a way to Dylan out.

Hearing this, Renea smiled and said, “Mrs. Morris, you must be joking, right? I’m just a powerless student. How can I have the ability to get a convicted murderer out.”

Seeing Renea’s attitude, Susan’s face turned red with anger. She had already softened like this, but still, she refused to help her.

Seeing that Renea was about to walk into the house, Susan snapped, “Aren’t you investigating your origin?”

Renea stopped in her tracks.

“That couple surname Davis are not your real parents!”

Renea turned back and looked at her with a playful gaze.

Susan continued, “What a paternity test or what jade pendant? They all are fake! Leon is just fooled you!”

“Fake? Why should I believe you?” Renea looked at her.

Susan sneered, “Believe it or not. Anyway, once you do a paternity test, you will find out. Don’t tell me you don’t know how secretly go to for a paternity test.

Renea looked at her, but she didn’t say anything.

“Leon did this all, because he didn’t want you to stop investigating about the jade pendant again.”

Susan had already gone out of her way. After spending so many years as a couple, she had never thought that Leon was a dependable man.

Leon is a man, who didn’t even want his son, his daughter, or even her, his wife, just for the sake of Morris’s family. For the sake of Blue Records Entertainment.

However, Dylan was her son, whom she had given birth to after so much pain. Leon, can not care about him, but how could she, his mother not care about Dylan’s life?

“As long as you’re willing to help me get Dylan out, I will give you the real jade pendant.” Susan said.

Renea said coldly, “Do you know where the real jade pendant is?”

“Leon is hiding it, but I have a way to find it,” Susan said confidently.

“Then let’s wait until you find it. But before that, I can make the case not open so soon, for a period of one month. If Mrs. Morris still can’t get the jade pendant I want in a month, I’m afraid I can’t do anything about it by then.” Dropping her words, Renea took a step towards the house.

Susan clenched her fits tightly as if she had made some sort of determination in her eyes.

“Right,” Renea turned back and hooked the corner of her mouth, “Just a reminder, the matter of me looking for the jade pendant should only be known to you and Dylan, right? Then how did Leon know about it? Think about it, and don’t get stirred up again for something you want to do.”

“What do you mean?” Susan questioned.

However, Renea didn’t pay attention to her anymore and worked inside.

Susan fell into deep thought and Renea’s words kept echoing in her ear.

To be honest, Leon shouldn’t have known about it. How did he suddenly know that Renea was looking for a jade pendant? This was something that only I, Dylan, and Kailey knew about, other than Renea.

Renea will never tell him, and I didn’t say anything either... As for Dylan, Leon would never visit him... then the only person who left was...

Could it be Kailey?

Susan revealed an incredulous expression.

“Mom, its okay. We’ll figure out a way to get my brother out together.”

Kailey’s soft voice pulled Susan out of her thoughts. Looking at the well-behaved and understanding daughter in front of her, she suddenly felt very unfamiliar to her and the word just said by Renea rang in her mind.

‘Could it really be the at Kailey had told Leon about her search for the rose jade pendant?’ Susan wondered.

“Mom, what’s wrong with you?” Being looked like this made Kailey quite uncomfortable.

Susan reacted her sight and faintly said, “Nothing. I was just wondering if the matter of Renea looking for the jade pendant was only known to me. Then how did your father know about it? And he even took out a fake jade pendant to fool Renea.

“What? The jade pendant is fake?” Kailey was shocked.

Susan snorted, “Of course, it’s fake. Since your father had the intention to hide the jade pendant, then how could he take it out so easily?”

“So, Renea also know that the jade pendant is fake?” Kailey asked tentatively.

Susan shook her head thoughtfully and looked at Kailey. Then she said, word by word, “If Renea knew that, the police would not come to the door today.”

Kailey nodded her head as if she understood.

“Kailey, your brother’s case will be in court soon. You will help me, right?” Susan asked as she pulled Kailey’s hand.

“Of course,” Kailey answered quickly and surely, “Mom, what do you mean by I will help or not? Dylan is my own brother. If something happened to him, I am more anxious than anyone else.”

“You are really mom’s good daughter,” Susan said, as she held Kailey’s hand tightly.

Kailey raised her eyes to look at Susan and asked with a tentative tone, “Mom, have you already thought off a way to save my brother?”

“The only way to save your brother is to find that real rose jade pendant.” Susan slightly deepened her eyes as said word by word.

To be honest, she was also curious as to what this jade pendant involved that made Leon prefer to sacrifice his son rather than take it out.

This was what Kailey was puzzled and curious about as well.

“Mom, do you really think Renea can save my brother?”

“What are you not wanting to help me?” Susan’s face instantly sank.
