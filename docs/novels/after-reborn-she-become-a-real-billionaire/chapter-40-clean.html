Chapter 40

Dylan was in a panic now.

Seeing the situation, Spencer immediately arranged for someone to dismiss the reporters.

Dylan fled under the escort of the bodyguards.

“What the ****! it’s shocking? Is Dylan such a playboy?” Upon looking at the news, Stella swore excitedly.

Renea smiled as she looked ahead in an unfathomable manner.

If Dylan hadn’t intentionally given her a sense of warmth in her past life, she wouldn’t have foolishly fallen into the trap.

This was only the beginning of her plan. She aimed to gradually dismantle everything they held dear.

The scandal about Dylan spread like countless aunts, instantly permeating the entire network.

From Twitter to online forums, magazine to newspaper, Internet to internet, all were filled with news of Dylan’s promiscuity.

Furthermore, these were videos of Dylan erupting in anger and shouting at reporters. It further validated the authenticity of his scandal and gave off an impression of indignation.

A loud slap rang out.

Leon was so angry that he slapped Darren across the face. “****! Look at what you have done?”

Dylan covered his face and felt wronged.

“Honey, what are you doing? This have already happened. Should not we focused on resolving the issue, Instead of beating him now?” Susan quickly defended Dylan.

Leon was currently fuming with anger. He was extremely dissatisfied with Susan’s defense of Dylan and directed his furry toward her. “It’s all because of your indulgence. Look at the mess he has caused now. The Morris family is on the verge of ruin because of him.”

“I...” Susan felt aggrieved.

Kailey, who was standing at the side muttered, “Dylan has always been careful with these affairs. Why would these photos get leaked?”

Her words instantly reminded Susan to think of Renea.

At this moment, Renea walked in from outside.

Susan angrily charged forward. However, as she approached Renea, Renea sidestepped causing Susan to miss her target and nearly stumble.

“Tell me did you spread those photos?” Susan demanded.

Both Leon and Dylan also turned their scrutinizing gaze towards Renea. After all, Renea had previously mentioned something about Dylan’s scandal.

Renea scoffed, “If it were me, I would not use those insignificant photos and chat screenshots of uncertain authenticity. I had just revealed the incident from his high school days. Wouldn’t that even more shocking?”

“You...” Susan was furious.

Leon responded swiftly. Currently, Renea is getting close with Aron and the White family. Thus she was beneficial to him.

“Alright, enough with this internal strife. Since little Renea is also entering the industry, exposing her brother’s secrets would mean tarnishing her reputation, right?” Leon reasoned.

Susan had not expected Leon’s sudden defense of Renea, which gave her a sense of crisis.

Thus, her enmity towards Renea intensified.

Leon turned to Dylan and asked, “Among the news circulating online, which parts are true and which are false?”

“All... all of them are true.” Dylan replied softly.

When Leon was about to rush forward and confront Dylan physically, Renea spoke coldly, “There is no use of beating him. Now, we should come up with solution for this matter instead. Don’t let these affairs affect the Blue records.”

Leon instantly calmed down. He coldly commanded Dylan, “I will hold a press conference tomorrow. You need to explain the news and minimize this scandal’s impact. Frame it as merely a few romantic relationships. Your popularity isn’t very high, nor do you have any many haters. With a sincere attitude, the public will likely accept it.”

Dylan nodded repeatedly as he was willing to do anything for the crisis now.

“No!” Renea immediately disagreed.

“This incident is a deliberate act of retaliation. If Dylan were to clarify things and gloss over certain events, it might provoke the culprits even more. If they hold even more damaging evidence. The situation will escalate further.” Renea reasoned.

Shane pondered Renea’s words and found them quite reasonable. Suddenly, he realized she was intelligent and understood why Sir Morris had decided to give her a portion of the inheritance.

“Renea, what do you think we should do now?” Leon asked gently.

“Dylan should focus on his work on the set and let the situation naturally fade. As you mentioned, his popularity isn’t high. Once the attention wanes, fewer people will bring it up again. There will be penalty of opportunity to restore his reputation in the future. It won’t be problem as long as he managed to create an image that gains popularity and attracts loyal fans.” Renea advised.

Leon wholeheartedly agreed with her words and praised her intelligence.

Kailey’s expression darkened followed by a sense of crisis.
