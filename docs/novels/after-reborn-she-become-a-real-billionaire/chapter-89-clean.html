Chapter 89

Renea hooked the corner of her mouth and snorted, “Your surname is Davis, My surname is Morris, how come you be my brother?”

“Do you... You didn’t want to recognize your ancestors?” Gabe said anxiously.

“Is it the ancestors who can return, not how?” Renea said as she pressed closer. The corner of her mouth was chilling, despite the smile on her face.

Gabe didn’t expect that he would be intimidated by the biting eyes of a litter girl. With a panicked expression, he involuntarily stepped back.

“How... What? Are you afraid that your good and comfortable life will be over if you come back? You didn’t want to come back, because you are not willing to suffer, right? Because of money, you didn’t even want to recognize your mom and dad?” Gabe said.

Renea looked at him with a smile.

Gabe also suddenly became bold, “You didn’t even want not recognize your own parents, right? Are you not afraid that I will stink your reputation? I’ve checked, you study in this Academy, because in the future, there is a chance of debut and became a big star, right? However, if your reputation stinks, then I want to see how can you debut?”

Speaking of which, Gabe’s guts grew a few points bigger, and his greedy face was on display.

“However, If you really don’t want to go back, I won’t force you, as long as...”

Gabe said as he made a gesture of asking for money and the corner of his mouth raised in a wry smile.

“You want money?” Renea’s smile deepened, “I can give it to you!”

Seeing Renea has compromised, Gabe was happy and more certain that this trip today was the right one.

“Don’t worry. I’m a person who always keeps his promise. As long as you pay the money, I will pretend that I don’t have you as my sister. And I can also keep mom and dad from harassing you. How’s that?” Gabe said with a smile.

Renea nodded, “Sounds good!”

Gabe was even happier and more excited. And his greedy face became even more hideous.

“However, thousand dollar are too little for me to keep my promise. If you want to keep my promise, then you have to give me ten thousand dollars!” Gabe said.

“Okay,” Renea answered again.

Seeing how quickly Renea had agreed, Gabe felt some regret for not asking for more.

However, thinking that he would have a lot of these kinds of opportunities in the future, he was relieved.

“Then give me the money,” Gabe said.

“Don’t worry. I will personally deliver it to the Destiny Garden neighbourhood tomorrow,” After dropping her words, Renea turned around and left.

Gabe chased after her and angrily rebuked, “Are you not playing some kind of trick with me, are you?”

“It seems like you don’t believe me,” Renea asked rhetorically, with a playful smile at the corner of her mouth.

Gabe was speechless by her words, In the end, he compromised and said, “Then I believe that you do not dare to play tricks with me. It’s tomorrow, right? However, let me tell you, if I didn’t see you in the destiny neighbourhood tomorrow, do not blame me for telling everyone about your business.”

Renea ignored him, turned around, and went back to the school.

The next day

Renea took a leave early in the morning and left the school. She came to the Destiny Garden neighborhood, where Mr. and Mrs. Davis lived.

This place is just a little bit better than River District but is just a little bit better.

The houses in this place looked like old buildings in the seventies. The location of this place is located in a partial suburb, so there are many factories located here and it is also near the black market area. The rent at these places is cheap, but the people who live here are very messy.

There are part-time workers and migrant workers at construction sites. The black market is also in the majority.

In every few steps, you could see bare-chested, tattoos, cigarette-chomping men and occasionally you could hear a few curses and profanities.

Renea took one step forward, careful with each step, or she would step on the poop of cats or dogs and on the random urination man who came back drunk in the middle of the night.

A foul odor surrounded the entire neighborhood.

“You brat! How dare you steal money for the family again? See, if I don’t beat you to death!”

Selena ran after a man of about 20 years old with a stick and cursed. She looked completely different from the tearful Selena of yesterday.

“Ren... Renea!” Seeing her here Selena was shocked and stopped. She stood at the side of the road with her stick.

Gabe also stopped and looked at Renea in a measured manner. The corner of his lips hooked up with a gangly smile.

‘She’s quite trustworthy,” he thought inwardly.

“Renea how... How did you came here?” Selena was excited. Then. She looked at Gabe and said, “Gabe, why are you still here. Go and get get your father! Tell him that Renea is here.”

Gabe let out a light laugh and pretended not to recognize her. He said, “Who are you? Are you my that sister who disappeared nineteen years ago? That sister who didn’t want to recognize her family after being living in wealth and glory for so long?”

“Gabe, what nonsense are you talking about? Selena said as she hit Gabe’s butt with a stick in her hand.

“Why are you still standing here? Go and get your father!”

Gabe covered his butt and left in aggravation.

Selena immediately changed her face went up with a joyful smile and said, “Renea don’t mind. That brat is just like that. He didn’t even have a proper form of speech!”

“Are you not going to invite me inside?” Renea asked.

As for Gabe’s words, she didn’t care about them at all. What kind of harsh words she had not heard before. These painless words from Gabe, she didn’t even put them in her eyes at all.

“No, I’m afraid...” Selena was halfway through her words when she met Renea’s sharp eyes and swallowed back what she was about to say.

She didn’t know why, but whenever she looked at Renea’s cold eyes, her heart became weak, and it was hard for her to say anything that came to her lips.

“It’s my house, and I had lived here before, didn’t I?” Renea said as she looked at her with a slight smile. However, her smile was cold and chilling.

Hearing that, Selena’s face was burst red and white. Seemingly smiling, she said, “Of course...”

Selena then led Renea upstairs. The stairways were piled up with debris and garbage. The pungent smell was all around the place.

Up on the third floor, Selena opened the iron door and invited Renea in.

However, looking at the mess in the house, Renea frowned.
