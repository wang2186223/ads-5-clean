Chapter 59

The reporter: “Excuse me, are you Ms.Tia Hughes?”

“That’s me!” Tia was a little flattered by the amount of people.

The reporter: “We are reporters from the Express Press and we are here to learn about your father’s illness and your relationship with the daughter of the Morris family.”

Tia wiped her tears and said gratefully, “Thank you, thank you for your concern. My father is not in a very good condition now. If he doesn’t get surgery soon, I am afraid... I am afraid that he will...”

Tia grew sadder as she spoke. In the end, she began to choke with sobs and could no longer speak.

The reporter then comforted her and took out the newspaper’s donation. “This is a small gesture from us, please accept it. We will help let other media know about this, and then we will publish your situation through the donation site, so more people will learn about your situation. I believe this can raise money for your father’s surgery and then can have a smooth operation.”

“Really? Thank you so much!” Tia was agitated.

“Miss Hughes, can you tell us more about you and Renea?”

When Renea was mentioned Tia’s face immediately darkened.

The reporter continued, “I hear that you and Renea were classmate in high school?”

When Tia heard that question she scoffed and said, “We’re not only high school classmates, but also best friends. I didn’t think she had become so heartless when she went to college.”

The reporters then asked, “Can I know what’s with the luxury cars that appeared in the video?”

“That was a post posted on a campus forum. Someone saw that she got into two different luxury cars for two days in row. Someone said that she had found herself a sugar daddy.”

“A sugar daddy!” This was a piece of big news to the reporters and they began to dive into the matter deeper.

“You look like you might know something. Can you tell us?”

Tia immediately put on a frightened look and waved her hands repeatedly, saying, “I don’t do anything. Don’t ask me. She had threatened me yesterday, saying that she had got a lawyer to sue me. I can’t afford to mess with her. She is the daughter of the rich and powerful family, while I am just an ordinary person.”

Since Tia had said so the reporter did not probe any further.

Even though the interview today seems to have no content, there was a big news beneath it.

As expected, as soon as the interview was uploaded online, it attracted a lot of attention.

“How can this fake daughter be so shameless? She even threatened Tia.”

“How can she bully people like this? Doesn’t she know that she is merely a fake daughter? Where did she get the confidence to threaten others?”

“It seems that she did really find herself a sugar Daddy, since she had the audacity to threaten Tia like this.”

“Even if she didn’t find her sugar daddy, she also spending the money of the Morris family.”

“I really don’t know why the Morris family still keeps such a shameless person at home.”

Things are slowly getting out of hand and even the Morris family was involved.

Leon, who originally didn’t care about this could not stay silent anymore after the Morris family was involved.

“Renea, what the hell is going on?”

“It’s all fake.” Renea just said those three words slightly. She didn’t even bother explaining.

Leon was not pleased with her attitude and said, “Settle this matter right away. Your brother’s matter was not settled yet, and now you have gotten yourself into trouble again. Our family’s reputation will be ruined by you guys sooner or later.”

“By the way what’s going on with the luxury cars and sugar daddy? What have you done?” Leon questioned again.

Originally he wanted to rely on Renea to establish a relationship with the White and Kingston families. If she were to mess around outside, how would he be able to get close to the White and Kingston families?

Before Renea could speak, Kailey beat her to it, “Dad, that’s what other thinks. It’s not true. As for Tia’s matter, its just a misunderstanding between Renea and her. Well, however, whoever started it had put an end to it. As long as Renea goes to explain everything to Tia, this matter will be over.”

“Then you go and explain this matter clearly,” Leon ordered coldly.

“By tomorrow, I want to see all the news about you disappear from the Internet.”

Renea didn’t respond. Instead, a faint smile flashed on her face.

“Renea are you trying to bribe me with money? This is a blatant humiliation and even if I needed money right now, I wouldn’t say anything against my will. Listen, I was not the one who posted the school forum. I didn’t know if you have been kept us as a sugar baby, but others have indeed seen with their own eyes that you have gotten into the server luxury cars.”

Tia released a video. In the video she was holding a bank card in her hand and accusing Renea seriously she said to the camera.

People who were wondering before were now almost certain that Renea was being kept by someone as a sugar baby, otherwise, why was she rushing to bribe Tia?

Leon slammed the newspaper on the dining table and said, “Look at what you have done?”

Renea however, just took a brief look at the newspaper and continued to eat breakfast.

Susan picked up the newspaper and gave it a quick look. She then scolded angrily, “Why are you so stupid? If you are not completely sure, why did you go and bribe her?”

Renea pointed at Kailey and said, “Scold her not me.”

Hearing this Leon and Susan looked back at Kailey at the same time.

Susan was the first to ask, “Kailey, what is this all about?”

“I’m sorry” Kailey lowered her eyes and apologized in a low voice, “When I saw that Renea was unwilling to reconcile with Tia, I went to find her. We had a good conversation and Tia promised to clear Renea’s name as well.”

“I... I felt sorry for Tia, so I gave her my pocket money, hoping she could help her a little.”

“Are you dumb? If you give her money at this time, doesn’t it mean you are giving people the opportunity to get the goods on you?” Leon scolded furiously.

Kailey bowed her head even lower and explained pitifully,”When I left at that time, Tia promised me again and again, that she had clarified the whole thing for Renea. Could it be that Renea said something to Tia afterwards and enraged Tia and caused her to change her mind?”

“Isn’t that obvious? If it were not for that Renea said, how could Tia not take the money and expose what Renea did?” Susan said with certainty.

In Susan’s eyes, if something went wrong, it was certainly not her daughter’s fault.

The corners of Renea’s lips curled into a sneer when she saw how shamelessly they were when they were blaming her.

“Kailey, do you want me to show you the surveillance video of the hospital?” Renea said out of the blue.
