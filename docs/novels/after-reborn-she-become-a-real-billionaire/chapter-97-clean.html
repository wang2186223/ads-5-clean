Chapter 97

Today is the date of Dylan’s case in the court, and the media are all there in droves.

However, not even a single person from the Morris family appeared at the scene.

All the evidence and testimony were provided by Miley’s mother.

Most favorably, there was also a video of Dylan and Miley’s conversation on the rooftop.

Although they could not hear what the two were saying, from the looks of the two, they were arguing. Dylan looked excited and even slapped Miley.

Most pertinently, this video is the day when Miley jumped from the rooftop.

“No, no, no, Your Honor. I didn’t kill the person. She jumped herself,” Dylan argued.

Plaintiff’s attorney: “Defendant when you say that my client jumped on his own, do you have any evidence that you were not there?”

“I can’t prove it...” he said. How can he prove that he was not there when he saw Miley jump down from the rooftop with his own eyes?

Plaintiffs’ counsel: “Your Honor. It was obvious that the defendant couldn’t prove that he was not present because he was the one who pushed her. Today many women and mothers are present. And I want to ask from them, what kind of mother would harm her child?”

“If my client didn’t want to live and chose to commit suicide, she wouldn’t have prepared so many daily necessities for the child before he was born after knowing that she was pregnant. So, it was obvious how much she loved the child. As far as I understand, the defendant broke up with my client before my client became pregnant, right?”

“That was her... She was trying to threaten me with the child!” Dylan angrily rebuked.

Plaintiff’s attorney: “So, you just pushed my client off the rooftop, right?”

“I didn’t...” Dylan tried to defend himself, however, there was no reason or evidence to convince the crowd.

The Internet has been already cursing him for a long time now.

Miley’s mother, who had been silent, got up with tears in her eyes and choked, “I’m here today because I just want to ask justice for my daughter to respond to the justice that is there. And I don’t want her to die as an unknown weather. The most chilling thing to me is that after the incident, not only did the murderer not repent, but he even attempted to bribe me with money. And when he saw that I was unboost, he even found someone to threaten me.”

“Your Honor, my client also has a recording to prove her saying, and I ask that it be played in court.”

The judge nodded.

“Your daughter is dead already, and do you have proof that I killed her?”

“Who else could it be if not you? You murder. I will not let you go!”

“Heh, not letting me go? What can you do to me? Sue me? Do you have any evidence? You had better be good and take this money, Or don’t blame me for being rude to you. Remember, I just accidentally pushed her, it was her who fell without standing still.”

The conversation is the smack in the face who listens to it.

Dylan, as the Crown Prince of Blue Records Entertainment, had always possessed a humble and polite persona in the circle. Although he had been in the industry for many years, he had never acted out of his persona.

However, today Dylan’s persona has been completely crumbled in front of everyone.

Fans who were previously trying to clear his name are now disassociating themselves, saying that they were hurt by his behavior.

Dylan’s face was like ashes. Right now, he knew very well that, even if he pleaded a hundred times, no one would believe that he had not killed anyone.

He had never expected that this woman would be so rigid. Not only she didn’t want money, but she also recorded everything to leave a backhand.

Since the evidence is conclusive, the court made a verdict that Dylan was found guilty of murder and was sentenced to death and deprived of political rights for life...

Everyone knows the truth, and Miley’s mother’s wish has been fulfilled. At that moment when she walked out of the court, she felt so relaxed.

As she came out, her eyes fell on Renea who was standing under the tree and she walked over with big strides.

“Miss Morris, thank you. Thank you so much!” Miley’s mother said as she bent her legs and was about to kneel in front of her.

Renea stopped her and said, ‘You don’t have to do it.”

“If it was not for you, this beast Dylan could not have been ambushed.”Miley’s mother said excitedly.

Three years ago, after she knew about the truth behind her daughter’s death, she had thought of letting Dylan ambushed. However, at that time, she had no money or power. As a single mother, she was completely powerless. If it was not for Renea she would never be able to give justice to her daughter.”

“I’m not exactly doing your favor here.”

Although Renea said so, Miley’s mother still took her as a benefactor.

“Miss Morris, if there is anything you need my help with in the future, just say it. As long as I can do it, I will certainly be obliged!”

“No need!” Renea said as she turned to leave. Her back was heartbreakingly lonely.

The lesson of her previous life’s exorcism had taught her, that she could not rely on anyone in this world, but only herself. That is the only way when she can be strong and would not be harmed.”

In Dylan’s matter, the Morris family does not react at all. Especially Leon, he pretended as if the one who was executed was not his son.

Susan secretly wiped her tears when she saw the news.

Kailey, on the other hand, was the happiest person at the moment. For her Dylan was a major hidden danger in the way of her success. Now there would be no one to steal from her in the future, and she would be the sole heir of the Morris family.

“Renea, I have found my heroine,” Carson said.

On the phone, Carson sounded both emotional and excited.

“Whether it’s her appearance or a temperament, she fits every way in the image of my heroine.”

“Who is she?” Renea asked.

“Amelia Albert!”

“Amelia Albert!” Renea frowned slightly.

According to her knowledge, she is the artist who had signed under K.W Media long after.

“Renea, what’s wrong?”

Renea: “She is a new signing artist under K.W. Media and a new signing of K.W. Media only shoots for their own company’s play and didn’t have a right to pick the play outside of their company.”

This is also the reason why stars, who are just starting, want to go to K.W. Media. However, K.W. Media is particularly strict in recruiting. After all, Kevin has a pair of poisonous eyes.

“Right! Oh, I forgot about this in my happiness, “Carson’s tone instantly became lost.

“I will look again then, but she fits.” Carson’s tone was filled with regret.

Renea held the phone without saying anything. After all, it was not within her ability.

And she never made commitments beyond her means.
