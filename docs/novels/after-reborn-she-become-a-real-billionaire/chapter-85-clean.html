“I read the notice that tomorrow we have a very important scene. Jasper, if you don’t mind, can I come to your room for rehearsal?”

Jasper looked at her, almost drooling.

If you can sign up with Blue Records Entertainment, you don’t have to worry about gaining good resources. Even if it were not top-notch resources, it was still much better than the average small entertainment company.

To this feeling of being held up, Jasper was particularly enjoying himself. As a result, he didn’t reject Rita’s gesture of goodwill.

“Mr. Thomas. director over there is calling you, asking you to rehearse your intimate scene with Mr. Franklin,” the assistant came over and said.

Jasper’s face sank as he muttered in a low voice, “What’s the point of interacting with a man?”

The assistant was silent on the sideline.

Even though Jasper was reluctant, he still got up and walked over. Because it was his job, so he could not only reluctantly deliver it.

In this drama not only did Jasper not only has to play a few secret ambiguous scenes with his opponent, but he also has to this kind of ambiguous scene from time to time. This was the highlight of the drama so that they could get more CP fans.

“Renea why don’t you come back home today?”

Renea received a call from Leon.

However, this was not the first time that he had called her. Ever since she had started living at school Leon had called every week and asked her to come back home.

“No, I am not coming.” Renea replied coldly.

“Renea just take this time to come home today. I have something important that to tell you.”

“What is it? Just tell me on the phone.”

“I found your real parents!”

Leon’s words made Renea freeze for a second.

I have asked them to come home tonight, so you also come back home after the class.”

After hanging up the phone, Renea’s calm heart suddenly had a small rattle. However, it was only for a moment, soon it returned to normal as if nothing had happened. After the class, Renea packed her things and walked out of the classroom.

The Morris family

Standing outside the door, Renea could hear the laughter in the living room.

“Renea you’re back, quick come over and sit.” Susan greeted her warmly.

This was the first time she had been so enthusiastic about Renea.

Kailey also ran over, took Renea’s hand, and said excitedly, “Renea, I have good news for you. Dad has found your real parents.”

“Renea, come over here and meet your real parents.” Leon also said with a smile.

Then, Renea looked at the middle-aged couple sitting on the sofa, dressed in plain clothes. And at the same middle-aged couple also looked at her with teary eyes.

They looked at each other quietly for a while.

The woman in the middle-aged couple tearfully opened her mouth first, “You are.. my daughter, Renea.”

The middle-aged man also looked at her with teary eyes.

“Renea, why didn’t you greet your parents yet?” Susan urged from the sideline as if she was afraid Renea would not recognize them.

“Parents?” Renea spoke coldly, “Do you have any proof?”
