Chapter 77

Renea scoffed, “You can’t even admit your girlfriend’s existence. Why should I like you? It’s good to be narcissistic but if you are over narcissistic you will become a joke.”

“Pftt!”

Stella could not help but laugh out loud.

When she saw Jasper’s narcissistic face, she wanted nothing more than to slap him and wake him up.

Sure enough, no one can stand Renea’s sharp tongue.

“Renea don’t you know what’s good for you.” Jasper shook his head angrily and left.

When he became famous, he would just wait for her to kneel and lick it. Jasper thought to himself.

Renea looked at the time and put away her book. She picked up the ordinary clothes bag and said, “Stella I’m going out for a while. I have to attend the lecture in the lecture in the afternoon, so if I am late, give me a call.”

Stella made an OK gesture.

If she had not made an appointment with Carson to talk about the important matters of the shoot, Renea would not have skipped classes. Furthermore, this afternoon, the class was the class of the head teacher.

Not long after leaving the school, Renea was surrounded by a group of people before she even reached the coffee shop opposite the school.

She was very familiar with the leader. It was Primo who had been beaten by her yesterday. The man behind him was also the group of people from yesterday.

“He came to me so soon.”

Renea looked at them coldly.

Primo stepped forward and fell to his knees.

This sudden move caught Renea off guard.

“I’m sorry I was wrong. I hope you can forgive me.”

“Plop, plop...”

The man behind him also knelt one after another.

“Please forgive us.”

This scene attracts a lot of people’s attention.

“Madam, what do you think we should do to get your forgiveness? As long as you said we will be in the oil pot and go to the sea of the fire.”

“Fu*k off!” Renea rebuked angrily.

“Okay!”

Primo and others rolled on the ground, which made the onlookers laugh out loud.

Renea turned to leave. However, she was quite curious. Primo had said yesterday that he was looking for Dragon to make trouble for her. But now, he was here kneeling in front of her.

It was obvious that Primo was forced. The only person who could force him was Dragon.

Howard Dragon was not a coward. Even if he did not take Primo seriously, he would not let him disgrace himself.

She had already arrived at the coffee shop. Renea, didn’t think too much about it.

“Renea here,” Carson waved at her.

Renea strode over.

“Renea, I don’t know what you like to drink, so I ordered a glass of juice for you.”

Carson said with a smile. He seemed to be in a good mood.

“Thank you.” Renea sat down.

She didn’t care much about food and drinks. As long as she could fill her stomach and quench her thirst, she could do it.

“Renea take a look. This is the list of actors and the list of actresses. At present, we have selected these people through the application of me, the director, and the producer. If you don’t have any problem, let’s start the selection.”

Renea took the document, gave it a quick look, then closed it, “You and the director are both professionals in business management. If you think there is nothing wrong with it, you can start it. As for the cost of opening it, I will call you later.”

It was time to sell the stock of King’s group that she had bought last time.

At present, as long as the play was started, the following expenses would not be too big. And the funds in her hands could be supported. The stock market was turbulent. She could make the sum of money by relying on her foresight in her previous life.

After King’s group raised this time, it would fall out of the stock market. There was no need for her to enter the stock market and do things that she was not sure of.

“Renea, are you sure you want to start the business secretly and don’t want to find investors?” Carson asked, feeling that it was inappropriate.

If they didn’t attract investment, she would have to pay for the production process herself.

“It’s alright we still have money for our shoot,” Renea said with a smile.

Carson looked at her, but he was quite frustrated. He was also a person who had been beaten up by society, but he couldn’t even figure out the thoughts of the altar girl in front of him.

“How was the preparation for ‘My Sweet Home’?” Renea asked.

“We have already chosen actors. The male lead is fine, but the female lead is testing her acting skills. We have not found a suitable one yet.”

The script was written by Carson himself, just like his own child. The image of the female lead had been drawn in his mind. If he couldn’t find the person in his mind, he wouldn’t feel like he wanted to perform it.

“There is no hurry. If there is not a suitable one, let’s continue with the screening. We will able to find a suitable one from so many actors.”

Carson nodded and said, “We will continue to take a look. If there is no suitable one, I will pick one from a similar one.”

The two of them talked about things that happened after the shoot. Carson left first, but Renea was not in a rush to leave. Instead, she probed her head on the table and looked out the window, enjoying the tranquility.

“Can I sit here?”

This sudden voice brought Renea back to reality. She raised her head and frowned slightly as she looked at the man in front of her.

Darren was also stunned. He looked at the photo and felt it was similar. But, now that he saw the real person, he felt her eyes were the same.

Originally, he came here to see her out of curiosity. He wanted to see what kind of charm the woman Richard and Kevin cared about had.

What made him even more curious was that he had checked her on the Internet. There was nothing except that she was an adopted daughter of the Morris family.

It was too clean. His intuition told him that it was not that simple. Just as he wanted to understand more about her, he was stopped.

Even though he was a top master in the hacker world, he couldn’t break into her account.
