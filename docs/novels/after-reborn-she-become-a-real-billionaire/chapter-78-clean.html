Chapter 78

Renea didn’t agree. Darren didn’t stand at the ceremony and sat down.

Renea ignored him and looked out of the window again.

Darren didn’t say anything. He just looked at her and her eyes.

“Your eyes are so beautiful!” Darren could not help praising her.

Renea’s brows creased slightly. ‘Why did everyone say that her eyes were beautiful when they saw her?’ She thought.

Darren continued, “It’s similar to my fourth brother’s eyes.

“Fourth brother?” Renea looked at him from head to toe.

Even though the man in front of her looked like a hooligan with an evil smile, she didn’t hate him at all.

“Kevin!”

After Darren’s reminder, Renea discovered that he did look a bit like Kevin.

On the Internet, there was Kevin’s personal information, including his family members, his parents, his brothers and his being number fourth.

“Student, how about adding me on WhatsApp? I know Kevin. We can be considered friends.” Darren handed over his phone with a smile.

Renea took a look and rejected with a smile, “I have only met Mr. White once.”

The implication was that Kevin and she were not familiar with each other, so they didn’t know each other.

Darren smiled and didn’t feel embarrassed because of being rejected.

“What are you doing here?”

A gloomy voice comes. Aron walked over with a cold and gloomy face.

Renea startled slightly. This was the first time she had seen such a cold expression from Aron.

When she looked at Darren again, she saw that his smile had disappeared, and his expression was cold.

They knew each other, and there was a grudge between them.

Aron sat down next to Renea and looked coldly at Darren, “What are you doing here?”

Before Darren could speak, Aaron continued, “Stay away from her, or don’t blame me for being rude.”

Renea had a sudden curiosity, as her eyes looked back and forth between the two of them.

Darren snorted coldly and said, “What are you so nervous about? I’m just having a chat with Renea.”

Renea!

Aron’s cold eyes deepen slightly.

Renea was also stunned. Darren knows her name is not strange, but when they became intimate.

“Don’t look at me with that kind of look. Do you think I’m like you, playing the old cow eating young grass? I’m just treating Renea as my sister.”

Darren said nonchalantly.

He didn’t know why. When he saw Renea for the first time, he felt incomparably close to her, probably because her eyes were too similar, making him unconsciously treat her as his sister.

Aron’s face instantly darkened into charcoal.

Renea nearly spats out a mouthful of coffee.

To be honest, from the beginning, she did not think these two people were what enemies were. Although they look at each other coldly, there is a familiarity between them. That kind of familiarity, like a conflict between friends, which still had not been resolved until that moment.

Renea very sensibly and opened her mouth and said, “Aron, I still have class in the afternoon. You guys talk, I’ll go back to school first.”

“Hmm, Hmm!” Aron nodded, his gaze was soft as water, completely unlike the eyes when he looked at Darren.

He had come to see if the Gang of Dragon Cruel had come to apologize.

After Renea left Aron’s complexion instantly sank dark. “When did you come back?”

“Does it have anything to do with you?” Darren said, with a trace of humor.

Aron did not bother to pay any more attention and got up to leave.

“I heard that your company system was hacked by a top hacker.”

Aron stopped, “It’s already been resolved.”

“You...” Darren looked back at him.

“No, there was a help, and we don’t know who it is yet.”

“Hmm!” Darren looked interested.

Aron saw throw his mind and said coldly, “Should we go and take a look?”

“Just take a look!” Darren got up and followed.

Marshall was stunned as Aron bought Darren back into the company.

‘Is this a reconciliation?’ he thought.

The two were close to breaking up a fight over a woman before.

That’s how long it took to make up.

However, he didn’t blame Mr. Kingston for being angry. Darren’s vision was not good. Even he can see that the woman is not a good girl, spreading the net everywhere, but Darren likes her very much.

Darren opened the computer and lightly entered the AK group system.

Looking at the layer of reinforced firewall, his entire body was shocked.

“Oh My God! There are such powerful people on the international hacker network. How come I don’t know?”

“This person has hidden his ID as if he is deliberately trying to keep people from knowing.”

Still shocked. Darren tried to maneuver the computer and breach the firewall, but it was useless.

“It’s awesome and beautifully done.”

Marshall was shocked at the side. Someone who could make the Great God Darren praise him would be a really powerful person.

“You’ve got a good firewall this time, and I’m afraid no one will be able to break through it. So, feel free to use it. However, I have to find this person.”

Darren began to put into the computer operation. Although the other party deliberately hid the ID, Darren top in the top hacker, for it was nothing. According to the footprints left by the other party, he can follow the trail and find the source.

Renea who was in class, her cellphone, emitted a strong vibration. She frowned and pulled out her cell phone. A small red dot appeared on the cellphone shield. And this small red was rapidly approaching another small green dot on the shield.

Renea’s expression became grave.

“Teacher, I have a stomachache and want to go to the restroom.” Renea raised her hand.

A teacher who was giving a lecture looked at it and realized that it was Renea who had the best grade. So, she didn’t say anything, “Go ahead.”
