Chapter 64

In the room, Renea was sitting in front of the window looking calmly at the fine drizzle outside. The corner of her mouth was slightly raised, but there was no warmth on her face. Instead, it looked cold as ice.

After a while, she slowly looked away, opened the drawer, took out the wooden box, then took out the fish tank and put a small, dried fish head into the blood fish tank.

Soon, the dry fish head was swallowed by the blood in the fish tank.

Looking at the blood in the fish tank, the smile on Renea’s lips deepened. Her deep and serene eyes seemed to be covered in layers of dust, bottomless.

Now, there were only four dried fish heads left in the wooden box.

Tia was a fraud with a large amount of money. She was sentenced to 10 years of imprisonment and was stripped of her life.

The case of slandering Renea has been sorted out, and it could be said that she had committed an even heavier crime.

Although Tia’s father had undergone an operation, he was already in advanced age cancer. In addition, his daughter had an accident, so he could not survive.

After losing two relatives in a row, Isabella’s mind was broken, and she became crazy.

She ran to the gate of the Morris family every day and insulted Kailey.

“Kailey you despicable **** you took advantage of my daughter to harm her, but you refuse to save her.”

Everyday scolding made Leon quite dissatisfied with Kailey.

“What have you done?”

Kailey felt wrong as she said, “What, can I do? It was all because I helped Tia last time that her mother insisted on asking me to save Tia. What can I do?”

“Honey, you really can’t blame Kailey for this. This woman is now crazy and is just cursing blindly.” Susan was protecting her.

Now that something had happened to Dylan all of her hopes had fallen only on Kailey.

No matter what she had to maintain the image of Kailey in front of Leon.

Although Leon was very dissatisfied, he didn’t blame her too much. He said, in a low voice, “Find someone to get rid of this crazy woman. If she keeps making trouble at the gate, it wouldn’t be good for the reputation of the Morris family.”

“Okay, I will get to someone to do it later.” Susan nodded repeatedly.

Leon looked at Kailey and said nothing more.

Dylan already had an accident and for the sake of the reputation of the Morris family, he had to cut the father’s son’s relationship with him. At present, the Kailey was the only hope of the Morris family. The future of the Morris family entirely depends on her.

As for Renea, in his eyes, she was just a pawn that could be used. He naturally wouldn’t hand over the Morris family to such an outsider.

No one knows what Susan had done. However, from then on, Isabella had never been seen at the Morris family residence.

The Morris family was quiet.

With a lot of investment, Leon immediately started the big drama based on the novel “Reborn: Magic of Love.”

One of the male leads in the drama was Jasper, and the other one was an unknown actor, William Franklin.

The two men mainly changed the drama. At present, the market is unclear and famous actors especially social stars would not take the risk to change the drama.

Moreover, the biggest disadvantage of changing the drama was that it could not be satirized and could only be broadcast as an online drama.

In the current entertainment market, there is still a certain gap between upper-star drama and online drama. For the time being, the data of the online drama would not be counted in the artist’s performance.

In today’s new era, even artists and actors were counting the data, because the investors only cared about the data.

Without beautiful data, it would be difficult to exchange good resources.

Therefore, in the current movie market, only some newcomers or unknown actors could be found.

To the locals, it was an excellent resource to be able to play the leading role in an online drama.

Because it was adapted from the novel, it had a group of book fans before the filming. And the tolerance level for the new people was higher than that of traffic.

Just like now, the actors had been recruited and the following comments were all the same.

“He looks good. Let’s see if he works has been ruined after the play.”

“At present, it looks quite suitable. I hope it doesn’t change.”

“They look like a couple. I hope they don’t change.”

As soon as the official announcement was issued, the number of followers on Twitter by Jasper and William had repeatedly increased to nearly 10,000.

As a student, Jasper had become a popular figure in the Dream High Academy.

There were not only girls around them, but also boys.

As a member of an ordinary family, Jasper had never felt so proud of himself. He had also become arrogant.

“Jasper congratulations!”

“Kailey thank you. Thank you for fighting for this opportunity for me. Don’t worry, I won’t let you down.” Jasper said, as he held Kailey’s hand tightly and looked at her affectionately.

Kailey nodded and lowered her eyes shyly, just like a girl who had just fallen in love, shy.

The current Jasper was indeed Kailey’s bargaining chip. If he becomes famous and becomes a popular artist for the company, it will bring benefit to the company. As a recommended person, wouldn’t it be better for her to be a girlfriend?

Just when Kailey was thinking about it, an uninvited guest suddenly appeared.
