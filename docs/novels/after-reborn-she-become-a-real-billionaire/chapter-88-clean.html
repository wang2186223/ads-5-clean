Chapter 88

The one who spoke first was Lily York, who had ranked third in the preliminary exam. Before coming here, she was very famous in her high school for her talent and was the number one in the whole school, especially in dance. She had even won the National Awards.

However, she lost to Renea in dancing by three points and pulled third in the result. Because of this she always felt that it was shameful for her to be overtaken by a fake millennial.

However, Lily had thought that she would beat Renea in the midterm exam, and give her a clean sweep. However, things didn’t go the way she had planned. Yesterday when this score came down, she was again crushed by Renea and because of this, she was so angry that she didn’t sleep all night.

However, this morning, when she saw that Renea was being criticized by the whole school for disliking the poor and disowning her biological parents, she came up with a trick to take revenge on Renea.

Anyway, in this way, no one would suspect that she was doing this. She was jealous of Renea because she had better in the exam than hers. Instead, they all this she has done the right thing.

After all, a person who disowns her biological parents is a cause for a great dislike.

Renea looked sternly at her and asked in a cold voice, “Did you put the water there?”

“Renea, not only are you a white eyed wolf, but you can also talk nonsense with your eyes wide open. Which eye of yours see that I put it there?” Lily sneered and retorted.

“Who would not want to do justice to a white eyed wolf like you could dislike the poor because of rich, and who disowned her own biological parents? A person like you should be judged by ten thousands people!”

“Judgement? You are the judge?” Renea said as she pressed closer.

The glare of biting eyes scared Lily so much that she instinctively backed away.

“Without knowing the whole truth, you shouldn’t comment, don’t you know? Even a elementary school kid knows this.”

Renea had forced Lily to the point, where she had no way to retreat, and Lily’s face turned white with fear. Everyone else was also too scared to make a sound.

Lily gathered her courage and angrily rebuked, “Renea, you don’t need to preach here. If you don’t dislike the poor and love the rich, how come you would have delayed leaving the Morris family? And you didn’t even recognize your real parents when they came to the door.”

Seeing that Renea didn’t say anything Lily became a little more bold.

“You are just a shameless!”

“Snap!”

Suddenly the loud ringing sound in the air shocked everyone.

None of them expected Renea to hit someone in public.

Lily covered her face and cursed ugly, “Renea, you ****! How dare you to hit me? I will fight you to death!”

After saying that she rushed forward to fight with Renea.

Seeing the situation, the other rushed forward to stop the fight. And when they said they were stopping the fight, they meant that they had stopped Renea.

Several people pulled Renea by one arm, and someone wrapped an arm around her waist to keep her from moving.

Lily, who had happened to be mad as hell and wanted to beat Renea, had no one to stop her.

Seeing that the slap was about to land on her face, Renea turned her head sideways and slapped landed on the face of a classmate who was trying to stop her from fighting.

The heavy slap made the girl’s eyes bleary and her face felt hot and painful.

Lily saw that she lost her hands, so she raised her hand to slap her again, however, Renea took her backward, pulled the arm of the girl who was holding and arm, and made her receive the Lily’s slap.

One by one, two people were beaten by Lily. Seeing this, the others let go of Renea, fearing that the scourge would affect them.

Lily once again raised her hand to slap her, however, this time she was accurately caught by Renea. She dragged her hand to the front, then curbed her arm, with a calm expression on her force. It seemed like she didn’t use any force at all.

However, the tears and pain that flowed down from Lilly’s face could tell how much force she had used.

“Just because I didn’t count on your repeated provocations, it doesn’t mean I won’t count on them. So, don’t challenge my bottom line with your pretty tricks again,” Renea said coldly, her biting eyes like a cold dagger.

Lily was so scared that she had forgotten the pain in her arm.

The others also didn’t dare to come forward.

Renea forcefully shook Lily away. Then her cold eyes swept the crowd, and she warned sternly, “If he wants to say bad things about me in the future, you had better avoid me. Because if I hear you, what happens to her, will happens to you? If you don’t believe me, you can give a try.”

Dropping her words. Renea walked out of the side and started practicing her dance as if nothing had happened.

Lily screamed in pain, and the rest of the classroom rushed to help Lily to the medical room. She was diagnosed with a dislocated arm.

“It’s didn’t look like Renea use a lot of force. How could it be dislocated?”

“It’s horrible! It’s horrible!”

After seeing what happened to Liliy, they were glad that they had not just gone head-to-head with Renea

After the dance teacher walked into the classroom, she saw that Renea was the only one there. She was startled at first and then coldly questioned, “Why are you here alone? Where are the other students?”

“I don’t know.” Renea answered frankly.

Hearing that the dance teacher thought that the other students didn’t come because of Renea. She had also seen the post in the school forum and knew about what was going on. She already had a bad impression of Renea and because of this, it deepened even more.

“Since the other students didn’t come, you can study on your own this class.” After saying that the dance left the classroom.

Renea also didn’t expect her to teach her. For what she would teach her, she already knows. And the reason why she came here was only to train herself.

“Student Renea, there is someone looking for you outside the school?” A classmate, from outside the classroom door, said in a very friendly manner.

As if she didn’t hear, Renea continued her unfinished book until the time was up. After that, she put her books, packed up her things, and left the classroom.

At the entrance of the school, a boy about 20 years old, dressed in a flowing manner, wandered outside the door.

“You are looking for me?” Renea asked as she approached him.

Gabe looked at Renea in a measured manner and asked “You are Renea?”

“Yes!”

Getting an answer, the corner of Gabe’s mouth hooked up in a smile as he said, “I’m your brother. My name is Gabe Davis.”

Renea looked at him and didn’t say anything. She had already guessed it when she saw him.

“I heard that you inherited quite a lot of the Morris family legacy.” Gabe asked again.

Renea ignored it.

Gabe continued, “Your brother little tight these days. First, lend me 1000 or so for me to use.”

Hearing that, Renea turned around and was about to leave.

Seeing that Renea didn’t care about him at all, Gabe chased after her blocked her way, and said, “I’m your brother.”

“What’s your last name?”

Renea’s question made Gabe not react for a moment. “Surname... My surname is Davis, what’s wrong?”
