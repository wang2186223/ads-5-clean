Chapter 81

Heaven plaza.

Renea stood at the front door of the mall with a ponytail. She was wearing a red shirt with jeans, and the corner of the shirt, tied into the jeans, showing off her tall figure to the fullest.

Such a simple pairing of clothes allows her to show a different and youthful style.

She was ten minutes early.

Aron hasn’t come yet.

“Renea!” Jasper walked in wearing a flowery shirt and looked flirty.

Renea looked at him with a slight frown and ignored it.

“Let’s go, whatever you want to buy, I will pay for it as a gift for us, getting back together.” Jasper said boldly, putting his hand on Renea’s shoulder.

Looking at his filthy hand on her shoulder, Renea’s eyes turn cold. Then, a loud sound of click to be heard in...

“Ah!” Jasper groaned in pain.

“Renea, what are you doing?” Jasper held up his dislocated hand in anger.

Renea said coldly, “I should be the one to ask you this.”

“You are the one who asked me out, said I should come to buy clothes with you. What? Are you losing your memory now?”

“I asked you out?” Renea laughed softly. Thinking of the cellphone that had been touched yesterday, she immediately understood everything in her heart.

Seeing that she didn’t believe him, Jasper took out her cell phone and opened WhatsApp, and on it was indeed a message sent to him by Renea.

Renea you are pretentious. If you want to use this opportunity to prove your superiority, I advise you to put it away. I don’t have so much time to play with you right now.”

Jasper spoke his words extremely heavily knowing that only then did Renea know to take them seriously.

Thinking that she would be licking his boots and apologizing to him in a minute.

On the other side, Aron just got out of the car, when Kailey walked over and greeted him, “Mr Kingston, what a coincidence.”

Aron cast a sidelong glance at her and paid no attention.

Aron believed that talking to someone he didn’t know well, would just seem like a waste of breath.

“Is Mr Kingston also here to shop at the mall? It just so happens that I’m too. I have an appointment with Jasper. How about you Mr. Kingston? Kailey didn’t give up and continued to talk to him.

However, Aron still didn’t say a word.

“Sister... Jasper... how come they are...” Kailey called out in surprise.

Aron stopped his footsteps, followed her line of sight, and looked over. In the distance, he saw Renea and Jasper standing together. It seemed like they were talking about something as they were looking at the same mobile phone. Their actions looked very intimate.

Kailey looked at Aron’s face and spoke slowly, “I think that elder sister still had not let go of Jasper. After all, elder sister had liked Jasper for more than three years. Even though she break up now, it was not easy to let him go.”

Aron ignored her and walked over with big strides.

Kailey looked at Aron’s leaving figure and her lips curled up in a cold smile. She believed that Aron wouldn’t tolerate a woman who had another man in her heart.

“Renea!” Aron approached her and gave Jasper a cold look.

Even with just a single biting glance, Jasper was shocked.

Renea smiled and went forward, “Aron, you are here.”

“Sorry, I’m late.”

“It’s okay. Let’s go.”

Saying that Renea and Aron walked inside together.

Seeing this, Kailey’s heart was filled with jealousy. She had never expected that Aron would not be angry at all.

Jasper, on the other hand, was furious and felt that Renea had tricked him.

“Renea, what do you mean? Not only did you ask me out, but you also ask someone else out too?”

Hearing that, Renea and Aron stopped their steps and turned back to look at the same time.

Jasper stepped forward, took out the evidence of the text messages on his phone, and handed it to Aron saying, Mr. take a closer look, she asked me out too.”

“Renea, I didn’t expect that you’ve learned to diffuse the net now,” Jasper sneered.

Jasper looked at Aron and laughed softly, “Handsome boy, you are just another fish in her net.”

Jasper knew who Aron was, but he had never seen Aron in person or his photo. So, he thought that he was just an ordinary handsome man.

When Renea was with him, she only thought he was handsome.

Aron’s sharp eyes were slightly deeper as if they were covered with a layer of gray.

Jasper thought that he was angry at his word and was gloating to see how Renea would get dumped.

However...

Arron turned his gaze and looked at Jasper. His thin lips slightly opened as he spoke, “Do you know what happens when a fish dies?”

Jasper was bewildered, completely missing the meaning of Aron’s words.

“A dead fish stinks and how can a sticky fish deserve to be on the net,” Aron said lightly.

“You...” Hearing Aron’s words, Jasper’s face turned red with anger.

“Renea, let’s go.” Aron held Renea’s hand and left.

Competing with a dead fish will only bring you down.

Aron didn’t ask about Jasper and Renea didn’t explain.

In Renea’s opinion, there is no point in explaining what Jasper had done.

The two of them went straight to the top luxury selection of the mall as if nothing had happened.

This place is full of high luxury customized clothes and one of them is over eight figures.

“Welcome!”

The two of them just entered the store, when the salesperson came over and respectfully welcomed them. All the salespeople working here, come after professional training.

During the training, they were get familiar with all the tycoons of the entire Z city or the country.

When Aron walks in, the salesman immediately knows his identity.

“Mr. Kingston, this is new suit, just designed out by Dark designer. Would you like to try it?”

The salesman immediately recommended it.

Renea looked at the suit on the modeling track and shook her head, “No, it’s too formal. He will look old in it.”
