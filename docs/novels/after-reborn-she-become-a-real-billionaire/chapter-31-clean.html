Chapter 31

“Kailey!” Sophia and others came up to her.

Tia praised, “Kailey your dance just now was too beautiful. You look like an angel from above, an unparalleled beauty.”

“I’m just being my usual self. It’s better because Richard’s plays it well.”Kailey said with a smile.

“Kailey you are so modest. You dance so much better than Renea. I don’t understand how she gets a perfect score?”

Sophia began to feel intelligent for Kailey again.

At the mention of Renea, Kailey’s expression immediately changed and she retorted, “Sophia, please don’t bat mouth my sister. She is a good dancer too.”

“You are just too kind. I bet you have no idea that the ticket in Renea’s hands are fake. She could not even get in.”

At the thought of Renea, living in a wretched state, Sophia was pleased, as if she had vented the frustration she had previously endured.

Kailey exclaimed in surprise, “What the tickets are fake? How this is possible? My tickets are all provided by the organizers.”

“Who knows if she had switched the authentic ones .”

“Well, let’s note talk about her. Kailey, did you help us to get the autograph?” Sophia asked expectantly.

Tia also looked at Kailey with joy

The reason they decided to stay behind was to wait for Kailey to give them Richard’s autograph.

Kailey felt so guilty that she dared not to look into their eyes. She stammered, “I... I’m sorry, girls. Mr. White said he doesn’t like giving autographs. Beside it was my first time collaborating with him. So I didn’t want to push it. I promise I’ll try to get them for you next time, alright?”

Sophia and Tia pouted in disappointment.

“But you have promised us” Sophia was displeased.

Kailey clenched her fists helplessly. If it wasn’t for Renea, she believes she wouldn’t have embarrassed herself in front of Richard.

Jasper approached with a bouquet to relieve the situation for Kailey. “Kailey congratulations on your successful performance.”

“Thank you, Jasper,” Kailey said as she took the bouquet.

Jasper looked at upset Sophia and Tia. He said, ‘You shouldn’t blame Kailey. It’s not like you are not aware of Richard’s aloofness. After all, that was Kailey’s first collaboration with him. Since they are not close, he naturally would not give her his autograph. There will be a penalty for opportunities for them to cooperate in the future. By then, you don’t have to worry about getting his autograph.”

“Exactly I promise I will get Richard’s autograph for you next time,” Kailey said as she held Sophia’s arm to curry favor with her.

Sophia’s anger dissipated. “Fine you must get his autograph for me next time.” She said.

“I promise!” Kailey said.

Sophia finally let the matter go after that they left while chatting joyfully.

Stella and Renea walked out of the venue.

“He is too handsome! He is just too handsome.” Stella kept repeating these words along the way.

Renea rubbed her forehead and wanted to keep a distance from Stella.

!Oh crap!” Stella let out a loud cry that broke the silence of the night.

“I forgot to ask for his autograph,” she said.

Renea smiled and asked, “Aren’t you never a fan of any celebrity?”

“Who said I’m fangirling over a celebrity. He is a majestic being.” Stella said solemnly.

Renea thought in exasperation, ‘What an exaggeration!’

‘Richard appearance is just not bad!’ She thought to herself.

“Haha!” Stella linked arms with Renea and flashed a sly smile. She said, “Renea since you have already got Richard’s contact information, how about getting me an autograph next time?”

“Having his contact information doesn’t mean I have to contact him.” Renea said.

Renea had never imagined having any further interactions with Richard. However, she didn’t know that the future was holding something big for her.

“Just in case you guys come into contact, is that alright?” Stella looked at her pleadingly.

Renea nodded exasperatedly.

Seeing that Stella exclaimed, “I know it! You are the best!” Stella gives Renea a big hug!

The collaboration between Kailey and Richard was an exceptionally honorable event for the Morris family.

Even Leon, who rarely smiled, had a cheerful expression these days.

“Honey, when are you going to announce the official debut of Kailey? Susan asked eagerly.

“Should we announce it earlier, while this is still trending topic?” she asked.

Leon nodded thoughtfully and said, “I can arrange it, but it requires a suitable occasion for a natural flow. After all, Kailey had worn a veil to conceal her identity during the performance. That might be Richard’s intentions. If we use this incident for publicity, we would have trouble explaining to Richard.”

“But people in the industry already know now.”

“Well, its just their speculation. Its not like we openly declare it. When it comes to it, we can handle any inquiries.”

Susan thought for a moment and seemed to have an idea. She quickly said, “Honey, we could use Kailey’s birthday to host a birthday party. Since it’s not that Kailey has collaborated with Richard, those industry bosses will come knocking on our doors at that time. We can highlight Kailey’s exceptional talents and announce her upcoming debut.”

“Considering out position in Z city and the collaboration between Kailey and Richard, it’s likely that the four major families of Z city will also attend. If we manage to secure their investment for Kailey’s play, wouldn’t that address out financial concerns? Would we still require Renea’s inheritance?”

Susan’s words made Leon’s eyes lit up. Even though he didn’t say anything, his expression conveyed that he approved of Susan’s idea.

The news of Kailey’s upcoming birthday party spread quickly.

Leon went even further by inviting all the esteemed magnates from Z city.

To his delight, they all accepted the invitations, indicating their intentions to attend.

The news of Kaiely’s birthday party also made the news.

“A true hair is surely know how to make a grand entrance. After her welcoming party, she hold a birthday party now.”

“Don’t be jealous. It’s her ability that counts. She could already collaborate with Richard’s right after her return. It’s possible that she will announce her debut at this birthday party.”

“What about that fake heiress? She’s still not living in the Morris family residence, Is she? Talk about thick-skinned!”

“After all, she has been raised by the Morris family for eighteen years. The Morris family can consider her like their pet dog. Its not big deal for them.”

“Why is the gap between people so vast? Kailey come from small district, yet she is well rounded in talent and virtue. On the country, Renea was raised in a prominent family for eighteen years, yet she still gives off the unruly vibe.”
