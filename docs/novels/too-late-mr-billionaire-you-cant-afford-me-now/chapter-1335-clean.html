The news of Gifford’s disappearance had set his nerves on edge.

With the Scott Group’s board meeting looming, Ferris was set to announce his retirement, officially naming Eric as his successor to lead the empire.

Gifford’s vanishing act, perfectly timed, could hardly be a coincidence. Eric’s eyes darkened as he leaned forward. “Do you remember what Gifford is capable of?”

Hadley nodded, her expression blank but her mind racing.

How could she forget?

Gifford’s shadow loomed over Eric’s past—knife attacks, orchestrated car crashes, each one a calculated strike.

The worst had left Eric with a head injury, unconscious for days, teetering on the edge of life.

“But…” Hadley’s throat felt parched, and she shook her head, grappling for clarity. “What does any of this have to do with me?”

“Hadley…” Eric’s smile was laced with bitterness. “We may be divorced, and you’ve made it clear you want nothing more to do with me. But the Scott family? They don’t see it that way. To my siblings, you and Joy are my heart and soul—the people I cherish most in this world.”
