“Absolutely, I understand completely!”

“Ha! I have complete faith in you—just wanted to emphasize the importance!”

After ending the call, Hadley slipped her phone back into her bag, her heart still racing with excitement.

Tamara approached with an umbrella, ready to escort her to the car.

“Thank you.” Hadley linked arms with Tamara, her voice warm with gratitude as they walked toward the car. But midway, she suddenly came to a halt.

Tamara glanced at her, puzzled. “What’s wrong?”

Hadley didn’t respond right away. Through the veil of rain, she spotted two familiar figures descending the left staircase and slipping indoors.

One of them was Ayla, whom she’d met only days ago. Tonight, Ayla looked completely transformed—her outfit carefully curated, her makeup impeccable, every detail speaking of preparation.

Beside her walked Abigail Lopez.

Abigail had once managed Linda, but after Linda’s exit from showbiz, she had faded from public view. Now, these two women were showing up together.

Hadley wondered—was Abigail now managing Ayla?

“Friends of yours? Want to say hi?” Tamara asked again, following Hadley’s gaze, but Ayla and Abigail had already disappeared inside.

“Just former colleagues.” Hadley smiled and shook her head, keeping things vague. “Not particularly close. Let’s keep going.”

On the ride home, Hadley’s phone rang. It was Brady.
