Hadley’s heart raced. She suddenly recalled—back then, she had initially been captivated simply by his looks. At that time, she hadn’t realized that the man she loved would become her greatest downfall.

The next morning, Hadley was still nestled in sleep when she faintly heard Eric stirring. He didn’t even turn on the light as he moved silently so as not to wake her. Once he dressed in the dark and slipped out, Hadley opened her eyes, unable to drift back to sleep.

Suddenly, a wave of resentment surged within her!

Why had he been so reluctant to be even kind to her back then, when he could be so kind and attentive now?

She swung her legs over the side of the bed, tied her long hair into a loose bun, and stepped into the bathroom, planning to head to her apartment early for breakfast with Joy.

Leaving Silver Villas, Hadley used an app to summon a car to her apartment. After arriving, she passed by a pharmacy and, after a moment’s hesitation, decided to go inside to buy a pregnancy test.

She knew the timing wasn’t ideal, and the result might not be accurate—she would still need a blood test for confirmation. She just couldn’t help but take a test first.

Deep down, she desperately wished to be pregnant.

Back at the apartment, the soft hush of the afternoon stillness lingered. Joy was still asleep, her gentle breaths barely audible from the bedroom.
