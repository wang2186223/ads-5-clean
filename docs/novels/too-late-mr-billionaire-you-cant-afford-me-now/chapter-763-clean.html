“Where to today?” Linda asked, twining her fingers with his as she leaned into his shoulder.

“Your choice,” Ernest replied, as always. “Wherever you want to go.”

“Then I’ll decide.”

Linda’s smile widened—until her fingers brushed against something small, cold, and unfamiliar.
