No, I can’t let her go. As long as I lay hold of Norton Corporation, I’m sure that she will be willing to be with me.

Seeing right through his mind, Delilah continued, “Joan is never into Larry’s money. What she loves in him is his character.”

Even though what she said was true, Dustin thought he still stood a chance to win over Joan. If I really get my hands on Norton Corporation, there is a possibility that Larry would file a divorce out of inferiority.

Fate was truly a weird thing. Problems or difficulties might come in a series, one after another, and sometimes, they could come all at once, overwhelming everyone. Even though I feel tired of pursuing her at times, it’s not the time for me to give up yet. No one knows what’s going to happen next. With such thought in mind, Dustin smiled at Delilah before bidding her goodbye.

After the camp ended, Caiden drove Joan and Lucius home. As he had some work to do at home, he headed straight back to his place after dropping them off at their house.

When Jaden saw him back home, he munched on his chips and said, “Yo, Caiden. You’re finally back. How was it? Did you have fun these two days?”

“Don’t talk to me. I’m dog-tired.” Caiden did not even bother to flick a glance at his friend and flopped onto the couch.

Sensing something was off, Jaden stood up hastily and went over to Caiden. “Hey, are you okay? Are you feeling unwell? Should I bring you to the hospital? Your parents have entrusted you to me and

asked me to take good care of you. If anything happens to you, I couldn’t bring myself to face them anymore.”

Ever since he was young, Caiden had never liked anyone mentioning his parents, so now he became even more frustrated after hearing Jaden’s words.

“Jaden, how many times have I told you already? Don’t mention them in front of me, okay? I’m really tired now,” Caiden said, turning his face away from Jaden.

Upon hearing his words, Jaden shut his mouth immediately.

After a while, he fell asleep on the couch and started snoring lightly.

Seeing that his friend had fallen asleep, Jaden did not want to bother him and went back to his room quietly

He then took his phone from the table and started scrolling through social media. As he was browsing Twitter, he suddenly saw pictures of Caiden posted by the school teachers.

“What? Is this Caiden? No way. I’ve never thought that he’s someone who likes kids.” Jaden stared incredulously at his phone and tapped on the first picture.

It was a picture of Caiden helping Lucius to change clothes, looking like a perfect dad, while Lucius smiled happily at him.

“How did he manage to stay with kids for two freaking days?” Jaden muttered to himself.

Was he out of his mind? Why did he do that?

Just then, a female voice rang from the outside. “Jaden, open the door!”

Oh no! Why is she here now? Hearing Noelle’s voice, Jaden put down the phone right away and scrambled out of his room. When he saw that Caiden was still fast asleep, he smacked Caiden’s back frantically and called, “Wake up, Caiden! Go and hide in your room. My sister is here. Hurry up!”

However, he did not bulge at all.

Left with no other choice, Jaden kicked him in the rear. “Caiden Owens!”

Finally, he snapped his eyes open and yelled angrily, “What the hell are you doing?”

“Get back to your room now. Noelle is here!”

As soon as he heard her name, Caiden sprang up to his feet, wide awake. “What? She’s here? Tell her I’m not here.” With that, he dashed up to his room.

“Caiden, let me in! I know you’re in there!” Noelle shouted, banging on the door.

When Jaden saw that Caiden was back in his room, he quickly put back the throw pillows in their original place and sprinted to the door. “Hi, Noelle. Why are you visiting again? Did you miss me? Why is my little sister so clingy?”

Noelle pushed him away from the door and stepped inside. “Go away. I’m not here for you.”

How could this girl abandon her own brother for another man?

Dejected, he knitted his eyebrows and grumbled, “What about me, Noelle? I’m the one who always treated you well. Why are you here for Caiden instead? Did he buy you any handbags or lipsticks as I did for you?”
