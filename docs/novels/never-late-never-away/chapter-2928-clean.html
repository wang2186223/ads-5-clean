Everyone was stunned upon hearing Larry's announcement, for they realized that it would cost him a heavy price to remake a new batch of clothes.

"Is Larry out of his mind? How could he even think of that?"

"Tell me about it.Who on earth manages their business like this? I bet he'll go bankrupt soon"

"Yeah.Norton Corporation is not going to last long, it seems"

The people kept gossiping about Larry's solution to the problem.

However, what they did not know was that he had been preparing for this.

Turned out, he had decided to spend his own money to re-produce the clothes.

Larry had never been someone who cared about wealth, after all.

On top of that, he believed that he would receive a good deed in return, so spending that paltry amount of money was no problem for him.

"Say, Larry.How much have you spent on this?" asked Jessica cautiously as she lightly poked his arm.

Larry turned to look at her with a serious expression.

Despite so, he had no intention of answering her question.

"Well? Why are you keeping me on edge? Answer me!"

Jessica seemed extremely anxious, for she feared that Larry had invested all of his assets into re- creating the clothes.

How is he supposed to support Joan if he'd lost everything?

"Why are you so keen to know?" questioned Larry, growing annoyed.

"Never mind that.Just answer me!"

"Stop it! Enough with your nonsense!"

Finally, Larry had reached his limit.

Why must she grumble about every single thing lately? After a long while, the crowd finally dispersed.

Even so, Jessica kept trailing behind Larry as if she fear that he would flee from her.

"Caspian, won't you stop your future wife from pestering me?" stated Larry unhappily as he fixed his gaze on the other man.

Upon hearing that, Caspian was at a loss for words.

I‘m the one that always gets bossed around by Jessica.

There's no way I would dare to stop her.

Despite that, he still approached her and gently tugged on the corner of her shirt, signaling her to control herself.

The woman only shot him an "OK" gesture as she remained silent.

Scrutinizing Larry, she could not bear it any longer, so she took her bag from the side to fish out her purse.

After that, she calmly whipped out a few cards from inside.

Hmm? What is she doing now? Lifting his head, Larry looked at Jessica curiously.

"Larry, these are all the cards you gave in the past.I'm aware that you're not expecting me to repay you, but you're in a bind now, and I can't just sit idly by doing nothing.You can use these cards as you please, and by the time you get back on your feet, only then can you repay me."

Jessica glanced at the cards she had placed on the table with a pained expression.

I think all of these should add up to a million.

Goodbye...my money.

Unable to contain himself, Larry let out a chuckle.

Caspian, on the other side, also covered his mouth to try and stifle his laugh.

What in tarnation is going on in this woman's head? Staring at the cards, Larry was beyond amused by Jessica's gesture.

How could I take back what I have gifted someone? "No need.You can take them back,"

he remarked while pushing the cards back to Jessica.

The latter was tempted to put the cards back inside her purse, but she held herself back by turning her head away, staying firm with her decisions.

"Just accept my offer, Larry.Your problems are my problems too, so you can use the money as long as...you repay me" she replied in embarrassment with her head hung low.

How could she say that to Larry when all of that was his money, to begin with? Caspian gave her a nudge in an attempt to silence her.

However, Jessica did not even bat an eye at him.

"That's enough.Don't worry, Jessica.I'm not short of money" stated Larry with a smile.

Truthfully, he did indeed lack money.However, he valued Jessica much more valuable than money itself.
