Chapter 3310 Why Have You Chosen Her

Megan’s eyes gleamed, but she put on a stern front and cast the dress she had been holding aside. Glaring at Jacinta and the shop attendant, she reprimanded, “Seriously? It seems like you’re only good at picking on your customer! Since only top-notch celebrities are allowed to enter the store, why don’t you print a memo and paste it at the entrance?”

As she was at the top of her lungs, the man at the entrance of the store heard the content of their conversation.

He approached them with a frown and asked, “What’s with the commotion? What’s going on?”

Upon another glimpse, the man noticed the ladies were none other than Megan and Jacinta.

Startled by their presence, he took a step forward and greeted Megan, “Hello, Ms. Rockford. May I know what’s going on?”

The crumpled dress could be seen on the porcelain floor, while the shop attendant started perspiring in fear. Judging by Megan’s reaction and Jacinta’s expression, he soon ruled out the possibility of them being in an intense fight.

A few seconds later, he shoot daggers at the shop attendant next to them.

“I’ll teach her a lesson immediately. On top of that, please accept the dress as a token of apology from us.”

Megan scowled at the shop attendant because the man who had shown up was the store’s operational director—Hudson. She had perused his information prior to her departure to get herself ready for the upcoming photoshoot. The information she had gathered beforehand ended up being leveraged to get herself out of the nasty situation.

“I guess I can’t really afford any of the dresses available here! Allow me to turn down the offer because I don’t want to be accused of as a thief!”

As a result of Megan’s sarcastic remark, the arrogant shop attendant next to them started shivering in fear.

“I-I’m so sorry, Ms. Rockford. A-Actually, I’m also a fan of yours—”

Hudson next to the shop attendant stopped her from defending herself and reprimanded her, “Keep the rest to yourself because I won’t forgive you even if Ms. Rockford decides to let you off the hook! You have just picked on our valued customer! In fact, we almost lost a precious opportunity to work with Ms. Rockford because of you!”

Colors drained from the shop attendant’s face when she heard Hudson’s announcement. She looked at Megan in disbelief and asked, “Sir, are you serious? She’s commissioned for the brand’s upcoming photoshoot?”

“What else could it possibly be? Should we hire you for the job instead? Hurry up and get out of our sight at once!”

Hudson had no intention to waste his time with the shop attendant anymore because he would have to deal with the store’s valued customers.

The moment the store attendant walked away, Jacinta tucked her arms and asked with a scrunched-up face, “Hudson, have I been hearing things? Have you really commissioned her for a photoshoot?”

Once Hudson regained his composure, he nodded and replied courteously, “The candid photos Ms. Rockford uploaded on her account had portrayed her unique fashion sense. Therefore, the company has made the decision to commission her for a photoshoot with a similar theme to promote the brand.”

Jacinta’s expression darkened when she heard him. Infuriated, she stepped forward and drew Megan over to her side, glaring at her in the eyes.

Megan was almost lifted up because of Jacinta’s pair of high heels

“What do you want from me?” Megan tried to shrug her off, but Jacinta didn’t even budge. Instead, she fastened her grip, rendering Megan incapable of any movement.

Jacinta ignored Megan and glared at Hudson in an attempt to intimidate him. As compared to her usual innocent image, Jacinta seemed more like a wicked witch that would devour Megan soon.

“Hudson, do you mind telling me if she’s a match for me in terms of look? Why have you chosen her over me? She’s not even famous! I don’t mind losing to Mia, but I can’t comprehend the reason she has defeated me!”
