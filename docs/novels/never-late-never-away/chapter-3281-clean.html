Chapter 3283 Threat

“You guys should be familiar with your scripts. We will be filming a running scene at the pool today and it will be quite strenuous. Remember to warm up properly,” the director briefed the group after bringing them to the pool area.

Then, he instructed the artists to get prepared while asking the production crew to get the cameras ready.

Megan looked around, observing her surroundings as well as the guest artists for the episode. While she was doing that, the scenes of Let’s Run from her past life surfaced in her mind.

The first episode of Let’s Run was very well done, fully showcasing the lively and energetic sides of the artists and it turned out to be very popular among the younger crowd.

The swimming pool episode that the crew was currently filming, which also happened to be the first episode of the program, had a similar theme.

After the guest artists were done warming up, they went into the dressing room to change into their swimming costumes. At the same time, the filming equipment had also been set up and test shoots had already begun in the natatorium.

“OK! I need everyone to stay focused. Even though we have a script, the performance will still depend on your effort,” the director said as he scanned the artists in front of him. Everyone tensed up when they saw the serious expression on his face. Actual filming was about to begin.

After filming started, Megan’s performance remained stellar throughout. Even when she was pushed into the pool by the guys or when her face got hit by props, she kept the smile on her face and did not get angry.

The men at the filming site were all impressed with Megan’s professionalism and respected her tenacity.

“Wow, Megan, I couldn’t tell that you’re so tough!”

Bruce, who was in the same team as Megan, was just a D-list artiste. During break time, he couldn’t help but strike up a conversation with Megan. As he spoke, he wasn’t able to keep his eyes off her as they glimmered with excitement.

Megan was sitting by the side of the pool and shot a glance at Bruce. However, she wasn’t very keen to talk to him.

That was because, in her past life, that man started messing around after he became famous. To her, he was the epitome of a scumbag.

“That’s nothing. I’m just being professional,” Megan replied only for the sake of their “camaraderie” as teammates.

Bruce’s interest was piqued after seeing that the woman was being so cold towards him.

Smiling, he sat down next to Megan by the side of the pool and just like her, started kicking his legs in the water casually. Only dressed in swimming trunks, the man left no gap between himself and Megan.

“Tsk, tsk! Seems like you’re pretty aloof. There are not many women who can resist looking at me.”

As Megan had her head lowered, Bruce, with his handsome face filled with curiosity, bent forward, trying to see her expression.

However, Megan did not pay attention to the man. She was not the least attracted to Bruce, who flirted with women anywhere he went, especially when she already had Jake.

“Hey, why are you ignoring me?”

Megan sat very still by the side of the pool, just like a statue. Apart from the short reply she gave him at the beginning of their conversation, she had completely ignored Bruce currently.

“You are very noisy.”

Megan, who had already lost her patience, took a side glance at the man coldly as the surrounding atmosphere suddenly turned heavy with awkwardness.

Bruce was taken aback and shifted slightly backward subconsciously. After he regained his composure, he felt a flash of irritation.

Until then, no women had dared to treat him in such a manner.

Bruce’s tanned skin glistened with water droplets from the pool as he gritted his teeth and moved an inch closer to Megan. From afar, it seemed like the two of them were rather intimate and looked like they were almost kissing.

“It seems like you’re not yet aware of the situation you’re currently in. We are at the other pool. Even if anything happened to you now, no one would be able to save you in time.”

Megan couldn’t help but turn to look at Bruce after he threatened her. She wondered where he even got the guts to offend her. Could it be that he had done the same to other women before?

Unfazed, she stood up calmly and looked down at Bruce, who was still sitting by the pool. With a sneer, she said, “How dare you threaten me? Do I look like a pushover to you?”
