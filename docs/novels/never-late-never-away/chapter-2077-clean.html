Chapter 2077

“Uh… Well, he…” Jaden hesitated as he didn’t quite know what to say.

“Caiden! Are you not home? I’m Noelle! I’m here to visit you!” Noelle shouted at the top of her lungs.

In the room, Caiden was currently lying on the bed and huddled tightly under the covers.

Dear God, may she leave quickly! He had both his hands clasped together, and his gaze was earnest. I really can’t afford to cross this girl! After all, something bad always happens every time she’s there!

He still remembered that he took swimming lessons when he was young. He stood beside the swimming pool for a long time, not daring to go in. In the next moment, Noelle kicked him into the pool, and he almost drowned back then.

During their schooldays, she always slacked off without completing her homework. When questioned by the teacher, she claimed that her brother tore them up. And the worse thing was, she said that her brother was Caiden Owens! As a result, the teacher went to his house and berated him severely…

There had been countless bizarre incidents that he never wanted such a thing to transpire again. Even for his return this time, he had considered it for a long time before he finally made up his mind.

“Caiden!” Noelle lifted her head and screeched in the living room.

At that, Caiden covered his ears and held his breath, afraid that the slightest movement would get him busted.

“Caiden Owens, if you don’t come out, I’ll storm upstairs!” Noelle roared unyieldingly.

“Good heavens! What are you doing? Caiden isn’t home, so stop yelling. It’s no use even if you scream yourself hoarse since he can’t possibly appear before you immediately.” Jaden patted her on the shoulder.

“Where did he go, then?”

“I don’t know. Who knows where he went when he’s always going and coming as he pleases?” Jaden shrugged as he spoke.

Hmm? He’s always going out? Don’t tell me he’s dating? That’s unlikely, no? Does he have a girlfriend now? No, that’s impossible! He’s mine, so how could he have possibly been stolen by some other woman?

“Jaden, answer this honestly. Does Caiden have someone else out there?” Noelle stared at the person before her coldly.

Pfft! Water spurted out of Jaden’s mouth upon hearing that.

“Jeez, can you please be more circumspect in your speech? Why did you phrase it as ‘having someone else out there?’ He’s not married, so it’s normal even if he dates,” he hastily clarified.

“No way! What would become of me if he dates?” Noelle was a tad panicked at that moment.

Well, what does the matter of him dating or otherwise has to do with her?

“That’s his freedom, okay? Noelle, tell me how you’ve been these few years. Have you gotten yourself a boyfriend?” Jaden clutched her arm.

Hah! A boyfriend? I’ve been waiting for Caiden for so many years just so that we can continue where we left off back then when he returns one day. And now, he actually has another woman out there. How dare he?

“No! Argh! I’m so pissed! Why didn’t you tell me when Caiden started dating?” Noelle plopped down onto the sofa huffily, her expression as dark as charcoal.

Is he really my biological brother when he doesn’t even tell me such an important thing?

“Gosh, what’s wrong with you? Wait… Noelle, don’t tell me you have feelings for Caiden?” Jaden abruptly turned serious.

“Are you an idiot, Jaden? I’ve had feelings for him for many years.” Noelle’s voice quivered slightly.

God, this is bad! This is really bad! What should I do now? Caiden has feelings for that senior of his, so she’s probably going to be hurt. No, I should really make things clear to her.

“Ah, just forget about it, okay? Caiden already has someone whom he likes, and he has had feelings for her for many years. So, you should just give up,” Jaden advised with a sniffle.

Instead of waiting around for Caiden to reject Noelle, it’s better for me to tell her beforehand to avoid any awkwardness between them down the road.

“Who is she? It’s that senior of his, yes?” Noelle asked out of the blue.

Huh? How does she know about Joan Watts? Jaden eyed her suspiciously.
