Chapter 3293 Oozing Feminine Charm

After her eye shadow was done, Megan turned around and remarked, “It’s alright. I’ll still have to wear the accessory for a while. By the way, did you contact the producers of the romantic variety show you helped me arrange previously?”

Megan had rejected appearing on the show. However, after Jonas’ scandal broke out, she thought that appearing on it together with him was a good way to fuel the rumors further.

It would definitely benefit Jonas’ reputation. However, she would have to worry about how Jake was going to take it.

She didn’t want to be at the receiving end of his anger again. After all, she would just be asking for trouble.

Keeping her phone, Donna nodded. “I did. But this time, they were the ones who rejected us. They were concerned about Jonas’ scandal and worried that it might affect their ratings.”

The result was within Megan’s expectation.

Now that she was the one pleading for help instead of the other way round, the scales of self-interest were no longer tipped in her favor.

“Call them again and tell them that Jonas and I will be appearing together. On top of that, we will charge a quarter of our usual fees.”

As for her terms, she wasn’t feeling confident that the producers would accept them. But, despite the fear of negative repercussions, the producers couldn’t ignore the fact that both of them were still the two most popular male and female celebrities out there.

The potential reaction from combining both of them made them an obvious choice for anyone responsible for picking the cast.

“Isn’t that too little?” Donna widened her eyes in protest. After all, negotiating fees was her job as the manager and she felt that what Megan requested was just too low.

“Don’t worry. I have no interest in the show, to begin with. I’m only doing it to help Jonas.”

Megan didn’t care about the fees at all. After checking that her makeup was done, she patted Donna on the shoulders as if to reassure her. Without another word, she prepared to go on the show.

This time, they were filming the second episode of Let’s Run. Post-editing of the first episode had been completed and was being broadcast.

Just as expected, the show was a hit with the younger generation due to the fresh themes it explored.

Megan recalled that from the second episode onward, there would be a special guest invited onto the show. However, the director had yet to inform them who it was.

“Da*n it. What a shitty day. Why do I have to run into you here?”

Just when Megan left the makeup room, she saw Mia approaching in the opposite direction. Before she could say a word, Mia had beat her to it.

After tsk-tsking softly, she cursed in her heart. Looks like she’s more of a bit*h than a woman.

“The feeling’s mutual. I felt a sense of dread since this morning itself.”

Just as she spoke, Megan ignored her and headed to the filming location. Watching her leave, Mia had the urge to pursue and continue cursing her. However, she didn’t expect Osmond to hold her back and glare coldly at her instead.

When she saw his reaction, Mia had no choice but to relent. Fuming, she stormed toward the set.

“It appears that Mia is the special guest this episode.”

Looking around, Donna saw Mia hiding in the studio by accident. Feeling unsettled, she couldn’t help but inform Megan about it.

If Mia was there with them, they would definitely not be able to complete filming in peace.

“Don’t worry. Even if she tries to do me in, I have never been afraid of her.” Just as Megan chuckled, the mocking tone in her voice caught Donna by surprise.

I have only not seen her for a week. But why do I get the feeling that she has become so much more feminine? In a blink of an eye, the feminine charm that Megan was oozing suddenly made her feel like a totally different person.

However, Megan didn’t notice Donna’s stunned expression. Sitting in her specially allocated seat, she languidly watched the crew as they prepared the filming equipment.
