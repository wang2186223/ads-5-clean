Chapter 2218

Others had always considered marriage the end of a couple’s relationship. Joan wondered if that would apply to them as well.

Nancy tapped on Joan’s shoulder and said, “You know what? You need to stop overthinking things. I have you covered!”

“Actually, you need to take good care of yourself until your child is here with us. Don’t let other things get to you, okay?”

Nancy lowered her head and caressed her tummy with happiness written all over her face. Ever since she was pregnant, she would be overjoyed for no reason, and even the little things Jory did were amusing to her.

“It’s getting late. I’ll send you home.” Joan wrapped her arm around Nancy’s and showed her the way out.

Meanwhile, Caspian was on pins and needles in the ward because of Jessica’s suggestion to have a meal together with the two women.

“Hey, I brought you some soup.” Jessica entered Caspian’s ward with a thermos flask.

When Caspian heard the woman’s voice, he jolted up from the bed.

“What about the other two? Where are they?” he asked anxiously, wondering if Jessica had mentioned anything she should have kept to herself in front of Joan.

The woman replied nonchalantly, “They made their way back after our meal.”

Is that all? What did they talk about throughout their meal? He couldn’t be bothered about Jessica finding out that Nancy used to be his spouse or even the fact that Nancy had cheated on him with another man.

The thing that concerned him the most was Joan’s condition. He was afraid Joan and Larry would get into another conflict. After all, they had been through all sorts of ups and downs before their relationship finally stabilized.

“Is there anything else you wish to share with me?” Caspian looked her in the eye and asked in a serious tone.

Huh? Is he worried about Joan? Why is he getting so worked up when she’s not his wife? Larry should be the one who’s anxious!

“What else do you want to know? It was nothing more than a simple meal. On top of that, I have asked her to leave Larry alone.”

When Caspian heard Jessica’s reply, his eyes widened in disbelief.

Is this woman out of her mind? How could she say such a thing to Joan? Oh, God! Joan is going to overthink things again!

Irked by Jessica’s attitude, Caspian reprimanded, “Why did you bring up something like this during the meal? Aren’t you aware that Larry will never leave Joan for you? So what are you attempting at?”

Stop behaving like it’s such a big deal! Most importantly, it has nothing to do with you! I don’t get it! What’s so great about that woman? She’s not even a match for me! Why won’t Larry give up on her?

Since he won’t fall in love with me, I won’t allow her to have her way either!

“That has nothing to do with you! Why don’t you mind your own business?” Jessica tried to end the topic.

Does he even know how it feels to be in love? Stop nagging if you aren’t aware of the things going on between them! If they were really in love with one another, the affection they had wouldn’t waver just because of an outsider’s baseless accusation!

Out of the blue, she asked, “Speaking of which, who’s Nancy? Are you guys close?”

Caspian closed his eyes and gasped out his reply. “I consider her a sister of mine.”

A sister of yours? Is that all? Well, since she’s already pregnant, she can’t possibly be romantically involved with Caspian, can she?

“I think she’s not half bad.”

Caspian shrugged. Duh, I have good taste in women.

Nodding, Jessica continued, “I enjoy being around a bubbly woman like her.”
