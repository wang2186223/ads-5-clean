Chapter 2324

“Take some time to really think about what I said.”

Delilah then walked out of the living room while Larry slumped against the sofa and fell into deep thought.

Am I really in the wrong here? Must I be the first one to apologize?

“Ms. Young, come take a look at this! Will this do?” Joan called out to Delilah in the garden.

“Yeah, that’ll do fine! Keep up the good work, and you’ll probably be a master at this!” Delilah replied with a smile.

Joan had been thinking of becoming a florist, so she felt excited when Delilah complimented her.

“I’ve sent Larry the divorce agreement,” Delilah brought it up all of a sudden.

Joan froze for a moment, but she quickly regained her composure and continued watering the flowers.

“I see,” she mumbled.

Why do I feel like I’m starting to regret this? Was it a mistake to have agreed to the divorce Ms. Young suggested? Hmm… Screw it! Maybe my relationship with Larry really has come to an end, after all…

With that in mind, she dusted the dirt off her clothes and carried on with what she was doing.

“Have you ever regretted your decision?” Delilah asked.

Of course, I have! But, what good would that do? Maybe we simply aren’t fated to be together, so why should I regret letting go?

“Nope, I trust you!” Joan replied with a smile.

Delilah’s plan was simple. If Larry truly cared about Joan, he would do everything in his power to keep her by his side. If he didn’t, then a divorce would still be the best solution anyway.

“Look, Ms. Young! This flower is amazing! I thought it was done for when it began to wilt the other day, but it’s blooming now!” Joan exclaimed.

Larry had never expected things to take such a complicated turn.

“Larry, they’ve already sold him their shares, so you and Dustin are evenly matched right now. As of the moment, there’s still one remaining shareholder who has yet to transfer his shares. We have to get it before Dustin does, or Norton Corporation will be lost!” Caspian told Larry anxiously, truly feeling afraid for the first time.

“But no one has ever seen that shareholder, right?” Larry asked while staring out the window with a depressed look on his face.

There’s a mysterious shareholder in Norton Corporation that even Dad had never met in his entire life. Norton Corporation urgently required a huge amount of capital for its inception. That man wrote Dad a letter expressing his willingness to lend a helping hand, and they never contacted each other again after that…

With that thought in mind, Larry slumped against the sofa and sipped on his tea. He hadn’t slept well in a few weeks, and it pained Caspian to see Larry in such a haggard state, but the former didn’t know how to comfort him.

“Hey, how’s it going? Has everything been taken care of?” Jessica asked as she came in through the door.

She knew quite a lot about what was going on in Norton Corporation, so she was able to understand how Larry felt and came to check on him.

“Shh!”

Caspian motioned at her to keep quiet. Things were complicated enough as it is.

Jessica slowly sat down beside Larry and shot him a helpless look.

“Larry, let me know if there’s anything I can do to help out. I will be sure to give it my all!”

Caspian smacked his lips in disdain and turned away when he heard that.

Please… What can you possibly do to help? It’ll be a miracle if you don’t cause us any more trouble!

Jessica gave Caspian a nudge as she shouted at him, “Hey! What’s with that attitude of yours? I’m very serious when it comes to getting things done!”
