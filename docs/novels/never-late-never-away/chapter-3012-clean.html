Chapter 3012

"What's there to tell? I'm no outsider, so I'm free to come whenever I want!" Nancy said with a shrug.

Everyone burst out laughing at that moment.

It was this character of Nancy's that drew Jessica to her.She wasn't pretentious, she was carefree, and most importantly, she wasn't scheming like other women.

Jessica felt truly happy and comfortable around her.

After cleaning up and tidying the house for a while, they finally stopped to take a well-deserved break.

"Thank you so much for all your help" Jessica said as she plopped herself down on the sofa.

"Oh, don't say that! You know we'd do anything for you; Nancy immediately replied.

"Alright, alright.Tell you what, I shall cook for everyone tonight!"

With that, Jessica made a beeline for the kitchen.

Nancy's eyes widened in shock at what she had just heard.

What? Jessica cooks? Joan, like Nancy, was also staring at Jessica in bewilderment.

But what surprised her more was how Larry didn't even bat an eyelid to all of this.

Joan looked at him skeptically.

Does he already know that Jessica can cook? In actuality, it was perfectly normal that Jessica knew how to cook.

She had been with Caspian for years, so it wasn't surprising if she had learned to cook for him.

Everyone enjoyed themselves over the hearty, delicious meal, and after a bit of chit-chat, they soon took their leave.

On their way home, Joan couldn't stop nagging about how worried she was for Jessica, and Larry could only listen on in amusement.

Eventually, he had to coax her in order to calm her down.

"Okay, okay.Stop nagging.You know how Jessica's like.She isn't one to be messed with, and she definitely won't let herself be bullied."

Joan knew Larry had a point.

Jessica was undoubtedly a tough woman, but the problems she faced were just way too many and complex.

Besides, she was all too aware that with a character like Jessica's, she wouldn't be able to get over Caspian for some time.

They had been so madly in love before, and now that they had broken up, there was no doubt both of them would be nursing the wound over a period of time.

Because in any relationship, the more one had invested in it, the more they stood to lose when it turned sour.

Joan turned away and gazed out of the window.

She found it a pity that their relationship couldn't come to fruition, but she felt even worse because she couldn't do anything to help.

Perhaps that's just the complexity of human nature.It's something that we'll never fully understand.

"Don't worry so much.You can visit and chat with Jessica whenever you're free, so it helps keep her mind off Caspian" Larry said.

Joan nodded in silence.She won't be able to deal with this alone.She needs me now more than ever, and I shall be there for her.

Unbeknownst to them, instead of heading home, Nancy had met up with Caspian at a café.

After everything he had done to Jessica, Nancy was very disappointed with him.

Caspian had always come across as somewhat of a goof, so she never expected he would be capable of doing something as horrible as this.

"What on earth were you thinking? Are you going to dump Jessica just like that?"

Caspian's expression darkened as soon as those words left Nancy's mouth.

I love Jessica.I don't want to leave her either, but what other choice do I have? Caspian lowered his head, guilt written on his face.

"That's the only thing I can do, Nancy.I hope you understand.That woman is carrying my child" Caspian muttered.

Nancy scoffed.

His child? How can he even be sure that he fathered the child? Nancy had always known that Caspian was too gullible and stubborn for his own good.

Just because that woman claimed to have lost her virginity to him, he believed her with no questions asked.

The thing was that the woman's story could have been easily formed with the help of advanced medical technology.
