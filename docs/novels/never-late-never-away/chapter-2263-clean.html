Chapter 2263

Why is he still mad? It’s already been days, and I’ve told him that I didn’t do it on purpose.

The woman scratched the back of her head, feeling frustrated.

“Hey, Caspian. Do you wanna hear a joke?” she said.

“I’m reading a magazine,” he answered without hesitation.

This was the first time Caspian had rejected her.

In truth, however, he wasn’t feeling good about it either. Yet, whenever he thought of the kiss from the news, anger burned in him. To make matters worse, the man who kissed Jessica was none other than Larry.

“How long more are you going to stay mad at me? I already admitted to my mistake, so why do you still act as though I owe you eight million?”

Finally, she snapped.

“It’s only five million at most,” the man retorted.

Jessica couldn’t control herself and burst out giggling. She didn’t expect him to be so humorous.

“It’s already been a few days. Why are you still mad?” she asked as she patted his shoulder.

“Really? Was it that obvious?” Caspian asked as he stared at the woman before him.

Duh! You’re always pulling a long face. What else could you be feeling if not mad?

“Alright. Don’t be angry anymore. It was just an accident,” she insisted.

Of course, that’s what both Larry and Jessica think, but not for some people. Caspian sighed at the thought of it.

“What is it this time?” Jessica quickly asked.

“You better explain what actually happened to Joan for the sake of Larry. I’m sure both of them are still in a fight,” he told her softly.

No way… Larry and Joan have such a strong bond. How is it possible that there isn’t even the least bit of trust between them?

“Oh, please. Their relationship’s indestructible. Besides, they’re married. Why would Joan be doubtful of Larry all because of a piece of entertainment news?” Jessica mumbled, thinking that Caspian must be overthinking it.

Caspian actually understood her quite well. Sometimes, she might act as though she didn’t care about something, but in truth, it was quite the opposite.

“Are you a woman?” he asked.

What kind of question is this? Jessica’s expression turned ugly. Can’t he tell if I’m a woman?

“How can you not know how a woman’s mind works? Could it be that you haven’t been in a relationship before? But that doesn’t make sense. There should be a lot of men pursuing a woman like you. Were you never jealous when you were in your past relationships?” he asked.

Since when did he become so nosy? Jessica turned around, refusing to answer his question.

“Alright, then. Just stay away from Larry in the future,” Caspian deliberately changed the topic.

“What do you want to eat? I’ll go get it for you.” The woman didn’t want to continue the conversation anymore. No way. I’m still waiting for Larry to make a comeback.

“Anything is fine.”

Anything again? Jessica’s eyes dimmed. It was all because they ate at a random restaurant the other day that she ended up drunk and did such an embarrassing thing.

Caspian was the only one left in the ward. He had his phone in hand and was hesitating to make a call. However, he ended up dialing the number anyway.

“What is it?” The familiar voice sounded sweet as always, and the corner of his lips tilted upwards upon hearing it.

I guess she’s doing well. She must be living a happy life with Jory now.

“Um, you should go visit Joan if you have the time,” he said.

What’s going on? What happened to Joan? Did she fight with Larry again?

“What happened? Spit it out!” Nancy ordered firmly.

As expected, she didn’t read the news.

“What? He cheated on her? How is it possible? Larry would never do something like that. These reporters are so full of crap!” the woman couldn’t help growling.
