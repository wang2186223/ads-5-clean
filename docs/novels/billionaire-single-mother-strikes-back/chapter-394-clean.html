Chapter 396

"Alright."

Nathaniel needed to go to the office.

He had spent over two weeks at Rosewood Manor with Isabella, only handling meetings remotely.

Dominic drove Isabella and Oliver back to the manor first.

Then he took Nathaniel to Prestige Builders.

Isabella entered the villa with Oliver.

She saw Benjamin and Victoria sitting on the sofa.

Victoria was speaking to Benjamin in a soft, soothing tone.

Benjamin was nodding diligently at her words.

Isabella frowned.

She looked down at Oliver. "Momo, go upstairs first."

"I want to stay with you, Mommy! I'm a big man. I can protect you," Oliver insisted.

He stepped in front of Isabella, glaring at Victoria.

Victoria's face stiffened.

Had she become a villain in this young master's eyes?

Isabella must have poisoned his mind against her!

She must have been bad-mouthing her behind her back!

Victoria suppressed her anger.

She forced a faint smile. "Oh, Oliver is here too. Auntie didn't know you were visiting. I didn't bring a gift. Don't worry, I'm your Mommy's sister. I won't harm you."

"Hah! Can you even believe your own words? Your voice is so fake and annoying! Shut up! Leave now!" Oliver snapped impatiently.

Victoria's face froze completely.

Her words got stuck in her throat.

Did he just speak to her like that?

He called her...

Isabella didn't want Oliver involved in adult matters.

She gently ruffled his hair. "Momo, be good. Go prepare the rock-climbing gear. Give me five minutes to make her leave, okay?"

"Okay, then," Oliver muttered, pouting.

He still shot a scornful look at Victoria. "Listen, you bad woman! I'm warning you! Don't hurt my Mommy, or my Daddy will make you pay!"

Then he ran upstairs.

Victoria's expression shifted between shock and humiliation.

She was once the top supermodel.

Everyone used to respect and admire her.

Now even a child despised her!

How could a child say such cruel things?

It had to be Isabella.

Isabella must have slandered her behind her back.

She must have ruined her reputation.

Victoria wanted to tear that pretty face to shreds.

But Benjamin was here.

She could only glare at Isabella with reddened eyes.

"Isabella, I've already apologized. Can't you forgive me? Why are you turning a child against me?"

"Hah! Slander? Are you even worth my time?" Isabella rolled her eyes.

She turned her cold gaze to Benjamin. "This is my home. Why did you let a stray animal in without my permission?"

"She's not a stray animal! She's our sister!" Benjamin insisted. "Don't forget how she always protected you when we were kids! Why are you two fighting over a man?!"

"Over a man? Benjamin, let's get something straight," Isabella scoffed.

She pulled out her phone and played a video clip.

The footage of Victoria and Alexander in bed appeared instantly.

Victoria's moans filled the room. "Alexander darling, am I prettier? Or is Isabella prettier? Do you like me or her?"

"Alexander darling, I'm just scared you'll fall for her. I'm scared her money and looks will tempt you. I'm terrified you'll stop loving me..."

"Will you still take her assets for me? Will you divorce her and marry me?"

Every word was fake yet seductive.

Her expression in the video was completely different from the pure, innocent face she wore now.

Benjamin jolted.

It was easy to forget how vicious Victoria could be.

Especially when the Victoria standing before them seemed so gentle and kind.

Who would believe she had this side?

Until Isabella showed the video again.
