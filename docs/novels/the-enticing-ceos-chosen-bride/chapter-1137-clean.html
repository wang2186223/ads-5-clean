Because no one could catch his attention, let alone provoke his boundaries. He hadn't even considered that he needed self-control.

However, every time he was made acutely aware of his need for self-control, it was because of one person.

Now, every moment, he felt his self-control being depleted.

Even he didn't know what else that woman could do to completely shatter his patience.
