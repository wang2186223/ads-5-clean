“What about Nathan's?"

"Also no.

Granny Alyssa placed the document on the coffee table. "It seems you already know, so I won't say much. Take these 30% shares"

How should she describe Presley? Did he not want to let go of Granny Alyssa? So he had been delaying things until now?

But he didn't offer any defense, directly agreeing to the divorce in court. Then, under everyone's gaze, he gave the grandmother a significant amount, including the Harper Group.

It sounded generous, but in reality he was just using the divorce and Granny Alyssa to return the Harper family business to Damon in this way.

He wouldn't yield to her, nor would he apologize to Damon. But he still handed the Harper family business to Damon according to the plan.

Damon's abilities were beyond question, especially as he was now the CEO of the Obsidian Group. If the Harper family business returned to him, what would its future be like? It was hard for anyone to imagine.

The Harper family business was more important to Presley than anything else. Even in the divorce, he had to ensure a solid future for the Harper Group.

Perhaps she was overthinking. Maybe she was making people out to be too evil.

From another perspective, Robin was no longer capable of managing the Harper family business and Presley ultimately lacked the ability to manage the Harper family business. Apart from returning it to Damon, he really had no other choice.

The Harper family business was his life's work, the pride and symbol of the Harper family through the generations. No one would want to see the Harper family business collapse, and it hadn't come to that point yet.

“He's quite cunning, stirring up all this trouble. He was the one who forcibly took the company away, and now he wants to dump this mess on me. How generous of me to just forget past grievances and do as he wishes?"

Granny Alyssa smirked, "Yes, he’s clever. That's why he gave all these assets to me. If you were me, how would you handle it? Fine, if you don't want them, I'll manage them. At worst, I'll live a few years less, but at least I can leave something for my great-grandchildren."

Even though she didn't approve of Presley's ways, she couldn't deny the importance of the Harper farnily business.

30% of the shares were enough to ensure a comfortable life for her two beloved great- grandchildren. A few years of hard work was absolutely worth it.

Damon pursed his lips, his expression not too good. Granny Alyssa's words were clearly meant for him to hear.

Living a few years less? How could he let her live a few years less?

Although he didn't look happy, he leaned back on the sofa, not saying a word, but gave a light cold snort.

Alyssa on the other side, looking at the document in front of her, sighed deeply, showing her helplessness.

Chloe looked on, particularly moved by Granny Alyssa's words. Seeing the stalemate between the two generations, she glanced at the still silent Damon and couldn't help but say to Granny Alyssa, "Granny, don't be upset."

Taken aback, Chloe opened her mouth but didn't speak.

“You better not.."

“Shut up. You don't want it, and you are stopping others from taking it? What do you think you're doing? Trying to be your grandfather?"

"Chloe, my dear, do you really want your granny to manage the company? I want to live a few more years and take care of my great-grandchildren. I'm getting old. I don’t have many years left."

Alyssa’s face lit up as she looked at Chloe with even more anticipation.

Alyssa's face lit up, “Good, let's do that."

Chloe fell silent. Even though she knew it was a trap, she jumped in willingly. It was not something you saw every day.

But then again, any normal woman would probably jump at the chance.

You've got to admit, Alyssa was cunning. Giving Chloe the shares was probably no different than giving them to Damon.
