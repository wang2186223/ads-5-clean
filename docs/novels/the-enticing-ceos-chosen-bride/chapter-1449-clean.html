Robin's face was as grey as a bad weather. What the heck was Ava doing? Did she make a solemn promise just to let Queen Julia successfully discover Chloe's identity and make it public?

There was nothing more ironic than the current situation.

Robin just got his shares, not even for a minute, and the shareholders under him were selling off their shares right in front of him, reducing their value to nothing, while completely humiliating him.

Percy didn't see this twist coming. He glanced at Robin's gloomy face and shouted at the shareholders planning to transfer their shares, "Are you guys nuts? Even if the Harper Group ends up in Nathan's hands, isn't it still the Harper family's company? Is the Harper Group that easy to take down?"

The shareholders scoffed, "Our shares aren't that much. It won't affect you guys, no need to get all hot under the collar. But I have to say, to us, the real Harper Group is the one with Damon, the rest... forget it."

"If you don't believe it, just wait and see. Let's see how other partnering companies react when they find out the Harper Group has a new CEO. Sorry, we can't gamble with you. And, Nathan, our shares are ready for you to buy anytime, just name your price."

Nathan sighed, casually leaning back in his chair, "Why is everything on me? If the Harper Group is going belly up, and I don't even know who to give my shares to. I don't want your shares. Ah, Robin, Percy, do you want them? As long as you can afford it, all the shares Damon and I have are yours."

Percy's eyes lit up for a moment, but not long after, his expression dimmed. Getting shares was great, but they had more than 40% of the shares, he couldn't afford it.

This wasn't something a few hundred million or a few billion could solve. This was priceless.

Damon casually handed over such a huge asset to Nathan, what a joke.

The shareholders departed one by one in anger, leaving only Robin and a few shareholders standing there, exchanging uncertain glances, torn between decisions.

With an emotionless face, Damon pushed Yasmine towards the door of the conference room.

Kane's voice gradually faded away. There were only a few people left in the meeting room, yet his voice remained clear.

What a joke.

Presley, Robin, and Percy didn't know who Thanos was, but just from hearing the first part of what Nathan said, they knew it was dripping with irony.

However, Nathan's laughter didn't last long. He walked to the door, and the smile on his face disappeared, replaced by a chilling voice devoid of warmth. "You didn't feel an ounce of regret for my parents. Presley, Damon has given you too many chances. His guilt towards Chloe, well, maybe

you will never get his struggles. It seems that what you considered to be the finest in Damon was ultimately just a heartless workhorse you crafted for the family's interests.

Our parents are still missing, Damon doesn't have a say in his own marriage, the family is falling apart, and Chloe keeps bearing the burden.... All of these are the consequences of the family's power.

"You've completely exhausted Damon's patience."

Nathan finished speaking and he left without any hesitation.

The conference room screen was still broadcasting the news of Queen Julia planning to hold a coronation ceremony for Chloe on Christmas. Presley's gaze stayed on the screen, but Nathan's words were deeply engraved in his heart.

"Don't doubt Damon's determination... He's given you too many chances."

His body swayed a bit, and the cane in his hand dropped to the floor with a thud.
