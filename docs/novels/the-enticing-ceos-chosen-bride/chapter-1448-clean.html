Chapter 1448

"But God still had mercy on me. Just yesterday, I received a massive surprise. I learned that thirty- six years after I lost my daughter Jasmine, an angel came to me to tell me that my Jasmine was actually alive...

"Chloe, come here..."

The moment Queen Julia finished speaking, the entire conference room fell silent.

Everyone was staring intently at the screen, even holding their breath. They were all wondering if they had misheard the Chloe that Queen Julia had just mentioned.

However, when a tall and slender figure in a stunning evening gown stepped out of the shadows and into the light, and that familiar and beautiful face appeared in everyone's view, not only did the atmosphere on the screen become silent, but everyone in the conference room couldn't help but gasp.

"I shouldn't be saying so much at this banquet, but I'm too happy and I want to share this happiest moment of my life with you all, with everyone... Yes, Chloe, her name is Chloe. She is the biological daughter of my daughter Jasmine. She is my own granddaughter, the true and only princess of the Y Country royal family."

Queen Julia's words left no room for anyone to refute.

The conference room was silent for a few seconds and then suddenly erupted into chaos.

"Holy cow! Ms. Summers is a real princess?!"

"Weren't people saying she was a fraud just after the state banquet ended yesterday? How did she become a princess overnight?!"

"Queen Julia herself announced it, so it must be true."

"So the Jasmine Queen Julia was talking about is..."

Everyone's gaze shifted to Yasmine. Some people were staring at her in shock, looking very surprised.

"So you are Queen Julia's biological daughter!"

Presley's face turned pale, and he stared at the TV screen, his hand gripping the cane so tightly that his knuckles turned white.

How could this be?!

Chloe, who was constantly bullied and ignored in the Summers family, was actually a princess of the Y Country? That was insane.

Some directors who supported Robin nervously chuckled, seeming somewhat uneasy. "How could that be possible? If Chloe were really a princess, she would have used this identity to her advantage when Presley first tried to stop her from being with Mr. Harper. Why wait until now to reveal it? Is it just to have a princess title to outshine Ms. Wendy?! Haha, this Ms. Summers is really something, even daring to deceive the royal family."

But the applause and congratulatory voices coming from the TV made the speaker increasingly unsure of himself.

"No! This isn't true! Chloe is a fraud. She's a liar!"

A sharp voice suddenly rang out. On the screen, Wendy was sitting in a wheelchair, her face pale and twisted with anger, pointing at Chloe and screaming. "You've all been deceived by her! She's a fraud! She's from the Summers family in P City, and everyone in the Summers family ignores her. How could she possibly be related to the royal family of Y Country?

"Barbara, is she a fraud? Ava, tell everyone that Chloe is a complete fraud!

"Speak up! Didn't you say that Queen Julia's biological daughter Jasmine was dead? How could a dead person have children? So there's no need to think about it, Chloe is a liar!!!"

Everyone was accusing Wendy of being crazy. She was out of control, completely disregarding her image. In such a gathering of celebrities, Wendy was lifted up by several people and thrown out of the banquet hall. The scene was simply unbearable to watch.

Presley's face was indescribable at this moment. His body swayed and he nearly lost his balance.

Despite this, his gaze remained fixed on the TV screen, as if waiting for something.

"I've caused you all some trouble. This time, besides sharing my greatest joy in life with you all, I also want to announce something."

Presley saw the confused look in Chloe's eyes as she looked at Queen Julia, and he also saw Julia take a deep breath and say, "My daughter's birthday is on Christmas every year. I'm glad that I still have a chance to celebrate my daughter's birthday in my lifetime. There are still more than two months until Christmas, and this time I want to give her a proper birthday celebration.

"Then, on her birthday, I will also hold a coronation ceremony for Jasmine's favorite daughter, my beloved granddaughter Chloe. I hope my daughter and granddaughter will receive the attention and blessings of people all over the world."

The screen ended there, followed by a new round of broadcasting, mixed with excited voices of reporters.

The entire conference room seemed to have fallen into a deathly silence, everyone was so shocked they couldn't speak.

Nate's mouth was wide open, his chin almost dropped to the floor. What happened? What was going on? Wasn't Mrs. Harper invited to perform at the state banquet?

It was shocking enough that Mrs. Harper was mistaken for a fraud yesterday, but now overnight she had suddenly become the princess of the Y Country royal family.

"This change is too sudden. How did Ms. Summers become the princess of the royal family overnight."

"The coronation ceremony on Christmas, this is an important ceremony for her to officially return to the royal family and receive recognition."

"I remember that Princess Ava and her two daughters never had a coronation ceremony, right?"

...

Some people had already come to their senses and couldn't help but sneer at these words.

"Do they deserve to have a coronation ceremony? Princess Ava is only Queen Julia's adopted daughter. They should be grateful for having lived in luxury for so many years, and yet they still want a coronation ceremony? That's too much."

Presley's body swayed for a moment, and despite his shrewd yet feigned composure, his gazes still betrayed a hint of disbelief. The words that entered his ears were full of sarcasm.

"What's the big deal about the Alonso family now? Remember how Ms. Wendy used to strut around at the board meetings, all high and mighty? Now she's been kicked out of a royal banquet in Y Country, making a total fool of herself. What a disgrace."

"You've got that right. Even a fool wouldn't want to be with a woman like that. And she's still after Mr. Harper, clinging to him like a leech. The thought of living with such a woman, it's like a living hell."

"Presley, any words now? Your golden girl, Ms. Wendy, has just been booted from the royal banquet in Y Country. And her support, Princess Ava, turns out to be nothing more than the Queen's adopted daughter. Meanwhile, Ms. Summers, who you've always looked down on, is the real princess. It's hilarious. All this fuss to marry Damon to an unworthy woman, and now the company is in other people's hands..."

"This has got to be the funniest thing I've ever seen in my life."

Some of the directors just gave up. After all, Damon had already announced his intention to destroy the Harper Group. Now with his wife Chloe being the princess of Y Country, two smart and powerful people working together to bring down the Harper Group seemed like a piece of cake, didn't it?!

Presley stood there for a long time, frozen. This was the most ironic thing he'd ever seen in his life. He'd lived carefully all his life, accumulating wealth and power, only to end up the laughingstock in other people's eyes.
