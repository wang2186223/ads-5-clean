Chapter 1603

Royce nodded, neither confirming nor denying Chloe’s assessment. He knew all too well how long Robin had been suppressing his ambitions. To finally move for control of the company, Robin had clearly been willing to weave a tangled web.

“If you want to follow this path, then go ahead,” Royce said, his voice steady. “If something goes wrong, someone will surely be there to take the fall for you.”

Chloe gave a slight smile and a nod. The "someone" he was referring to was, of course, Damon. It seemed Damon had inherited more than just his father’s business acumen; he had inherited the Harper penchant for spoiling the women they loved. It appeared father and son would have plenty to bond over in the future.

“…What are you two blabbering about? Move aside, move aside!”

Elizabeth had been waiting to interrupt for minutes but hadn't found the right opening. Seeing a brief gap in the conversation, she practically shoved Royce out of the frame.

“Chloe and I are the perfect duo right now, okay? Why are you hogging all her time?” Elizabeth turned her focus back to the screen, her expression softening instantly. “Chloe, darling, you must take good care of yourself and my chubby grandson. Oh, that little boy is going to be my lucky star!”

Elizabeth was clearly annoyed with Royce for monopolizing their daughter-in-law. Her warmth was infectious, making Chloe’s heart swell with affection. It was no wonder Royce adored his wife so much; Elizabeth was so kind and lively that even Chloe found herself wanting to spoil the older woman.

“I will take good care of myself, I promise,” Chloe reassured her softly.

Elizabeth nodded with a satisfied smile. Then, she glanced over her shoulder. “Hey, why are you still here? I want to chat with my daughter-in-law in peace!”

“It’s time for breakfast,” Royce’s deep voice echoed from just out of sight.

“I don’t want to eat. I’m busy!”

“Eat first, chat later.”

“No!”

“Elizabeth!”

“You dare to scold me?!” Elizabeth gasped, turning to the camera with a look of mock betrayal. “Chloe, look at him. He bullies me just because he’s handsome!”

Chloe was both amused and confused. Was that an accusation or a compliment? Royce seemed to be at his wits' end with his whimsical wife. Elizabeth’s "crying" was clearly fake, and her anger was nonexistent, but even her playful nagging was a force he had never learned to restrain. In her hands, the sophisticated Royce Harper was truly helpless.

“I didn’t scold you, sweetheart,” Royce sighed. “Let’s just have breakfast.”

“But I want to stay here,” Elizabeth grumbled.

Chloe was about to intervene when she heard Royce’s voice filled with absolute resignation. “Fine. I’ll bring the breakfast over to you.”

Elizabeth beamed instantly. “Okay! I’ll wait for you.” Without a hint of her previous "anger," she leaned over and kissed Royce on the cheek.

“Who are you chatting with? Hmm?”

Chloe was still blushing at the display of affection on her screen when she was startled by a familiar, husky voice right against her ear. Elizabeth and Royce, still on the video call, watched as Chloe turned her head, only to be playfully pushed down into the bed covers. Damon’s tall figure loomed over hers as he began to kiss her deeply, oblivious to the audience.

Elizabeth’s mouth dropped open, her eyes going wide and round. The scene lasted only a few seconds before the screen flickered and went dark. Whether Chloe had deliberately covered the lens or simply dropped the phone in her surprise, the visual was gone—but the audio was very much alive.

Elizabeth blinked uncomfortably, but her curiosity quickly overrode her sense of propriety. She strained her ears, leaning into the phone.

“No... Damon...” Chloe’s breathing was short and suppressed, her voice a low, melodic rejection. However, the rustle of silk sheets and the unmistakable sound of soft kisses continued to filter through the speaker.

“Damon!” Chloe suddenly gasped, her voice laced with urgency.

The nightgown she was wearing had been Damon’s "gift"—a piece of handiwork designed with a specific purpose. His excuse at the time was that if he "couldn't eat," he should at least be allowed to "touch." Consequently, the gown was dangerously short with a neckline that barely clung to her shoulders.

Right now, Damon’s warm lips were pressed against the edge of that neckline. One strap had already slipped down her arm, leaving her skin exposed. Chloe clenched her teeth, terrified he would grow even bolder while the call was still active.

Hearing Chloe’s shout, Elizabeth finally snapped out of her shock. She shouted at the screen, “Damon! Damon Harper! You better be careful! Your wife has my grandson in her belly! Don’t you dare go all the way!”

Royce, standing to the side, rubbed his forehead in sheer embarrassment. What on earth was he supposed to do with this woman? Couldn’t she see that her daughter-in-law was likely dying of shame?

At the sound of his mother’s voice, Damon finally pulled back. Chloe bit her lip, her eyes closed as she tried to steady her ragged breathing. Elizabeth’s warnings were still echoing through the room. Chloe felt like she could spontaneously combust from the embarrassment.

Damon propped himself up on one hand, using his slender fingers to pull her strap back into place and smooth her tousled hair. He looked at her flushed face and moist lips, a slight, victorious smile on his face.

Chloe stared at him wide-eyed before punching him playfully on the shoulder. Damon chuckled, caught her hand, and kissed her knuckles. “How many times have I told you?” he whispered, his voice sending shivers down her spine. “You scratch like a little cat.”

“You punk!” Elizabeth’s voice crackled through the phone again. “You stop right now, do you hear me? If you hurt my precious grandson, I swear I’ll have your head!”

Chloe sighed, the moment a ludicrous mix of mortification and humor. Damon, pressing his thin lips together, looked uncharacteristically grim at the interruption.
