“Hey boss, how's Nathan doing? Haven't heard from him lately, has he gone into hibernation or did you beat him to a pulp?"

-b2 Inside the CEO's office of Harper Group, Robin's assistant was reporting the reply he just received.

“Mr. Robin, Obsidian Group has agreed to a video conference with you. If you agree, we can connect immediately."

Robin paused his signing pen, lifted his head and looked at him with a furrowed brow. "A video conference?"

The assistant nodded, "Yes, as far as I know, this has never happened before. Obsidian has never formally agreed to any form of invitation."

With a "thud", Robin suddenly threw the pen onto the table. The assistant's words came to a halt, looking at him somewhat puzzled. Robin's face was cold, and he spoke indifferently, "So I should thank them for giving me such an honor?"

The assistant opened his mouth, but didn't speak for a moment.

Robin chuckled, "A video conference, how grand."

The office fell silent for a while. The assistant was always watching the time.

After a few minutes, he had to ask carefully: "Mr. Robin, if you disagree, I can reply to them now."

Robin's face darkened even more, “Connect it."

The assistant breathed a sigh of relief, "Yes, I'll arrange it right away."

A few minutes later, the video call was successfully connected.

Damon was sitting in his office chair, reviewing the files of the past few months. Right in front of him was a large projection wall. At the moment, it was projecting Robin sitting in his office. Colorado was sitting on the sofa by the window of the office, his body facing the projection wall, but in the video, only half of his face was shown.

The angle seemed to be adjusted by him, claiming he needed to feel a sense of mystery.

“lL wonder why Mr. Robin is so eager to contact us, is there something important to discuss?" The voice of Colorado was completely different from just now. His face was cold, his voice was low, and he was not talkative, giving a feeling that it was hard to tell who the real him was.

His indifference deepened Robin's already unpleasant expression. Looking at his profile and listening to his voice, it was obviously a young man. As an elder before him, this attitude made him have no favorable impression of the man on the video.

What really made him feel annoyed was that the CEO of the Obsidian Group, who suddenly came out and suppressed the Harper family for several years, turned out to be such a young man. If Pesley knew, he would probably thrown a fit.

“Are you the person in charge of Obsidian Group?"

"I think Mr. Robin is so eager to invite, there must be something important.

Mr. Robin, you can feel free to speak up your mind."

Robin slowly closed his eyes, staring at Colorado's profile, his cold gaze almost piercing Colorado's face.

The silence was obvious, but Colorado didn't move at all. Time was lost in silence, but Colorado was bold enough. After all, Damon was by his side. He was forced to be here, not voluntarily. Moreover, the one who insisted on meeting was the Harper family, not him. Agreeing to meet Robin was already a great honor.

Robin seemed to understand that this conversation was initiated by him, and what he cared about was not whether the CEO was young or not likable.

What he cared about was the Obsidian Group in the other party's hands.

Naturally, the one who should take the initiative to speak was him.

"I've long heard of the name of Obsidian, but I've always been curious. In the past, we never crossed paths. Why has your group been targeting the Harper family recently?"

Colorado smirked, "Because of a wo..."

"Bang——"

Before he finished speaking, Colorado's head suddenly fell forward. Then Robin saw him reaching out to hold the back of his head and rubbing it hard.

Staring at the silver mouse that rolled in front of him and still spinning, Colorado almost jumped up with anger.

“What happened?"

The incident happened so quickly and without any warning. Robin didn't know what had happened at all. He only saw the man in the video suddenly disappear from the frame, with only half of his head at the bottom of the video, faintly visible.

After a long while, Colorado supported himself and resumed his previous posture. “Nothing. The reason is simple. A capitalist should always put profit first. When the money here is almost earned, you should go to develop new projects. The circle is not big, and I personally believe that there really isn't much to be puzzled about."

Robin smirked, "I may not know what you're saying, but your group's targeting is too obvious:"

“Well, that’s probably because all the money-making projects are in your hands." Colorado was still rubbing the back of his head, and for a moment he resumed his casual posture.

Robin frowned, "The economy of the European Union is mostly controlled by the Harper family. I think anyone with brains will definitely not clash with the Harper family"

Colorado smirked, "Because they have no power. The facts prove that we are much better than those with brains, aren't we? At least the business we want is now in our hands. Mr. Robin, just be straightforward about why you invited us this time. Is it to warn us, or to come and blame us?"

Robin's expression was not looking good; a conversation was actually dominated by someone else, making him feel pressured. He raised an eyebrow, pushing down the heavy annoyance brewing within him, and spoke again in a seemingly amiable tone.

"This isn't a warning, nor is it a punitive expedition. Since it's all about the benefits, wouldn't you agree that collaborating with us, rather than clashing with the Harper family, would be a win-win situation?"

Upon hearing this, Colorado blinked, his back involuntarily stiffening.

Robin looked at his reaction through the screen. Even though it was still a profile view, he could tell that his proposal had clearly elicited a strong response from the other side. A slow smile curled at the corners of his mouth as he quietly waited for a response.

However, the next moment, before he even had a chance to blink, he saw Colorado's head suddenly jerk forward and disappear from the video screen.
