Chapter 1346

She felt that all the humiliation she had suffered in her life was because of Chloe.

Everyone was waiting to see her make a fool of herself. She had endured the previous humiliation for a long time and was waiting for her father to finish his current work so she could teach Chloe a good lesson! Unexpectedly, Infinity Media was on the brink of bankruptcy thanks to Chloe.

If Infinity Media really went bankrupt, how would she face the ridicule from the outside world?

As the future leader of the Alonso family, how could she establish her authority at home and manage the Alonso family if she bankrupted a company as soon as she took

over?

Damn Chloe! How much humiliation could she bear?

Her car pulled up in front of Starlight International. The next second, the car door was flung open and slammed shut.

This series of actions clearly displayed Wendy’s anger, and the reporters watched as she walked over with a stern face.

The reporters didn’t approach her, but they all perked up. They whispered excitedly.

Woah, that aura… She’s really terrifying!”

“Are they going to have a public showdown?”

“She actually came straight to Starlight International. She must be really desperate!”

“I guess so, Infinity Media is almost bankrupt!”

Chloe watched as Wendy walked straight over, a smile still on her face. But before Wendy could enter, she was stopped by a group of black–clad bodyguards outside the lobby.

“Get out of the way!” Wendy yelled, obviously very angry.

The bodyguards ignored her, standing ramrod straight.

The reporters snapped a few pictures of Wendy, and Chloe slowly said, “Let her come up.”

Chloe didn’t think she would be intimidated by Wendy.

Upon hearing her, the bodyguards immediately stepped back to Chloe’s side. Their eyes were fixed on Wendy, ready to restrain her at any moment.

Wendy walked up the steps, her eyes fixed on Chloe. After a while, she turned to look at Dona. “What is the meaning of this? What are you doing here?”

“It’s obvious, I’m here to discuss a partnership with Ms. Summers.”

Wendy said angrily, “A contract with her?”

“Your contract and their contracts are all with my company. How dare you discuss contracts with others?! Dona, are you betraying me?!”

The most talented artists in the company were almost all trained by Dona. Now that Dona was leading everyone to collectively betray Wendy, how could she not be angry?

“It’s all your fault!” Wendy suddenly turned around, glaring at Chloe. “Using old tactics, don’t you get bored? Always poaching artists from other companies?”

Chloe was startled by Wendy’s shout and didn’t respond for a moment. After a few seconds, she recovered and suddenly burst out laughing.

Chloe looked at the furious Wendy and laughed. “What does it matter if I use outdated methods? They’re very effective. It doesn’t matter what tactics I use. As long as I can defeat you, that’s enough.”

She actually said that outright? Did she think the reporters weren’t there? Well, they should be thanking Chloe for creating news for them. And Ms. Summers‘ words made a lot of sense.

Upon hearing this, Wendy widened her eyes in surprise. She never thought Chloe would be so arrogant.

“Chloe, do you know that arrogant people usually end up in a terrible situation?”

Chloe didn’t care. “Really? Then I’m really in danger…”

With that, she turned to the media reporters and said, “Let’s all bear witness. If anything happens to me in the future, or if I disappear, it’s definitely because of Wendy.”

“You…” Wendy was so angry she wanted to punch her! In front of so many reporters, she actually…

If necessary, she would consider dealing with Chloe privately. But because of what she said today, her plan was almost impossible now.

That’s why Chloe had never fought with Wendy privately. Now that she was pregnant, she couldn’t afford to have any possible accidents. So, she chose to repeatedly emphasize her conflict with Wendy in front of the reporters.

But seeing Wendy’s angry face, the smile on her face gradually disappeared. It seemed that Wendy really intended to do something bad to her privately.

“Defeat me, Chloe, how can you be so confident? Just because Dona is now discussing contracts with you and with the artists? Think about it, can you guys succeed? What good will collaborating with Seth do? The current contracts in my company can outshine them!”

“Speaking of contracts, Ms. Wendy, I think I need to tell you something…” Dona’s voice suddenly rang out from the side, as she walked towards Wendy and said emotionlessly, “It’s the company that first failed to guarantee the artists‘ resources, and there are various details of breach of contract. The artists have officially filed a lawsuit against you. As a former collaborator, I feel I should remind you to prepare for what’s to come! After all, it’s not just one lawsuit.”

Dona’s words made Wendy’s anger reach its peak. “Are you encouraging them to sue me?!”

“To say I encouraged them would be an overstatement. Ms. Wendy, the artists have their own ideas…”

“Bitch!”

With a harsh “slap“, Wendy cut off Dona’s words. The reporters who had been watching the drama suddenly began to discuss it!

“Oh my, Ms. Alonso is so angry she’s hitting people! Quick, take a picture, take a picture!”

“Haha, being born into a wealthy family really means you can do whatever you want!”

“Because she’s being sued, she actually hit someone in public. The people of the Alonso family are really magnanimous!”

Dona’s head was knocked to the side by the slap, and it took her a few seconds to slowly turn back to look at Wendy. “Are you still angry, Ms. Alonso?”

“Do you think suing me will do any good? Do you think the lawyers of the Alonso family are incompetent?”

Dona chuckled “We all know you’re the heiress of the Alonso family, but in showbiz, you’re always bullying people using the Alonso family power, ain’t that a bit tasteless?

What’s even more laughable is, despite relying on this power, and looking at your current situation, you’re not better off!”
