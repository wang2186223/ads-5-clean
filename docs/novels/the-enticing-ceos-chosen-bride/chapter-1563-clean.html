Phoebe was stunned, "What does it have to do with me...."

"The way Mr. Azriel presented himself alongside you in front of everyone tonight, it felt a bit subtle, you know."

Just like the last time with Abelard. They even drugged her, forcing her into bed with that fool.

Azriel nodded, and got straight to the point. "If I say that I plan to marry your second daughter, do you have any conditions?"

Cole was caught off guard by this sudden proposal, but couldn't help but feel secretly delighted. They had just been discussing this matter, but didn't expect it to come to fruition so quickly.

"Mr.Azriel, why don't we discuss this properly after the banquet?"

Now was not the time to discuss such matters.

Well, since you don't want to discuss now, let's forget about it."

What kind of strange person was this?

"Right, you even made me marry a fool. Not to mention a pervert.

All to get his 30% shares for Wendy? You're really ready to sell me off?! Are you a father, or a bastard?!"

However, to secure 30% shares for his eldest daughter, he was ready to marry off his second daughter to a pervert without a second thought. It was indeed heartbreaking.

Having lived for so many years, when had Cole ever been publicly humiliated like this?

Betrayed by my own flesh and blood...
