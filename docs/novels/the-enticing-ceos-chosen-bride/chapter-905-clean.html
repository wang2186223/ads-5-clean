Chloe’s body stiffened instantly.

Chloe bit her lip, feeling a warmth well up in her eyes.

“Thank you… Mom.”

Her voice choked up as she buried her head in Elizabeth’s tender embrace.

She suddenly felt a sense of belonging that she had never before experienced.

Her rigid body gradually relaxed, and her slender shoulders trembled slightly.
