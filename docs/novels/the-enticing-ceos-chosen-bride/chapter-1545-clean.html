Seeing Wendy's crazed state, Cole frowned. He said, "How could he be so thoughtless? Don't count your chickens before they hatch!"

Wendy scoffed, "Just look, after the comments were withdrawn, the online attacks on her are even more vicious.'

She was right, the comments on Chloe had become even more fierce.

"Feeling guilty, is she? She has the nerve to do it, but not the courage to take the criticism?"

“Can she only handle flattery and not criticism?"

"I suddenly feel like it was so stupid of me to defend her online before. She only allows us to argue for her but doesn't allow others to criticize her!"

"She forced an elderly man to ruin his own reputation by testifying in court for her, she must be feeling guilty."

"A problem that could've been solved with some money, and now she's pushed two elderly people to this point, is she happy now?"

"Such a cold-blooded woman, today I finally see her true selfishness..."

"I used to idolize her, but now, looking back, I feel so stupid for sticking up for her. Thinking about everything she's done, it all feels so ruthless! She never leaves any room for others, it's so cruel..."

Cole merely glanced at the flood of similar comments, although the content varied slightly. Of course, there were also some netizens who consistently defended Chloe, but they were fewer in number.

His brows furrowed; he was doubtful about Damon's action. Did Chloe bewitch him so that he couldn't bear to see her suffer a bit?

So, he acted without thinking about the consequences? Because of that woman, he had hurt Wendy to this point, so it wasn't impossible for him to act this way.

He shook his head slightly and sighed softly. It's such a waste of good talent.

He said, "The business banquet is tomorrow night. I will personally go to Harper's Mansion tonight to invite Presley and Robin. If you're done with your things, go home now."

Hearing about the Harper family, the smile on Wendy's face disappeared. She said, "Grandpa Presley has officially retired now.

He probably won't attend..."

Cole responded emotionlessly with a cold hum, "His grandson made you into what you are now, and we haven't even gotten an explanation from him. The last time I asked for his help with Alonso Corporation, I got nothing. Today, I'm inviting him to a banquet, so he shouldn't refuse."

Wendy nodded. “If the head of the Harper family, Presley, shows up, those hesitant investors will definitely trust us. No matter what the recent gossip says, he’s still the leader of the Harper family."

“Right. If we're really going to hold a business banquet, we need to make full use of its effect.” Cole added. His gaze returned to the computer screen in front of Wendy, a hint of confusion in his eyes.

Why did he always feel that Damon's behavior was too irrational?

Was he really so foolishly infatuated with a woman? But he quickly put this question out of his mind. At most, it was about protecting a woman. No matter how big the issue was, it wouldn't have serious consequences.

He didn't need to bother with such trifles.

That night, at Harper's Mansion.

Presley had been in a foul mood ever since Damon's withdrawal of the negative comments about Chloe. Even though Damon had announced his departure from the Harper Group, he was still his grandson at the end of the day. The rash decision he made, being so influenced by a woman, completely went against his teachings.

What he feared most was happening.

He initially didn't want to bring up the topic, but Cole seemed to be deliberately provoking him. As they sat in the living room watching TV, the news about Chloe and Carolina's lawsuit appeared. Cole shook his head and sighed. "The developments in this matter are really unexpected. Ms. Chloe is so ruthless. But since she had the courage to make that decision, she shouldn't be afraid of others"

opinions, right? Damon cares about her too much, making such a rash decision... Didn't he think the consequences would be even worse when he withdrew the comments?"

After he finished, he sighed again, "Damon was the only promising kid I've seen in years, who would have thought... he would be so bewitched by a woman..."

Presley's face grew even darker, and the dinner he had just eaten seemed to be stuck in his stomach, unable to digest.

However, Robin, upon hearing this, narrowed his eyes slightly, a sparkle flashed in his eyes, and a hint of a smile appeared at the corner of his mouth, but it quickly returned to normal.

Seeing Presley's dark expression, Cole felt somewhat relieved, he placed two invitations on the table. He said, "Alonso Corporation is hosting a business banquet tomorrow night. I believe you're well aware of the purpose of this banquet, and I hope you can attend."

Robin glanced at the invitation, a touch of sarcasm flashed in his eyes. He asked, "I heard you're trying to get your eldest daughter to manage the company?"

Cole paused for a moment. "...l just want her to gradually familiarize herself with the business in the company. After all, the project at hand is very important, I can't retire at this critical moment. Wendy still has a lot to learn, so I hope you can help her when needed."

Robin just chuckled nonchalantly. "Well, of course."

Presley hadn't spoken a word the entire time. It was obvious Cole was intentionally making him feel uncomfortable. He wasn't oblivious to it. The two invitations were just lying there. Cole didn't even bother asking him; he didn't even get a chance to refuse.

Was Cole exploiting his guilt over what Damon had done to Wendy, acting so brazenly without any second thoughts?

But the thing was, with what Damon did to Wendy, turning a healthy girl into such a state, the Harper family would always been in debt to the Alonso family. Cole had hit the nail on the head, exploiting his vulnerability. Because of the guilt he had in his heart, he had no choice but to attend.
