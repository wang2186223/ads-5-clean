Rose turned her head to look at him but said nothing. Morrison’s brow furrowed. Clueless woman, he thought. He shrugged off the robe he was wearing and plopped into the pool in front of her.

Morrison glanced at the small foot resting on his shoulder. His voice dropped to a deep rumble. “Are you stepping on me, Rose?”

“Just... don't pull me in. I’m pregnant.”

His eyes narrowed. This woman was always playing the pregnancy card. However, he didn't move her foot. Instead, he began to rotate it gently, massaging her ankle with surprising tenderness. It felt incredibly soothing.

“Ah!” Lost in thought, she didn’t notice Morrison pulling on her toes until a sharp pain made her cry out.

“Are you so oblivious? You’re as stiff as a board, and you want to swim?” As he spoke, he applied a little more pressure.

“Ah, Morrison, that hurts!”

“Endure it,” he said coldly, though his hands eased off immediately. After another round of stretching, Rose stopped complaining and felt her tension melt away.

“Bite the bullet,” he tossed back, using an old phrase of theirs as he proceeded with his work.

“Alright then. Come down.” Morrison finally released her and looked up.

“Don’t let go! I’m scared!” Rose warned, her legs splashing about until they eventually hooked around Morrison’s waist. She was like an octopus, refusing to let go.

Morrison’s expression shifted through a myriad of emotions, his arms tightening around her waist. “Are you going to learn or what?” he asked, watching her delicate features up close.

“So, you’re my coach today?” she teased.

Morrison’s gaze softened. “You can find someone else if you want.”

Morrison stiffened. The proximity of their bodies made it impossible for him to concentrate. “What did you say?”

Morrison’s eyes widened in shock, but he didn’t pull away. Rose wasn’t usually this audacious, but she had longed for this man for nearly eight years. Watching him with other women had been a slow torture, and her patience had finally worn thin.

Her technique was clumsy, but she stubbornly refused to give up, barging into his territory. Morrison let her invade him recklessly, his arched back revealing that he was, in reality, playing along.

His initial indifference left Rose feeling disappointed. She released one hand, her cool fingertips sliding over his shoulder to his chest. The tender touch made Morrison’s body go rigid, his breathing becoming erratic.

He seized Rose’s wandering hand. “Do you even realize what you’re doing?”

Rose breathed lightly, her face flushed red. “It’s your fault for teasing me all day. You asked for this.”

Morrison’s grip tightened. “When have I ever teased you?”

“Every moment!” she whispered between tender pecks on his lips. Though Morrison tried to dodge, she always managed to steal another.

“Rose, are you taking advantage of me?”

“Maybe. But you tempted me first. It’s only fair I flirt a little,” she teased, her voice sultry.

Morrison chuckled at her audacity. What kind of woman was she? Bold, brazen, and completely unapologetic. “Exactly how did I tempt you? I’ll stop—if you can tell me.”

Rose shook her head. “You can’t stop. Not unless you get a new face.”

Morrison’s brows knit together. He pulled back, his voice growing cold. “So, it’s just my looks you’re after?”

Rose didn’t hesitate, nodding vigorously. “Yeah, yeah! Morrison, you’re incredibly handsome.”

Morrison’s mouth twitched, unsure whether to be angry or flattered. Yet, as he accused her of impropriety, she only grew more enthusiastic, stretching up to claim every advantage over him.

Morrison played along for a while, allowing her to catch him in several more kisses. “Enough! Are you going to learn or what?”

Rose clung to him. “Just a little longer.”

Morrison was speechless. This woman was really getting into it. As she pressed closer, Morrison suddenly took control. He pulled her deeper into the water, grasping her leg and moving her toward the poolside steps. He loomed over her, effortlessly reversing their roles. His kiss was a tempest compared to Rose’s gentle drizzle.

Rose tried to keep pace at first, but she soon grew fearful. She struggled to breathe until Morrison shifted his angle, allowing her to gasp for air. She tried to push him away.

“Stop, stop! Morrison, that’s enough! Let’s call it a day!”

His face darkened with frustration. Had she not been begging for more just moments ago? Now that she had riled him up, she was done? “Rose, you don’t get to call the shots now,” he growled, reaching for her leg again.

“Morrison, calm down!”

“It’s too late.”

Despite his anger, he was mindful of her condition and didn't drag her forcibly, but he closed the distance between them again.

“No, no! Please... just think! I’m pregnant right now!”

“The doctor said light exercise is fine.”

Rose panicked. “I think you’ve misunderstood the meaning of ‘exercise’!”

“No, you’re mistaken. Besides, we’re in public. We can’t do this here!”

“I’ve booked the whole floor.”

“But there must be cameras! I was wrong, okay? I’m sorry!”

Morrison paused, scanning the ceiling corners of the pool area. Sure enough—cameras.

Rose breathed a sigh of relief, straightening her disheveled swimsuit, only to notice a red mark on her chest. Her cheeks burned as she realized Morrison had left a trail of similar marks. She fixed her clothes and looked up to find Morrison watching her with the intensity of a predator.

Her heart raced. This was not the time or the place—especially while she was pregnant. Just as the prize seemed within reach, it was out of bounds.

Covering her chest protectively, she gulped. “Don’t do anything rash, Morrison.” Her gaze involuntarily drifted toward the bulge in Morrison’s swim trunks. Blushing furiously, she quickly averted her eyes. “You need to... cool off.”

Morrison’s face darkened, a hint of red touching his ears. He released her and dived into the pool. “Rose, you won’t get away with this.”

He left her with that threat, the sound of splashing water fading as he swam away. He did at least six laps before he finally approached her again.

“So, are you going to learn or what?”

Rose could tell he had cooled off, so she nodded and cautiously dipped her toes back into the water. With a stern face, Morrison took her hands. “Lift your legs up.”

She timidly raised one leg and then tried the other, but couldn’t quite manage to float. Flustered, she looked at him. “I... I’m scared.”

Morrison rolled his eyes. “Idiot.”
