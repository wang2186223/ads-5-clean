Jonah looked at Carolina with a calm expression and said, "Would you agree to drop the lawsuit right now if I asked you to?"

Carolina raised her eyebrows, "Do you think that's possible at this point?"

"Nothing is impossible as long as the court is not in session. You just need to tell me whether you agree or not!" Jonah said.

"I do not agree!" Carolina answered immediately without any hesitation, "I have been accused by everyone. What do you think I'm doing this for? You may be satisfied with the current situation of the Summers family, but I'm not! Nick hasn't achieved anything till now. As his father, don't you feel guilty at all? You might be content with the life you have without any complaints. That's your choice. But I can't! I, Carolina, am selfish and can't live such a poor life. Moreover, I can't watch my son live under someone else's thumb. He always has to obey others, living miserable all his life.

So, I don't care how shameless and thick-skinned I appear in the eyes of others!"

Jonah wobbled. He closed his eyes tightly, and after a long pause, he said again, "I beg you, drop the lawsuit immediately."

"Impossible!" Carolina refused again, "Don't you get it? There's no going back. This time, the break with Chloe can only end in a lose-lose situation!"

Carolina was decided. After speaking, she stepped on and resolutely walked into the courthouse. Nick was moved by Carolina's words. Although Carolina used to manage the household, she did give him the best in material life. At this point, she was still thinking for him. How could he not be moved?

Jonah stood in place. His body swayed non-stop, and his expression was full of sadness and heaviness.

Half an hour later, the court was in session.

There were many spectators in the courtroom, Nick, Jonah, Wendy, Nathan and Kane. Damon and Yasmine were the last to arrive, sitting in the spectator seats and watching Chloe's state at all times.

The judge first asked them if they wanted to reconsider to drop the lawsuit, or give them time to mediate privately. This kind of family dispute, if it could be kept private, then it should.

Chloe looked at Carolina, as if to say it all depended on Carolina's decision. As a result, Carolina said, "Five hundred million. If she's willing to settle privately, then I'll drop the lawsuit."

The courtroom was in an uproar. Most people felt shocked and found it laughable.

"In a blink of an eye, the money has doubled. This old lady's shamelessness is really beyond measure!"

Chloe gave a slight smile, "Ms. Carolina, you really have a way of showing people the depths of shamelessness.'

"How could you say that? I'm your grandmother! Are you going to disrespect me like this in court?!" Carolina yelled.

Chloe chuckled, then turned to the judge, "Even if we could settle this privately, I wouldnt give her a single cent. Since Ms. Carolina is so adamant, let's get started then. My stance is crystal clear. I won't agree to pay Carolina the money she's asking for. Not a billion, not a million, not even a dime."

Carolina scoffed, "You really won't quit until you've hit rock bottom, huh?" Once she finished speaking, she sat down, leaving her attorney to handle everything.

The lawyer listed several accusations against Chloe. As a young offspring, she had failed to fulfill her responsibilities. She showed complete indifference to Carolina, even displaying violent tendencies. This was referring to the incident a few days ago when Carolina was thrown out of Starlight International. In addition, Chloe was accused of being verbally abusive and disrespectful towards Carolina, including the incident where she set fire to the house, leaving Carolina homeless.

Every inappropriate behavior from the past that Carolina could remember was included in the accusations. However, most of them overlooked the context and the causes, only focusing on the parts that were advantageous to Carolina.

Throughout the whole process, Chloe just sat there quietly. After a rough summary from Carolina's lawyer, the judge turned to Chloe, asking, "Defendant Ms. Chloe, is what was just stated true?"

Leaning on her chin, Chloe smiled faintly, "True."
