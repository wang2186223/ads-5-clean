Chapter 531

The old lady shot Morrison a stern, warning look before hobbling over to Rose. With surprising tenderness, she took Rose’s hand into her own weathered ones.

“Rose, dear, don’t be scared. I’ve got your back, and I won’t let anyone mess with you!” she declared.

Rose was still struggling to process the sudden shift in momentum. She glanced up at Morrison, who was standing a few feet away with a dark, silent expression. He looked as if he were fighting the urge to bolt. Rose recognized the old woman immediately—it was Megan, Morrison’s grandmother. They had met years ago, during their school days, when Rose had visited his home.

“I…” Rose started, her voice trailing off.

“Don’t be scared,” Megan interrupted, her eyes twinkling. “You’re having a baby, which means we’re basically family now. I liked you the minute I saw you back then, and I like you even more now! You like Morrison, and he clearly likes you. Now that there’s a little one on the way, isn't it wonderful? Let’s get you home so we can talk about getting you two hitched!”

Standing nearby, Chloe couldn’t help but chuckle. Are all grandmothers this adorable and direct nowadays? she wondered.

“Grandma!” Morrison finally snapped, his face flushing with displeasure. “When did I ever say I liked her?!”

“What the hell are you playing at?!” Megan retorted, her temper flaring even higher. “Lashing out at me? I’ll teach you a lesson!” Without hesitation, she raised her ornate cane and whacked Morrison across the legs a couple of times.

The sight of a dashing man in a bespoke, expensive suit taking a public beating from a tiny old lady with a cane was nothing short of comical. Rose let out a genuine laugh. Although Megan was swinging with gusto, Rose could tell the old lady wasn't putting enough force behind it to do real damage. It probably stung, but it was well within Morrison’s tolerance limit.

Serves him right for being so stubborn, Rose thought, feeling a sense of vindication.

Hearing her laughter, Morrison shot Rose a frosty, narrowed look, but he didn't dare move while his grandmother was on the warpath.

Chloe, watching the scene unfold, finally stepped in to provide a voice of reason. “We can’t sort this out on the sidewalk, Rose. Let’s head back to your place first.”

Morrison’s expression turned even icier, and while Megan’s face grew serious, she refused to let go of Rose’s hand. Following Chloe’s lead, the group began the trek back to Rose’s apartment.

However, the peace didn't last long. As they reached the foot of the building, a swarm of reporters—who had been lying in wait—suddenly descended upon them like locusts. Rose was immediately jostled by the crowd, her shoulder bumping into Morrison’s chest again and again as the cameras flashed.

Finally losing his patience with the chaos, Morrison reached out and pulled Rose firmly into his arms, using his broad shoulders to shield her from the prying lenses.

“Miss Rose! Is this gentleman the father of your child?” a reporter yelled, thrusting a microphone forward.

Rose kept her lips pressed tight, refusing to give them a headline. Instead, it was Megan who stepped into the spotlight. She nodded cheerfully at the cameras. “Yes, he is!”

“So, is a wedding in the works?” another journalist shouted.

Megan’s smile grew even wider. “Absolutely. Very soon!”

“There are rumors that Rose has many suitors, but no official relationship. Have you two been dating in secret?”

Megan didn't miss a beat. “Yes, for quite some time!”

Chloe, Rose, and Morrison were all left speechless by the old lady's blatant reimagining of the truth. Even the seasoned reporters were taken aback by her enthusiasm. One of them asked awkwardly, “Ma’am... could we perhaps hear from the couple as well?”

Megan blinked, glanced at the stony-faced Morrison and the wide-eyed Rose, then turned back to the press with a dismissive wave. “You lot really can’t read the room, can you? These two are just incredibly shy. They’re far too overwhelmed by love to answer your questions right now!”
