The host’s face was full of excitement and his voice was slightly trembling.

Among the guesses and confusion from the audience, the host said with a smile, “Next, let’s introduce the leaders of the organizing committee of this international competition. Also the leaders of the global authoritative evaluation agencies, and a few authoritative judges. Let’s welcome them on stage with a round of applause.”

What followed was the host’s introduction and speech about each judge.

The reason for inviting the judges on stage was to demonstrate the fairness and transparency of this international competition, open to scrutiny and questioning in any form. Next was the highlight of the night.

“Next, we will announce the top three winners of this International Perfumery Competition, first of all.” The host deliberately paused for a moment, creating a sense of suspense.

Keira’s hands were tightly clenched together. As long as she could win an award, receive it, and prove her strength. No one would think of her as a plagiarist.

Chloe had no evidence six years ago, what evidence did Chloe have today to prove she was a thief?

Her heart was pounding. Tonight was the decisive moment! No, there was no such thing as failure, only success!

“First, let’s invite the three winners on stage. The works of these three winners are Etemity, Pure Snow and Firefly! Congratulations to these three perfumers for creating such top–notch works, let’s welcome them on stage!” The host announced.

Keira’s clenched hands suddenly relaxed, covering her pounding heart. Her face under the mask was pale, but full of relief.

She slowly stood up, wearing a stunning and beautiful high–end dress, like a fairy. She pulled up the man sitting next to her, hugging him excitedly, “Lance, did you see that? I really won an award.”

Lance gently hugged her back, but his face showed no emotion, “Congratulations.”

“You too, Lance!” Keira said.

With this, the future of the Olson Group was full of endless possibilities!

awesome she was.

Michelle scoffed, “Rosanna, you might get away with being a smart aleck once or twice. But if you keep it up, you’ll slip up eventually. Always putting others down, bigging yourself up – don’t you get sick of these cheap tricks?”
