Carolina spoke trembly, because if this was the truth, the world would be too ridiculous.

Viviana had turned her son into a betrayed man and Keira wasn't a descendant of the Summers family. Now, it seemed that Yasmine had turned her son into a cuckold, and Chloe wasn't a descendant of the Summers family as well.

What on earth had the Summers family done to end up marrying such women?

She had gathered all her courage to blow this matter up, but then Chloe suddenly claimed she had nothing to do with the Summers family? If that was really the case, then legally speaking, she indeed didnt have the obligation to raise her. No wonder she was so calm from the beginning. She had been prepared all along.

Carolina was clearly so angry that she was talking nonsense. Nick, who was sitting next to her, suddenly stood up. His face turned pale.

"What?! She's Yasmine's illegitimate child with another man?!"

The audience was in chaos. They were shocked by this outcome, but also felt as if they had expected it.

However, Jane, who was Standing in the witness box, clearly said, "According to DNA test results, the similarity index between Nick and Ms. Chloe is over ninety percent. The blood relationship is established."

An uproar ensued. This result was unexpected for everyone.

Chloe actually shared a blood relationship with Nick?!

Then this lawsuit, was Chloe doomed to lose?

Did this doctor plot against Chloe in court?

Everyone looked at Chloe with a bit of sorrow. She was so shrewd and yet was betrayed at the last minute. Being betrayed at this time, she was bound to lose the lawsuit.

Chloe tugged at her lips and smiled.

This result was disappointing. So she really did share a blood relationship with Nick. How nice it would be if she didn’t?

Every single person in the room, except for Damon who was indifferent, was shocked. Even Yasmine, her hands tightly clasped together and her face was pale. The shock in her eyes was no less than that of others. It seemed that she was the most surprised by today's outcome.

A warm hand suddenly pressed down on her shoulder. Bryson patted her lightly, trying to comfort her. Yasmine bit her lip tightly.

Her hands on the wheelchair were shaking uncontrollably.

This result was absolutely the most satisfying for Carolina and Wendy. They never expected that this suddenly appearing female witness would change her stance.

"Silence!" The judge maintained order with a deep voice. As the court gradually quieted down, the judge said to Jane, "Please continue."

Jane nodded, flipped out another document, glanced at it, and continued, "The DNA test involved Jonah and Nick. The result showed a similarity of over ninety percent. The father-son relationship is established."

Carolina sneered, "Does this even need to be tested? Who else could be the father of my son, if not Jonah?"

Jonah, sitting in the audience, was clutching his cane tightly. His wrinkled face was as pale as bone, his skin and lips trembling.

Carolina's words intensified his guilt and regret.

Jane gave Carolina a light look. That look was caught by Carolina.

There was helplessness, sorrow, and pity. These emotions were all in her eyes, making Carolina's heart suddenly tense. Before she even had a chance to catch her breath, she heard Jane continue, "The last DNA test involved Carolina and Nick. The result showed zero percent blood relation. No mother-son relationship."

"Oh my God!!!"

This result made Nathan and Kane both stand up.

“What's going on?!"

"Oh my God!"

"Why would it be like this?! The only son of the Summers family is not Carolina's biological child?!"

"But looking at Carolina's confidence just now, it doesn't seem like it!"

The courtroom was in unprecedented chaos. This result was a scenario that no one could have imagined even if they thought of a hundred possibilities.

Just look at how brazen Carolina had been recently. She was such a shameless woman, thinking about fighting for something for her son. Her attitude towards Nick was obviously that of a biological

mother. Otherwise, how could a person like her care about Nick's situation? Now it turned out that she had no blood relationship with Nick, not at all. How could that be?!

"Jonah and Carolina are husband and wife. Nick is Jonah's son but not Carolina's son. This means..."

Some people managed to sort out the family relationships and instantly understood something. Their eyes swept across the room and gradually all focused on Jonah in the front row.

Whispers were all discussing the truth.

"So that's what happened?"

"Infidelity huh."

"I never heard a thing about it."

"It seems that Carolina didn't know. Wow, to be able to hide it for so many years of sharing the same bed, that's impressive."

"I never thought things would turn out this way."

The low whispers sounded like they were magnified a hundred times, clearly entering Carolina's ears.

"No..." Carolina's face was pale at this moment. She shook her head blankly, "This can't be possible."

She shook her head in denial. All the chatter around her was seeping into her brain and echoing non-stop. "Shut up, all of you!

This is impossible!!!" She suddenly screamed, pointing her finger at the spectators, hysterical.

Carolina yelled, "Nick is the baby I carried for ten months! Are you saying those ten months of pregnancy were a hoax?! The old servants can vouch for me, and the hospital must have my delivery records and birth certificate! Who dares to question if Nick is not my son?!"
