Chapter 1450

"Presley!" James rushed forward to steady Presley, who was about to collapse.

Presley stabilized himself and pushed James away. "I'm fine."

He sat down on a nearby chair, Wendy's heart-wrenching voice from the TV echoing in his ears, unbearable to listen to.

"Don't worry, I'll take good care of the Harper Group." Robin said calmly.

"Yeah, after all, Robin has lived more than two decades longer than Damon. With so many years of work experience, how could he possibly be worse than Damon? You can rest assured about this."

James picked up the walking stick, and Presley took it, his hands trembling.

"The main reason I chose the Alonso family was because I was worried that the Alonso family would cause trouble for us in the future. After all, the strength of the Alonso family is obvious, and they have Princess Ava of Y Country as their backing. It's better to make the Alonso family a complete ally than to offend them. And now, Damon is threatening to take action against the Harper family, Yasmine and Chloe suddenly became the real princesses of Y Country. Can you really manage the Harper Group well?" Presley's voice lost its previous firmness.

Robin frowned, "I believe Damon is just bluffing. He's been managing the Harper Group for years, so how could he destroy it just like that? Don't just listen to Nathan. Damon was just impulsive and spoke rashly."

Presley shook his head, but before he could say anything, the TV screen switched to a scene outside the Harper family's corporate building.

Damon appeared on the screen, surrounded by reporters. Despite the lack of bodyguards, the reporters naturally kept a respectful distance due to Damon's imposing presence, holding up their microphones to ask Damon questions frantically.

"Mr. Harper, has today's board meeting ended? Who will be the next chairman of the Harper Group?"

"We all know that Presley places great importance on the woman's family background. Now that your fiancée has been confirmed as the real princess of Y Country, Ms. Chloe, her status is naturally much higher than Ms. Wendy's. Presley has no reason to , right?"

Damon remained expressionless, his deep gazes sweeping over the restless reporters. The scene fell silent.

"The Harper Group's chairman will be taken over by Robin. I have officially resigned as the executive of the Harper Group, and I have given up all my shares. From now on, any issues of the Harper Group have nothing to do with me."

The scene was silent for a few tens of seconds before it exploded.

"Resigned as executive and gave up the shares?!"

"So you didn't know about Ms. Summers being the royal princess in time during the meeting, did you?"

"What are your plans now?"

Finally, Damon spoke calmly. "I will do everything in my power to strike at the Harper Group."

Upon the release of this news, the entire P City, and even the areas where the Harper Group operated globally, were shocked by Damon's calm yet powerful words.

Damon, the former executive of the Harper Group, now said that he would strike at the Harper Group with all his might. This shocking news spread around the world at the speed of light.

The morning after the banquet ended, Queen Julia was preparing to have a designer tailor a princess dress for Chloe.

Upon learning that Damon had resigned as the executive of the Harper Group and planned to strike at the Harper Group with all his might, Chloe decided to return to H Country immediately.

Queen Julia said, "This is not difficult for Mr. Harper. Since he can completely give up the Harper Group, it shows that he has enough confidence to accomplish this." She did not want Chloe to leave so soon.

Chloe shook her head, "Although you didn't inform me about the coronation in advance, I don't want to put you in a difficult position. I agree to attend the coronation, but I won't stay in this palace. I have my own career and life. Damon is now my husband, I trust his decisions and abilities, but I must return to his side."

Chloe was firm, and Queen Julia was torn. She felt both relieved and helpless, "You know, the moment you got into trouble, he sent people into the palace. Now Becky is being held in the hospital, waiting for him to deal with the matter personally."

Chloe smiled, "He won't let me be wronged."

Queen Julia shook her head, "Do you know what he told me?"

Chloe looked at Julia.

"I once wanted to explain to him what happened, but he said he didn't want to know. Even if you did something wrong, it would be right in his eyes." Queen Julia smiled, "I mean, aside from the whole Becky thing, he's just too biased. He said he just trusts you. You can bully others, but others can't

bully you. No matter how much trouble you get into, he can take the blame for you. I didn't even get a chance to help you myself."

Damon's double standards filled Chloe with satisfaction and sweetness.

"Well. I guess you're out of luck. I've been hogging Damon too much, depriving him of his boyfriend and husband duties. In the future, I need to give him more space to make his own decisions."

Queen Julia nodded, "You two have a very interesting dynamic."

Chloe just chuckled, not responding.

"But my role in your life is different from Damon's. You can't take away my rights and responsibilities. He loves you because he's your husband, and I love you because I'm your grandma. Chloe, I once said I'd give you all I have, not to compensate you, but to satisfy myself."

Chloe looked helpless.

In the end, Queen Julia still smiled, gently patted her hand, and said, "Go on, I trust and support your decision, because I want you to be happy."

Chloe's eyes sparkled, looking at Queen Julia's kindly smile, and she smiled. Finally, she opened her arms and hugged Queen Julia. "Thank you."

This was the closest and most proactive affection Chloe had shown since meeting Queen Julia.

Queen Julia hugged Chloe back emotionally, tears filling her eyes. She thought she could never bridge the gap between them in her lifetime. "I wasn't there when you were born, didn't give you the care you deserved, and didn't watch you grow up. I'm sorry for everything."

Chloe gently patted Julia's back, "It's okay, I'm doing great now, and I'm grateful you're still so healthy."

Chloe stood up, looked at teary-eyed Julia, smiled, wiped the tears from her eyes, and gently kissed her forehead. "Grandma."

Queen Julia was stunned when she heard the clear and gentle word from Chloe's mouth. Then, the tears she just wiped off started flowing again like a spring. She quickly nodded, and after a while, she choked out a loud "Yes".

Chloe was touched. She wiped away Julia's tears again. "I have to go now. I need to get back to Damon right away."
