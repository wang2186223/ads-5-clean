"Are you disappointed in me, Mr. Quinn? You used to praise my work."

"Georgina, are you reading too much into things?"

"Unless it’s work-related, please stop coming to my office. I’m busy. Now leave."

Georgina’s expression turned ugly, her facade of sweetness crumbling completely.

Knowing him, he was probably already having someone trace the source of this hit piece and let his people handle the investigation quietly.

Whatever desperate person started this rumor clearly didn’t know who they were messing with.
