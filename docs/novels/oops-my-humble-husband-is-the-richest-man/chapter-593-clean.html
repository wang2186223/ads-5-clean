Chapter 593

Susan's eyes widened in shock.

She finally realized—she had been tricked!

"Liar! You lied to me!"

She sprang up from the floor like a madwoman, lunging straight at Evelyn.

Her hands flew toward Evelyn's throat, aiming to strangle her.

Just as her fingertips were about to touch skin—

Two kicks came from both sides simultaneously!

Thud!

Susan was sent flying backward, landing hard on the floor.

Evelyn turned her head.

On her left was Alexander.

On her right was Grace.

Evelyn gave a light cough.

Grace didn't look at her, simply gesturing to the people outside. "Take her away."

Several staff members immediately stepped forward and dragged Susan out.

Only then did Grace turn to Evelyn. "Anything to say to me?"

Evelyn hadn't expected her to be so direct.

She paused. "Grace, I—"

Before she could finish, Grace interrupted her. "Who else needs arresting?"

Evelyn was taken aback. "What?"

"The Fox." Grace's tone was calm. "Shouldn't we arrest them too?"

Evelyn stared at her.

Their eyes met.

Suddenly, Evelyn understood—Grace knew she was pretending.

She didn't ask for reasons.

She didn't ask when it would end.

She simply cooperated silently.

Evelyn smiled. "Of course we should arrest them. We are law-abiding citizens, after all."

She took out Susan's phone. "This is full of evidence of the Fox inducing crimes. It's enough to make an arrest."

"Good. Let's go."

The group left in a grand procession.

Grace naturally got into the same car as Evelyn.

The car started moving.

Only then did Grace look at her. "Are you hurt?"

"No, everything's fine." Evelyn replied, then asked in return, "Why did you come to New York?"

"My father transferred me here." Grace's tone was flat. "He said it's not good for a girl to always be around corpses. He wanted me to change my environment and clear my mind."

As for why she needed to clear her mind—

Grace didn't say, but Evelyn understood instantly.

It was because of "Evelyn's" death.

Her best friend had perished at sea. Grace must have been heartbroken.

Evelyn had originally thought her life was bitter, that she had few friends, and that even if she "died," not many would care.

But she never expected so many people remembered her.

A sudden warmth surged in her heart.

Her phone vibrated at that moment.

Evelyn took it out. It was a message from Mason.

Ever since she exposed him as "Sister's Guardian," Mason had stopped hiding when contacting her.

Mason: [It seems you already have a way to complete the third task.]

Evelyn frowned.

This person really had been monitoring her all along.

She replied: [Yes. Once it's done, can I go to the Sterling Family to save my mother?]

Mason: [That should be the case.]

Seeing those four words, Evelyn let out a slight sigh of relief.

She looked at Grace again.

It was to save her mother that she had let "Evelyn" die.

Once her mother was rescued, she could "come back to life."

After all, Evelyn still had many friends waiting for her return.

Thinking this, Evelyn gently took Grace's hand.

The car soon arrived at the Castillo residence.

Seeing Evelyn enter with a group of Special Operations Division staff, the Castillo household immediately descended into chaos.

Anthony frowned deeply. "Miss Evelyn, what is the meaning of this?"

The old Mr. Castillo's face was dark. "Miss Grace, are you determined to completely sever ties with the Castillo family?"

Nicole, watching from the side, sneered. "How amusing... Grace, are you stupid? Do you have any evidence that the Castillo family poisoned anyone? And you bring people to search the Castillo house?"

She took a step forward, walking up to Evelyn. "You even dared to call the police? Utterly unreasonable! Do you think arresting everyone in the Castillo family will get you the antidote?"

Nicole took a step back, laughing loudly. "You know what? In the Castillo family's house here in New York, there's a special room for storing medicines! The antidote you're looking for is in one of the small compartments—and there are a thousand bottles of medicine in there! Can you accurately pick out the one you need from a thousand bottles?"

Her eyes gleamed with triumph. "I never imagined you'd be stupid enough to call the police! Hahaha, this is hilarious!"

After her arrogant speech, Nicole turned to old Mr. Castillo. "See? This is the kind of fool you wanted to side with? What good could come from following her! Old Mr. Castillo, do you see now what choice you should make?"

Evelyn watched her calmly. "Finished?"

She raised her hand and pointed at Nicole. "This person is the anonymous Fox online!"

Nicole's pupils contracted sharply.
