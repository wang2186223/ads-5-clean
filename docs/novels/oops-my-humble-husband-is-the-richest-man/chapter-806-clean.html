Chapter 806

Mr. Valentine was taken aback for a moment before speaking directly, "Does Chloe miss her daddy? I'll have that rascal come back to keep you company right away!"

During this period, Chloe had been calling Evelyn 'Mommy' and had also been coaxed into calling Alexander 'Daddy.'

Mr. Valentine thought Chloe was calling for Alexander.

But the little one shook her head vigorously, pointing at the TV screen and calling out in her childish voice, "Daddy! That's Daddy!"

Mr. Valentine frowned, his face full of confusion.

When Evelyn and Alexander returned home, Mr. Valentine called them into the study with a serious expression. "Chloe kept calling for 'Daddy' today. I think something's off. Has she remembered that scumbag Zack?"

Mr. Valentine looked troubled. "I've already had Zack thrown into hard labor in the suburbs. He'll never see the light of day again. But if Chloe really misses him, what should we do?"

Evelyn immediately shook her head. "It shouldn't be that. She's terrified of Zack. And... Zack isn't her father."

Mr. Valentine was stunned. "What did you say?"

Evelyn gave a bitter smile. "That day when Elian came to the house pretending to be Chloe's father, I took Chloe's DNA for testing. I found out Zack shares no blood relation with her."

Mr. Valentine's brow furrowed tightly. "I investigated your sister. She seemed honest and straightforward. I never expected her to be so bold. But it's a good thing it's not Zack's child. I was originally going to spare his life, but now it seems there's no need!"

As he finished speaking, a flash of killing intent passed through Mr. Valentine's eyes.

Evelyn thought about what Zack had done to her sister and felt this wasn't excessive.

Perhaps Zack had known all along that Chloe wasn't his child, which was why he was so cruel to her...

Mr. Valentine asked hesitantly, "Then who is Chloe's biological father?"

Evelyn smiled bitterly. "I don't know."

Mr. Valentine sighed. "Your sister is just like your mother, full of secrets. I still can't figure it out."

Evelyn fell silent for a moment.

Her sister indeed held too many secrets. She was still uncovering them.

Her sister was like a treasure map. On the surface, she seemed ordinary, weak, and easy to bully, yet surprises kept popping up.

Perhaps her sister wasn't as incompetent as she had imagined.

Evelyn sank into deep thought.

Seeing the grave expressions on their faces, Alexander spoke up. "Since Chloe misses her daddy, then I, as her daddy, will spend more time with her."

Evelyn said, "I'll spend more time with her too."

The two left the study and went to Chloe's room.

Chloe was obediently playing with a plush toy. The Sterlingny was watching over her, and Mina, with her large pregSterlingt belly, was also there.

Evelyn asked, "With your belly that big, aren't you afraid she might bump into you?"

Mina smiled. "Chloe is very sensible. I've never seen such a well-behaved child... She knows what she can and cannot do. She remembers after being told once."

These words made Evelyn's heart ache.

She remembered the first time she saw Chloe, how thin and frail the child had been.

A child this small should have baby fat, but back then, Chloe's little face was pointed, with hardly any flesh on her body.

Evelyn said softly, "Chloe suffered a lot when she was little."

The atmosphere in the room grew heavy.

Alexander said, "The future holds only blessings. Don't look so worried."

Evelyn couldn't help but laugh.

As they were talking, Natalie walked over, her face full of worry. "Evelyn, I'm afraid I can't help you this time. I went through the medical texts again and thought of every possible method. That person's infertility truly cannot be cured. His only hope is to find that fated woman to have descendants."

Evelyn asked, "Does it have to be a son? Does their family really have a throne to inherit?"

"No, girls in their family can also ascend the throne. The previous ruler of Aldoria was his mother... So as long as there's a child, boy or girl, they are equally precious! It's just a pity we don't know who this fated woman will be."

Evelyn also sighed.

Joanna said, "Without a child, we can't cooperate with him. What should we do?"

Natalie said, "Oh, if only a cute baby would fall from the sky, or if the country would issue a child? Our Sterling Family has difficulty with offspring. I've been researching this. It's just hard. There's no other way!"

As they were talking, little Chloe secretly turned on the TV and stared at the screen. She picked up the remote and changed channels several times before settling on the news broadcast.

Evelyn laughed. "Little Chloe, being with Daddy, has even learned to watch the news broadcast."

Everyone laughed.

At that moment, the news broadcast was a rerun. It soon switched to footage from Aldoria.

Little Chloe's eyes lit up, and she immediately called out, "Daddy!"

Everyone looked at her. Evelyn asked in confusion, "Who are you calling?"

Little Chloe pointed at the television. "Daddy!"
