Chapter 621

Grace Lorenz's face was pale with fury.

Her usually cool and detached features were now twisted with anger.

She glared fiercely at Leo Wright.

The broom in her hand smashed hard toward his arm.

Leo Wright grabbed the broom handle.

His gaze was icy as he looked at Grace.

He seemed to want to retaliate.

But glancing at her, a woman, he ultimately just wrenched the broom away with force.

He threw it heavily to the ground.

Grace was pushed back several steps by him.

She swayed slightly.

Evelyn Ross did not step forward.

She had seen Leo pull his strength at the last moment.

This man usually mocked and ridiculed Grace.

But he still knew his limits.

Yet the next second—

Grace's sidekick, the mole named Nathan White, stepped forward to support her.

But it was as if he didn't have a firm grip.

He suddenly shouted, "Grace, watch out!"

Before the words finished, he and Grace fell to the ground together.

He cushioned Grace beneath him.

Ignoring the scrape on his own arm from the ground, he looked at Grace anxiously.

"Grace, are you okay?"

Grace glanced at him blankly.

Then she hurriedly asked, "I'm fine. How are you?"

Nathan White clutched his arm.

He glared angrily at Leo Wright.

"Leo Wright, you've gone too far! The old master isn't even convicted yet, and you dare lay hands on Miss Grace? Do you have any respect for the old master at all?"

Leo Wright frowned.

He looked at his own hand.

He didn't become suspicious, just sneered coldly.

"I didn't even use much force, and she fell. This generation of the Lorenz family is truly useless. Still want to lead the Special Operations Division? Are we supposed to let a delicate young lady like Grace take charge in the future?"

Every Chairman of the Special Operations Division had to undergo rigorous selection.

Although the Lorenz family had always held the Chairman position, it was through strength that they commanded respect.

Old Master Benjamin Wright was once unmatched in combat within the division.

In Grace's father's generation, he was unwilling to practice martial arts.

Coupled with Old Master Benjamin's intention to groom Deputy Chief Marcus Wright, Grace's father simply became a forensic examiner.

Deputy Chief Marcus Wright, with his beer belly, looked like a seasoned operator, single-mindedly wanting to replace Old Master Benjamin...

But back in the day, Deputy Chief Marcus was also undefeated in the division.

His combat skills were even personally taught by Old Master Benjamin...

Leo Wright, at his age, was also a top fighter in the division.

His desire to enter the Cortez Sect for training back then was also to conquer everyone.

In this line of work, there was always an unspoken rule of testing skills through combat.

So Leo's mockery of Grace immediately resonated with those around him.

Daniel Clementson behind Leo immediately chimed in.

"Exactly. Miss Grace looks so frail. No wonder the old master said on the surface he'd hand the division over to Deputy Chief Marcus. With her like this, can she really take over the Special Operations Division? Pfft..."

The people on Grace's side instantly flared up.

"What did you say?"

They directly took action.

During the scuffle between the two sides, Nathan White punched Daniel Clementson in the face.

Daniel's nose bled profusely.

He shouted, "Alright, going for the kill, huh? You Lorenz people really don't take us Wrights seriously! Brothers, get them!"

Grace's father hurriedly stepped between them.

"Everyone, stop fighting. Let's talk this out."

Unfortunately, Grace's father didn't serve in the Special Operations Division.

These hot-blooded men, especially those behind Deputy Chief Marcus, didn't acknowledge him.

Deputy Chief Marcus watched coldly from the side.

A trace of resentment showed on his face.

Evelyn Ross furrowed her brows tightly.

The mole was caught now, but the key point was...

She had no evidence!

Forget about Daniel Clementson.

Take Nathan White.

He had always been obedient to Grace within the division.

He followed her on missions, protected her.

He was one of the most loyal to the Lorenz family.

Now, if she told Grace that Nathan was a mole, even with their close relationship, Grace would be suspicious!

She directly looked at Alexander Vance.

Alexander had already guessed her intention.

When he suspected the two were moles earlier, he had already sent their information to Thomas Watson for investigation.

Seeing Evelyn look over now, he frowned and shook his head.

It meant he hadn't received feedback yet.

Or, Thomas couldn't find anything suspicious about these two.

Indeed, people bought by the Fox... how could they be easily investigated?

They definitely wouldn't leave obvious evidence.

But without exposing these two moles for stirring trouble, if she stepped out now, she would definitely be suspected by both sides...

Then it wouldn't be a fight between the two factions, but her being beaten by both!

But just let it go on like this?

Evelyn looked at the few people from the International Department standing to the side.

She saw disdain on their faces.

Clearly, they were waiting for both sides to fall out, to reap the benefits.

Evelyn's brows were tightly locked.

Even without evidence to reveal the true faces of these two moles, she had to step in forcefully!

If she waited any longer, both sides would really end up severely hurting each other!

Evelyn's eyes turned sharp.
