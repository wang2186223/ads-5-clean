Grace Lorenz curled her lips into a cold smile at those words.

"My grandfather's case hasn't been cleared up yet, and the Wright family is already in such a hurry to kick me out?"

Leo Wright's face darkened.

"Stop making baseless accusations! We're all thinking about the department!"

"Is that so?"

Grace did not back down.

"From my very first day in the Special Operations Division, you've targeted me at every turn. Now you've finally found your chance, haven't you? Too bad, you and I are of equal rank. You don't have the authority to fire me."

Her gaze swept over the surrounding staff.

"If you want to drive the Lorenz family out, just say it. We're not people who cling to power. There's no need for these underhanded tactics."

Leo was left speechless.

A steady voice suddenly rang out.

"Now, now, my dear niece. That's putting it rather strongly."

Everyone turned to look.

A middle-aged man was striding over.

A gentle smile was on his face, but his eyes held a sharp glint.

He exuded an aura of quiet authority.

"Yes, Director Wright."

Leo hurriedly corrected himself.

The Special Operations Division was disguised as an ordinary company to the outside world.

Internal personnel also used corporate titles.

Leaders were referred to as Chairman or Director.

Grace let out a cold laugh.

"Director Wright? Shouldn't it be Deputy Director?"

"Grace is right. Let's stick with Deputy Director for now."
